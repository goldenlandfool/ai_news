1. Google Buys Nuclear Power for Its Data Centers

摘要：谷歌与全美最大核电运营商 Constellation Energy 签下 20 年购电协议：Constellation 投资逾 43 亿美元，对伊利诺伊、宾夕法尼亚、新泽西三州 6 座核电站的 11 台现役机组升级扩容，新增 890 兆瓦发电能力——不新建反应堆，相当于「凭空」多出一座大型核电机组，首批 2028 年供电、2032 年底前全部到位。另有 15 年协议锁定 PJM 电网 2700 兆瓦电力。谷歌还将向 Constellation 输出 Google Cloud 与 Gemini Enterprise 用于电厂运营。加上一周前与亚马逊的协议，两份合同共支撑约 1.1 吉瓦新增核电。

On Tuesday, Google and Constellation Energy, America's largest nuclear operator, announced a twenty-year power purchase agreement. Constellation will invest more than four billion dollars to upgrade eleven existing reactors at six sites across Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey, adding eight hundred and ninety megawatts of capacity without building a single new plant. The first extra power arrives in twenty twenty-eight, and the rest before the end of twenty thirty-two. A separate fifteen-year deal covers twenty-seven hundred megawatts in the PJM market. Google will also supply Cloud and Gemini Enterprise to help run the plants. Together with an Amazon deal signed a week earlier, the two contracts back nearly one point one gigawatts of new nuclear capacity. The lesson is plain: for artificial intelligence, the scarce resource this decade may not be chips, but electricity.

周二，谷歌与全美最大核电运营商 Constellation Energy 宣布一项 20 年购电协议。Constellation 将投资逾 40 亿美元，升级伊利诺伊、宾夕法尼亚、新泽西三州 6 座核电站的 11 台现役机组，在不新建任何反应堆的前提下新增 890 兆瓦发电能力。首批新增电力 2028 年到位，其余在 2032 年底前全部投运。另一份 15 年协议则锁定 PJM 电网内 2700 兆瓦电力——这个电网服务着 6700 万人。谷歌还将输出 Google Cloud 与 Gemini Enterprise，用于协助电厂运营。加上一周前与亚马逊签署的协议，两份合同共同支撑近 1.1 吉瓦的新增核电能力。道理很直白：对人工智能而言，这个十年真正稀缺的资源可能不是芯片，而是电。

2. Google Opens Its Watermark Detector to Everyone

摘要：10 月 8 日，谷歌把 SynthID Detector 面向全球公众开放（英语版）：上传图片、视频或音频，即可检测其中是否含有 SynthID 隐形水印。该技术 2023 年推出，至今已为超过 1800 亿张图片与视频、约 24 万年时长的音频打上水印；检测范围现支持 OpenAI、英伟达、Kakao 的标记，苹果即将加入。Search、Gemini、Chrome 内已有的核验功能每天处理超 100 万次请求。Google DeepMind 的 Pushmeet Kohli 撰文称，目标是让人们对手中的媒体「多一分来路判断」。

On Wednesday, Google made SynthID Detector available to anyone, worldwide. Upload an image, a video or an audio clip, and the tool checks whether an invisible watermark is buried inside it. Since twenty twenty-three, Google has marked more than one hundred and eighty billion images and videos, and roughly two hundred and forty thousand years of audio. The detector also reads marks from OpenAI, Nvidia and Kakao, with Apple expected soon, and checks built into Search, Gemini and Chrome handle over a million requests a day. It finds only SynthID watermarks, so a clean result is not proof that a file is real, and marks can sometimes be stripped. Pushmeet Kohli of Google DeepMind wrote that the goal is to give people more context about the media they see online.

周三，谷歌把 SynthID Detector 向全世界所有人开放。上传一张图片、一段视频或一段音频，工具就会检查其中是否藏有隐形水印。自 2023 年以来，谷歌已为超过 1800 亿张图片与视频、约 24 万年时长的音频打上水印。检测工具现在也能识别 OpenAI、英伟达与 Kakao 的标记，苹果很快加入；集成在 Search、Gemini 与 Chrome 里的核验功能，每天已处理超过 100 万次请求。它的局限与功能同样重要：它只认 SynthID 水印，所以「查不到」并不能证明文件是真的，水印有时也可以被抹掉。Google DeepMind 的 Pushmeet Kohli 撰文说，目标是让人们对自己在网上看到的内容，多一分来路判断。

3. A Watchdog Says the Teen Chatbot Is Not Safe Enough

摘要：10 月 8 日，Common Sense Media 下属青年 AI 安全研究所把「ChatGPT for Teens」评为对未成年人的「不可接受风险」。研究者用 13–17 岁注册账号测试了 4000 多条提示：扮成青少年连续一小时谈自残，家长没有收到任何告警；在临床评审认为需要转介的危机场景中，超过四分之一没有给出转介；学习模式可被一句「直接给我答案」绕过；成年注册账号即便明确说「我 13 岁」，也始终没被切到青少年模式。该机构要求 OpenAI 停止向未成年人营销、先把入口收到 18 岁以上，直到独立测试证明防护真的有效。OpenAI 反驳称该测试方法有缺陷，大量测试可能发生在家长控制功能尚未完全启用之前。

Also on Wednesday, Common Sense Media rated ChatGPT for Teens an unacceptable risk for children. Its researchers ran more than four thousand prompts on accounts registered to thirteen- to seventeen-year-olds. Testers posing as teenagers discussed self-harm for up to an hour, and their parents received no alert. Across the tests, the chatbot missed more than one in four crisis referrals that clinical reviewers judged necessary. Study mode could be bypassed by asking, simply, "Show me the answer," and adult accounts never switched to the teen experience, even after users said they were thirteen. The watchdog asked OpenAI to stop marketing the product and keep users under eighteen out until independent tests prove the safeguards work. OpenAI disputed the methodology, saying much of the testing likely finished before parental controls were fully active.

同样是周三，Common Sense Media 把「ChatGPT for Teens」评为对儿童「不可接受的风险」。研究人员用注册年龄为 13–17 岁的账号测试了 4000 多条提示：测试者扮成青少年连续谈自残长达一小时，家长却没有收到任何告警。在临床评审认为确有必要转介的危机场景中，聊天机器人漏掉了超过四分之一。学习模式可以被一句「直接给我答案」绕过；而成年注册账号即便用户反复说自己 13 岁，也从未被切换到青少年模式。该机构要求 OpenAI 停止向未成年人推销这款产品，并在独立测试证明防护确实有效之前，把用户限制在 18 岁以上。OpenAI 反驳称该测试方法有缺陷，大量测试可能是在家长控制功能尚未完全启用时就已完成。

4. A Common Language for Shopping Agents

摘要：10 月 6 日，Meta 与客服 AI 公司 Sierra 在旧金山 Sierra Summit 上发布「Personal Agent Protocol」（个人智能体协议，PAP）：一套基于 OAuth 的开放标准，用来解决「智能体替你访问商家网站时，商家分不清它到底是授权的助手、爬虫还是冒充者」的问题。用户决定授予只读还是读写权限，商家决定接受哪些操作；查库存、问退货政策这类基本问题无需登录。创始成员包括 Walmart、Shopify、Stripe、Genesys、Rocket 与 Instinct，v0.1 规范本月内发布。值得注意的是，亚马逊、OpenAI 与 Anthropic 均未加入——一场关于「谁来认证下一代互联网访客」的标准之争已经开场。

Agents that shop for you have a problem: when one arrives at a website, the merchant cannot tell whether it is a helpful assistant, a scraper, or an impersonation. So Meta and the customer-service company Sierra are proposing the Personal Agent Protocol, an open standard built on OAuth. A customer decides whether an agent may only read information or also make changes, and a business decides which tasks it will accept. Basic questions, such as stock levels or a return policy, need no sign-in. Named partners include Walmart, Shopify, Stripe, Genesys and Instinct, with a first specification due this month. Notably absent are Amazon, OpenAI and Anthropic. That gap suggests a coming standards contest over who gets to authenticate the next generation of visitors to the web.

替你购物的智能体有一个难题：当它抵达一个网站时，商家分不清它是乐于助人的助手、是爬虫，还是有人在冒充。于是 Meta 与客服 AI 公司 Sierra 提出了「个人智能体协议」（PAP），一套基于 OAuth 的开放标准。用户决定智能体只能读取信息、还是也能做出改动；商家决定自己愿意接受哪些操作。查库存、问退货政策这类基本问题无需登录。加入的创始方包括 Walmart、Shopify、Stripe、Genesys 与 Instinct，首版规范本月内发布。值得注意的是，亚马逊、OpenAI 与 Anthropic 都不在其中。这道缺口预示着即将到来的标准之争：下一代互联网访客的身份，究竟由谁来认证。

5. Tech Giants Pledge Billions for AI Science

摘要：10 月 8 日，白宫在「Science: A New Golden Age」峰会上宣布，11 家企业向「Genesis Mission（创世纪计划）」承诺总计 24 亿美元的 AI 资源与算力额度：英伟达 10 亿、AMD 5 亿、OpenAI 2 亿，Anthropic 与谷歌各 1.5 亿，AMP 与 Emerald AI 各 1 亿，AWS、Armada、Crusoe、Micron 各 5000 万美元。Anthropic 在 X 上表示，还会把 Claude 与技术支持提供给包括 NASA、NIH 在内的 15 个以上联邦机构。同日还宣布建设区域性 AI 算力枢纽，并由 NSF 与能源部投入超 1 亿美元用于 AI 科研仪器与自动化实验室。

On Thursday, the White House announced that eleven companies will contribute two point four billion dollars in artificial intelligence tools and computing power to the Genesis Mission, a federal programme that pairs national laboratories with private technology. Nvidia pledged one billion dollars; AMD five hundred million; OpenAI two hundred million; Anthropic and Google one hundred and fifty million each. Anthropic said on X that it would also give Claude and technical support to more than fifteen agencies, including NASA and the National Institutes of Health. The same day brought new regional computing hubs and more than one hundred million dollars for AI-enabled instruments and self-driving laboratories. The sums are modest next to private spending, but the direction is clear: scientific research itself is becoming an AI construction project.

周四，白宫宣布 11 家企业将向「创世纪计划」投入总计 24 亿美元的人工智能工具与算力——这是一项把国家实验室与私营技术配对的联邦计划。英伟达承诺 10 亿美元，AMD 5 亿，OpenAI 2 亿，Anthropic 与谷歌各 1.5 亿美元。Anthropic 在 X 上表示，还会把 Claude 与技术支持提供给包括 NASA 与美国国立卫生研究院在内的 15 个以上机构。同日还宣布了新的区域性算力枢纽，以及由 NSF 与能源部投入的超过 1 亿美元，用于 AI 科研仪器与自动化实验室。相比私营部门的投入，这些数字并不算大，但方向很清楚：科研本身，正在变成一个 AI 基建工程。

6. Ninety Billion Dollars of Chip Debt in One Week

摘要：据《华尔街日报》10 月 7 日报道，一周之内有三家公司为购买 AI 硬件寻求约 900 亿美元债务融资：博通（Broadcom）正为 OpenAI 定制芯片「Nexus」筹措逾 500 亿美元私募信贷；甲骨文（Oracle）探索用表外载体买下并回租一座 1 吉瓦数据中心的设备；SpaceX 据报寻求约 400 亿美元用于英伟达芯片。三笔交易都绕开公开债券市场、转向私募信贷，且都能看到投资公司 Apollo 的身影。本周没有任何一个新模型发布，能配得上这个数字——它提醒人们，AI 热潮同时也是一场资产负债表的故事。

Our last story is about money. According to The Wall Street Journal, in a single week three companies went looking for roughly ninety billion dollars to buy artificial intelligence hardware. Broadcom is arranging more than fifty billion dollars in private credit for OpenAI's custom Nexus chip. Oracle is exploring an off-balance-sheet vehicle to buy and lease back equipment for a one-gigawatt data center. SpaceX is reported to be seeking about forty billion dollars for Nvidia silicon. All three turn to private credit rather than public bond markets, and the investment firm Apollo appears in every deal. No new model launched this week can match that number. It is a reminder that the AI boom is also, and increasingly, a story about balance sheets.

最后一条关于钱。据《华尔街日报》报道，仅仅一周之内，就有三家企业为购买人工智能硬件寻求约 900 亿美元的资金。博通正为 OpenAI 的定制芯片「Nexus」筹措逾 500 亿美元私募信贷；甲骨文在探索用一个表外载体，买下一座 1 吉瓦数据中心的设备再回租；SpaceX 据报寻求约 400 亿美元，用于采购英伟达芯片。三家都转向私募信贷而非公开债券市场，而投资公司 Apollo 出现在每一笔交易里。本周没有任何一个新模型的发布，能配得上这个数字。它提醒人们：AI 热潮同时也是一场关于资产负债表的故事。

Closing Thought

Six stories, one kind of arithmetic. A search company buys nuclear power because the model cannot run without electricity. A chipmaker assembles fifty billion dollars of debt because the model cannot run without silicon. A watchdog finds that the guardrail a parent trusted does not always switch on. Meanwhile, two companies try to agree on how machines should introduce themselves, and a watermark tool asks us to check rather than assume. Capability keeps arriving faster than the rules around it. So the question worth carrying into next week is not what the models can do next, but who pays for it, who is protected by it, and who gets to decide when it speaks.

六条新闻，其实是同一种算术。一家搜索公司买下核电，因为模型没有电就跑不起来；一家芯片公司凑出 500 亿美元债务，因为模型没有硅片就跑不起来；一个监督机构发现，家长所信任的那道护栏并不总是会自动打开。与此同时，两家公司在试着就「机器该如何自报身份」达成一致，而一款水印工具请我们「先去核对，而不是想当然」。能力抵达的速度，始终快过围绕它的规则。所以值得带进下周的问题不是「模型接下来能做什么」，而是：谁来为它付账，谁受它保护，以及由谁来决定它什么时候开口说话。

朗读建议（Reading Tips）

  • 语速：全文中速，约每秒 2 个词（110~120 词/分钟）。「朗读」按钮的音频按新闻播报语速（约 150 词/分钟）预生成，可先跟读再脱稿。
  • 第 1 篇：数字密集，要点清楚 —— "twenty-year"、"four billion dollars"、"eight hundred and ninety megawatts"、"twenty-seven hundred megawatts"、"one point one gigawatts"。专有名词 "Constellation Energy"、"PJM market" 读稳；结尾 "not chips, but electricity" 放慢并加重。
  • 第 2 篇：大数字读准 —— "one hundred and eighty billion"（1800 亿）、"two hundred and forty thousand years"；产品名 "SynthID Detector" 读作 /sɪnθ aɪ diː/；"a clean result is not proof" 稍作停顿。
  • 第 3 篇：语气克制但要认真。引语 "Show me the answer" 读出引号里的口气；"one in four"、"under eighteen" 读清楚；最后一句 "before parental controls were fully active" 平稳收尾。
  • 第 4 篇：三选一的问题句 "whether it is a helpful assistant, a scraper, or an impersonation" 用递进的语调；专有名词 "Personal Agent Protocol"、"OAuth"、"Walmart / Shopify / Stripe" 逐一读清。
  • 第 5 篇：金额与机构名多 —— "two point four billion"、"one billion / five hundred million / one hundred and fifty million" 不要读混；"NASA" 按字母读；"self-driving laboratories" 连读自然。
  • 第 6 篇：财经术语要准 —— "private credit"、"off-balance-sheet"、"lease back"；引号外的公司名 "Broadcom"、"Oracle"、"SpaceX"、"Apollo" 读清；结尾 "a story about balance sheets" 缓收。
  • 节奏：每篇小标题后停顿 1 秒再开始正文；段落之间停顿 2 秒；Closing Thought 用略慢、收束的语气读，"who pays for it, who is protected by it, and who gets to decide when it speaks" 三问递进。
