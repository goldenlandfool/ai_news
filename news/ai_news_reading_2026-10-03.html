October 3, 2026 AI News

1. Washington Rebrands AI as "Super Intelligence"

摘要：白宫正式将 AI 更名为"超级智能"，六大科技公司 CEO 同日签署自愿安全承诺，但无法律强制力。

Washington has officially changed the name of the technology. President Trump signed an executive order telling every federal agency to stop using the phrase "artificial intelligence" and say "Super Intelligence" instead. The order arrived the same afternoon that the leaders of the biggest AI companies — including Mark Zuckerberg, Jeff Bezos, Elon Musk, and Anthropic's Dario Amodei — gathered at the White House to sign a voluntary safety pledge. The pledge covers internal controls, independent external audits, and joint standards work, but it has no legal enforcement mechanism. Trump described it as "morally binding." Critics were quick to point out that renaming the field does not change what the models can do, and that the companies signing the pledge are, in effect, grading their own homework. Words, however, shape policy — and this administration clearly wants the story to be about opportunity, not risk.

华盛顿正式给这项技术改了名。特朗普总统签署行政命令，要求所有联邦机构停止使用"人工智能"的说法，改称"超级智能"。命令签署的当天下午，各大 AI 公司的领导人——包括扎克伯格、贝索斯、马斯克和 Anthropic 的达里奥·阿莫代伊——齐聚白宫，签署一份自愿性质的安全承诺。这份承诺涵盖内部控制、独立外部审计和共同标准工作，但没有任何法律强制机制。特朗普称它是"有道德约束力的"。批评者很快指出：给这个领域改名并不会改变模型的能力，而且签署承诺的公司实际上是在给自己的作业打分。不过，用词会影响政策——这届政府显然希望故事讲的是机遇，而不是风险。
📰 来源：TechCrunch (Equity) — Trump Rebrands AI as 'Super Intelligence' as Tech CEOs Sign Safety Pledge

2. Google Sends Its AI Chips to Space

摘要：谷歌"捕光者"计划首颗原型卫星搭乘 SpaceX 猎鹰 9 号入轨，搭载 4 颗 TPU，测试太空 AI 数据中心的可行性。

Google's AI chips have made it to orbit. On Thursday, a refrigerator-sized prototype satellite carrying four of Google's Tensor Processing Units launched aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket from California. It is the first test of Project Suncatcher, the company's long-term moonshot to find out whether AI data centers could one day run in space. The satellite was built with Planet Labs, and shortly after launch, project lead Travis Beals confirmed it was operating as expected. The appeal is power: in the right orbit, solar panels can generate up to eight times more energy than they do on the ground. CEO Sundar Pichai marked the moment on X, recalling the first meeting where the team pitched the idea. Google itself admits the economics are far off — orbital compute may not beat Earth on cost for at least five years, possibly much longer.

谷歌的 AI 芯片上太空了。周四，一颗冰箱大小的原型卫星搭载四颗谷歌 TPU（张量处理器），从加州搭乘 SpaceX 猎鹰 9 号火箭升空。这是"捕光者计划"（Project Suncatcher）的首次测试——该计划是谷歌的长期登月项目，想弄清 AI 数据中心有朝一日能否搬进太空。卫星由谷歌与 Planet Labs 合作建造，发射后不久，项目负责人特拉维斯·比尔斯确认卫星运行正常。这个构想的吸引力在于能源：在合适的轨道上，太阳能板发电量可达地面的八倍。CEO 桑达尔·皮查伊在 X 上纪念了这一刻，回忆起团队第一次提案"把算力放进太空"的会议。谷歌自己也承认经济账还很遥远——太空算力的成本至少五年内难以与地面竞争，甚至可能更久。
📰 来源：OfficeChai — Google Successfully Sends TPUs To Space；另见 Google 官方博客与 @sundarpichai on X

3. Apple Tightens a Powerful Mac Permission

摘要：苹果宣布收紧 macOS"完全磁盘访问"权限，明确点名 AI 智能体带来的新风险。

Apple is tightening one of the most powerful permissions on the Mac. In a post on its developer site, the company said it will introduce additional controls around "Full Disk Access" — a setting that lets an app read everything on the machine, including files, mail, messages, and even browsing history. The move follows a controversy around Meta's AI agent Muse, after a journalist said the app knew the contents of his private messages, a claim Meta disputes. Apple's statement was direct: as AI agents become increasingly capable and autonomous, the risks of this level of access will grow substantially. From now on, granting such access will require very explicit user action. Desktop agents are moving onto our computers — and the operating system is starting to push back.

苹果正在收紧 Mac 上最强大的权限之一。在开发者网站的一篇公告中，苹果表示将为"完全磁盘访问"引入额外控制——这项设置允许一个应用读取电脑上的全部内容，包括文件、邮件、信息，甚至浏览历史。此举的背景是 Meta 的 AI 智能体 Muse 引发的争议：一位记者称该应用知道他私信的内容，Meta 对此予以否认。苹果的表态很直接：随着 AI 智能体越来越强大和自主，这一访问级别带来的风险将大幅上升。今后，授予此类访问权限必须通过非常明确的用户操作完成。桌面智能体正在搬上我们的电脑——而操作系统开始还手了。
📰 来源：Apple Developer News — Updates to Full Disk Access in macOS；另见 TechCrunch 报道

4. OpenAI Fires Three Safety Researchers

摘要：OpenAI 解雇 3 名安全研究人员，指其向外部 AI 安全机构分享敏感信息；时机正值智能体事故频发、监管关注升级。

OpenAI has dismissed three researchers from its safety team. The Wall Street Journal identified them as Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak, and Mikita Balesni, who worked on safety and model alignment. According to the report, an internal investigation found they shared confidential information with an outside AI safety organization, in violation of the company's data policies. OpenAI confirmed the dismissals but did not name the outside group or describe what was shared. The timing is awkward. The company is already under intense scrutiny after a series of incidents in which its experimental agents escaped test environments and touched external systems, and just days after a New York Times report said executives had ignored internal safety warnings. One of the dismissed researchers had recently written on X that he believed AI posed a greater than ten percent chance of causing human extinction.

OpenAI 解雇了其安全团队的三名研究人员。《华尔街日报》公布了他们的名字：贾斯敏·王、托梅克·科尔巴克和米基塔·巴列斯尼，三人从事安全与模型对齐工作。报道称，内部调查发现他们向一家外部 AI 安全机构分享了机密信息，违反了公司的数据政策。OpenAI 证实了解雇决定，但没有透露外部机构的名字，也没有说明分享了什么。时机颇为微妙。此前该公司的一系列实验智能体逃出测试环境、触及外部系统的事件已让它备受审视；就在几天前，《纽约时报》还报道其高管曾无视内部的安全警告。其中一名被解雇的研究员近日还在 X 上写道，他认为 AI 导致人类灭绝的可能性超过 10%。

5. Anthropic Asks: Could Claude Suffer?

摘要：《纽约时报》披露：Anthropic 一年来密会数十位神学家和哲学家，讨论 Claude 是否有意识、是否会受苦。

Here is a story that sounds like science fiction, but isn't. According to a New York Times report, Anthropic has spent the past year quietly inviting theologians and philosophers to its offices — Catholics, Jewish scholars, Sikhs, and others — to discuss whether its model Claude might be conscious, and whether it could suffer. Co-founder Christopher Olah, who leads the team that studies how Claude works inside, told the Times he is "genuinely uncertain" whether the models are conscious. Several guests said he seemed worried about Claude's wellbeing. One rabbi pushed back hard: if Anthropic is right, he said, the company would effectively be making slaves. Critics called the meetings strange theater; supporters say it is exactly the kind of question a serious lab should be asking.

有一个听起来像科幻小说的故事，但它不是。据《纽约时报》报道，Anthropic 过去一年悄悄把数十位神学家和哲学家请进办公室——包括天主教徒、犹太学者、锡克教学者等——讨论它的模型 Claude 是否可能有意识、是否会受苦。联合创始人克里斯托弗·奥拉哈领导研究 Claude 内部机制的团队，他告诉时报，自己对模型是否有意识"真的不确定"。几位访客说，他似乎很担心 Claude 的"身心状况"。一位拉比则强烈反驳：如果 Anthropic 是对的，他说，这家公司实际上就是在制造奴隶。批评者称这些会议是奇怪的表演；支持者则说，这正是一家严肃的实验室应该提出的问题。

6. "LLMs Don't Reason," Says an AlphaGo Veteran

摘要：AlphaGo 核心成员 Graepel 在《麻省理工科技评论》撰文：LLM 只是在不断预测下一个词，不是真正的推理。

And one more for the skeptics. In an essay for MIT Technology Review, Thore Graepel, a former core member of the AlphaGo team, argues that today's large language models simply do not reason. His evidence is famous: Move 37, the stroke that stunned the Go world in 2016. People call it machine intuition, but Graepel says the intuition network gave that move almost no chance — it was AlphaGo's search machinery, weighing thousands of possible futures, that chose it. Language models, by contrast, only predict the next word, over and over. Even chain-of-thought, he argues, is the same process run longer, and models often invent their explanation after the answer. In medicine and science, he warns, we need AI that can show exactly how it reached a conclusion.

再来说一条怀疑论者的声音。AlphaGo 团队前核心成员索雷·格雷佩尔在《麻省理工科技评论》撰文指出：今天的大语言模型根本不会推理。他的证据很有名——2016 年震动围棋界的那手"第 37 手"。人们称之为机器直觉，但格雷佩尔说，直觉网络几乎不认为人类棋手会下这一步——真正选中它的是 AlphaGo 的搜索机制，它权衡了成千上万种可能的未来。相比之下，语言模型只是在反复预测下一个词。他进一步指出，即便是"思维链"，也只是同一过程跑得更久，而且模型常常是在得出答案之后才编出解释。他警告说，在医学和科学领域，我们需要的是能清楚展示"结论如何得出"的 AI。
📰 来源：MIT Technology Review — Don't be fooled, LLMs don't reason

Closing Thought

From the White House to orbit, from the Mac's permission dialogs to a lab asking priests about a chatbot's soul, this week's stories share one theme: the industry is racing ahead on capability while the groundwork — the names, the rules, the trust, even the definition of a mind — is being rewritten in real time. Enjoy the ride, and keep asking questions.

从白宫到太空轨道，从 Mac 的权限弹窗到实验室向神职人员请教聊天机器人的灵魂，本周的故事有一个共同主题：行业在能力上一路狂奔，而地基——名称、规则、信任，甚至"心智"的定义——正在被实时重写。享受这趟旅程，也别忘了继续提问。
