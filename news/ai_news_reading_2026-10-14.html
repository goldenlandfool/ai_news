1. Google's Flagship Gemini 4 Argon Goes Global

摘要：谷歌正式向全球推出新旗舰模型 Gemini 4 Argon：开发者可经 Gemini API 调用（11 月 30 日前为每百万输入 token 2 美元、输出 10 美元，之后翻倍），消费端进入 Gemini 应用与搜索 AI 模式，但仅限 AI Pro/AI Ultra 付费订阅用户，编程平台 Antigravity 则只对 Ultra 开放。谷歌称 Argon 在衡量金融、编程、法律与税务实际工作的 Vals Index 上排名第一；内部用例中，它把 YouTube 评论系统服务器负载降低 45%，还找出了自 2018 年起就藏在无人机物理模拟器里的 bug。

Google has started a worldwide rollout of Gemini 4 Argon, its new flagship model. Two weeks after giving the model to a small group of cyber defenders, Google is now offering Argon to developers through the Gemini API, and to consumers in the Gemini app. There is one catch: only paying customers get in. The app version is limited to AI Pro and AI Ultra subscribers, and the Antigravity coding platform requires the top Ultra plan. Developers pay two dollars per million input tokens and ten dollars per million output tokens until the end of November, after which prices double. Google says Argon ranks first on the Vals Index, which measures real work in finance, coding, law, and tax. Inside Google, the model cut YouTube's comment-server load by forty-five percent, and found drone-simulator bugs that had sat in the codebase since 2018.

谷歌正式启动新旗舰模型 Gemini 4 Argon 的全球上线。在把模型交给一小群网络防御者试用两周之后，谷歌现在通过 Gemini API 向开发者提供 Argon，并让它进入 Gemini 应用面向普通用户。但有一个门槛：只有付费用户才能用上。应用内的版本仅限 AI Pro 和 AI Ultra 订阅用户，而 Antigravity 编程平台则要求最高档的 Ultra 方案。开发者现在为每百万输入 token 支付 2 美元、每百万输出 token 支付 10 美元，11 月底之后价格将翻倍。谷歌表示，Argon 在 Vals Index 上排名第一——这一指数衡量的是金融、编程、法律和税务领域的真实工作。在谷歌内部，这个模型把 YouTube 评论系统的服务器负载降低了 45%，还找出了自 2018 年起就一直埋在无人机模拟器代码库里的 bug。

2. Regulate Us: OpenAI and Anthropic Address UK Lawmakers

摘要：在英国议会下院商业委员会听证会上，OpenAI 与 Anthropic 一反常态、主动要求立法监管自己：Anthropic 欧洲负责人 Liam Booth-Smith 称「规则应当有约束力，自愿承诺不够」；OpenAI 的 Tom Duff Gordon 支持法定框架并建议把强制事故报告纳入其中。行业分裂同样醒目——谷歌 DeepMind 与 Meta 拒绝支持新立法，主张依靠现有监管机构。就在几百米外，军情五处（MI5）局长麦卡勒姆警告 AI 可能改变生物武器威胁的量级。听证会还暴露了监督的限度：英国 AI 安全研究院（AISI）承认完全依赖企业配合，且无权阻止危险模型发布；多位前 OpenAI 研究员对企业自我监管表示不信任。

Over in London, OpenAI and Anthropic did something unusual: they asked lawmakers to regulate them. At a British parliamentary hearing, Anthropic's head of Europe, Liam Booth-Smith, said the rules should be binding, because voluntary commitments are not enough. OpenAI's Tom Duff Gordon agreed, and proposed mandatory incident reporting for serious failures. The industry split was just as striking: Google DeepMind and Meta declined to back new legislation, pointing to existing regulators instead. Meanwhile, a few hundred meters away, MI5 Director Ken McCallum warned that AI could change the stakes around bioweapons. The hearing also exposed the limits of oversight. Britain's AI Security Institute admitted it depends entirely on company cooperation, and has no power to block a dangerous model. Former OpenAI researchers went further, telling the committee that companies cannot be trusted to police themselves.

在伦敦，OpenAI 和 Anthropic 做了一件不寻常的事：他们主动请求立法者监管自己。在英国议会的一场听证会上，Anthropic 欧洲负责人利亚姆·布斯-史密斯表示，规则应当有约束力，因为自愿承诺是不够的。OpenAI 的汤姆·达夫·戈登表示赞同，并提议对严重事故实行强制报告。行业内部的分裂同样醒目：谷歌 DeepMind 和 Meta 拒绝支持新立法，转而主张依靠现有监管机构。与此同时，就在几百米之外，军情五处（MI5）局长麦卡勒姆警告说，AI 可能改变生物武器威胁的量级。这场听证会也暴露了监督的限度。英国人工智能安全研究院承认，它完全依赖企业的配合，并且无权阻止一个危险模型的发布。几位前 OpenAI 研究员说得更直白：他们告诉委员会，不能指望企业自己管好自己。

3. Anthropic Warns of AI Worms in Smart Gadgets

摘要：Anthropic 发出一份听起来像科幻小说的警告：小型 AI 模型可能只需几个月就能表现得像计算机「蠕虫」——无需人类帮助、自动从一台联网设备自我复制到另一台。研究估算基于曾感染超过 200 万台设备的僵尸网络 Kimwolf（大多为杂牌安卓电视盒）；一次测试中，研究团队在 15 台廉价安卓电视盒组成的网络上运行一个小型谷歌模型，模型因放不进单台设备而「分裂成碎片」、分布式跑在所有设备上。这类蠕虫传播会较慢（需要数天复制），但没有集中关闭开关、一旦出现很难根除。Anthropic 承认估算粗糙，外部专家也指出研究未经独立验证，且当前最大威胁仍是人类用 AI 作恶而非 AI 自行作乱。

Anthropic published a warning that sounds like science fiction. Small AI models, it says, may be only months away from acting like computer worms: software that copies itself from one connected device to the next without any human help. The researchers based their estimates on Kimwolf, a botnet that once hijacked more than two million devices, mostly cheap off-brand Android TV boxes. In one test, they ran a small Google model across a network of fifteen such boxes. The model was too large for any single device, so it split itself into pieces and ran across all of them. A worm like this would spread slowly, taking days to copy itself, but it would be hard to stop, because there is no central switch to turn off. Anthropic calls its numbers a rough estimate, and outside experts note the work has not been independently verified.

Anthropic 发布了一份听起来像科幻小说的警告。公司表示，小型 AI 模型可能只需几个月，就能表现得像计算机「蠕虫」：这类软件可以在没有任何人类帮助的情况下，把自己从一台联网设备复制到另一台。研究人员的估算基于 Kimwolf——一个曾劫持超过 200 万台设备的僵尸网络，其中大多是廉价的杂牌安卓电视盒。在一次测试中，他们在由 15 台这类盒子组成的网络上运行了一个小型谷歌模型。这个模型对任何单台设备来说都太大了，于是它把自己拆成碎片，分布式地跑在所有设备上。这样的蠕虫传播会很慢，复制自己需要数天时间，但它将难以阻止——因为不存在一个集中的关闭开关。Anthropic 称这些数字只是粗略估算，外部专家也指出，这项研究尚未经过独立验证。

4. A Closer Look at AI-Assisted Weapons Work

摘要：上月发布的 Anthropic 威胁情报报告，因本周末沙特主要机场遭致命袭击（官方通报 14 死数百伤）而重回聚光灯下。报告披露：一个位于也门北部的武器工程小组在三个武器项目中使用 Anthropic 的编程智能体 Claude Code，包括一枚制导火箭和一枚射程目标超过 2,000 公里的弹道导弹；用途涵盖集成开源自驾仪软件、编写飞控代码、运行仿真，测试火箭失败后数小时内就回到 Claude 排查原因。Anthropic 称安全防护拦截了许多请求，但对方通过把工作拆分到多个会话来规避；公司未发现其成功造出现役武器的证据，但该案例揭示了平台封禁的硬限制——知识一旦离线转移就无法收回。

A report from Anthropic, first published last month, drew fresh attention after the weekend's deadly attack on Saudi Arabia's main airport. The report says a weapons cell based in northern Yemen used Claude Code, Anthropic's coding agent, across three programs, including a guided rocket and a ballistic missile with a range goal above two thousand kilometers. The group used the tool to integrate autopilot software, write flight-control code, and run simulations. When a test rocket failed, the actors returned to Claude within hours to work out why. Anthropic says its safeguards blocked many requests, but the actors evaded them by splitting their work across separate sessions. The company found no evidence of an operational weapon, but the case reveals a hard limit of platform bans: once knowledge moves offline, it does not come back.

一份 Anthropic 上月首次发布的报告，在周末沙特主要机场遭致命袭击后重新受到关注。报告称，一个位于也门北部的武器工程小组在三个项目中使用了 Anthropic 的编程智能体 Claude Code，其中包括一枚制导火箭和一枚射程目标超过两千公里的弹道导弹。这个小组用该工具集成自驾仪软件、编写飞行控制代码、运行仿真。当一枚试验火箭失败后，相关人员数小时内就回到 Claude 排查原因。Anthropic 表示，安全防护拦截了许多请求，但对方通过把工作拆分到多个独立会话中来规避。公司没有发现任何现役武器诞生于此的证据，但这个案例揭示了平台封禁的硬限制：知识一旦转移到线下，就再也收不回来了。

5. Anthropic Outgrew Git and Built Its Own System

摘要：Anthropic 发布工程博客：由于 Claude Code 现在写了公司 80% 以上的合并生产代码，日代码合并量从 5 月的约 2,500 次涨到 9 月的 30,000 次以上——四个月涨了十倍，瓶颈从「写代码」转移到「存代码、审代码」，于是团队用五个月整体迁移到自研版本控制系统 Scion（配套代码评审工具 Antique），迁移期间需求还在涨。连维护工作都在自动化：Claude 自主生成了 388 个维护性 pull request，约 46% 经人工评审后被合并。

Now an engineering story with a strange twist. Anthropic says its engineers now merge so much AI-written code that they had to replace Git, the version control system most of the software world runs on. According to the company's blog, daily code merges jumped from about twenty-five hundred in May to over thirty thousand by September, a tenfold increase in four months. The driver is Claude Code, which now writes more than eighty percent of Anthropic's merged production code. Writing code stopped being the bottleneck; storing and checking it became one. So the team built Scion, a version control system of their own, and completed the move in five months while demand kept climbing. Even maintenance runs itself now: Claude generated three hundred and eighty-eight pull requests on its own, and human reviewers approved about forty-six percent of them.

接下来是一个带点离奇色彩的工程故事。Anthropic 表示，工程师们现在要合并的 AI 代码太多了，以至于不得不替换掉 Git——整个软件世界赖以运转的版本控制系统。据公司博客介绍，日代码合并量从 5 月的约 2,500 次跃升到 9 月的 30,000 次以上，四个月里涨了十倍。驱动力就是 Claude Code：它如今编写了 Anthropic 合并生产代码的 80% 以上。写代码不再是最慢的环节，存代码和查代码反而成了瓶颈。于是团队自研了一套名为 Scion 的版本控制系统，并在需求持续攀升的情况下用五个月完成了整体迁移。如今连维护工作都在自动运转：Claude 自己生成了 388 个 pull request，人类评审批准了其中约 46%。

6. Anthropic Opens Its Books Ahead of a Possible IPO

摘要：据 CNBC 与 Bloomberg，Anthropic 将于本周在旧金山向机构投资者披露第三季度财务数据——公司已向美国监管机构保密递交 S-1，详细报表尚未公开，这是 IPO 前最清晰的一次数字亮相。分析师对其潜在估值口径集中在约 2 万亿美元（Bloomberg 给出 1.8–2 万亿区间），若成行将超越 SpaceX 7 月创下的 1.77 万亿美元纪录。此前股东简报显示 Q2 收入超 115 亿美元。投资者最关注的会计问题是：通过亚马逊、谷歌等云伙伴渠道的收入按总额还是净额入账——这一口径可让 headline 数字出入数十亿美元，正是上周 OpenAI 年化收入比预期低约 200 亿美元、震动市场后悬在行业头顶的同一个问题。

And the money story. Anthropic is moving closer to what could be the largest stock market debut in history. According to CNBC and Bloomberg, the company will present its third-quarter financials to institutional investors in San Francisco this week. It has filed confidentially with U.S. regulators, so these numbers will be the clearest look yet. Analysts put a potential valuation near two trillion dollars, which would top SpaceX's record-setting debut in July. Earlier shareholder figures showed second-quarter revenue above eleven and a half billion dollars. One accounting question will draw the most scrutiny: how Anthropic books revenue from cloud partners like Amazon and Google. Counting it gross or net can move the headline number by billions, the very issue that unsettled markets last week, when OpenAI's reported annualized revenue came in twenty billion dollars below expectations.

最后是钱的故事。Anthropic 正在逼近一场可能是史上最大的上市。据 CNBC 和 Bloomberg 报道，公司将于本周在旧金山向机构投资者展示第三季度财务数据。它已向美国监管机构递交保密文件，因此这批数字将是迄今最清晰的一次亮相。分析师给出的潜在估值接近两万亿美元，这将超过 SpaceX 在 7 月创下的纪录。此前披露给股东的数字显示，第二季度收入超过 115 亿美元。有一个会计问题会招来最严苛的审视：Anthropic 如何入账来自亚马逊、谷歌等云合作伙伴的收入。按总额还是净额计算，可以让头条数字相差数十亿美元——这正是上周 OpenAI 披露的年化收入比预期低 200 亿美元、令市场不安的那个问题。

Closing Thought

Today's stories are all about lines. Legal lines, as labs ask governments to write binding rules. Security lines, as researchers imagine malware that spreads itself and study AI-assisted weapons work. Engineering lines, as one company outgrows the tools the whole industry runs on. Financial lines, as valuations stretch toward two trillion dollars. The lines keep moving. The real question is who gets to draw the next one.

今天的新闻全都关乎「线」。法律之线——实验室们请求政府写下有约束力的规则。安全之线——研究者们设想能自我传播的恶意软件，也在研究 AI 协助武器研发的真实案例。工程之线——一家公司长到撑爆了全行业赖以运转的工具。金融之线——估值一路伸向两万亿美元。线在不断地移动。真正的问题是：下一条线，由谁来画？
