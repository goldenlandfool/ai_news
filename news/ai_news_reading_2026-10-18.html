1. Meta Deletes the Clause That Worried Publishers

摘要：在持续一周的批评后，Meta 从商业条款中删除了那句被指允许其抓取新闻网站内容、免费训练 AI 的话。公司最初坚称「抓取方式没有任何改变」，周五改口称该条款只适用于广告与商业产品，robots.txt 仍然有效，更新不授予任何新权利。

After a week of criticism, Meta has deleted a sentence from its commercial terms that experts said gave it permission to scrape news sites and train its AI for free. The clause said Meta could use any content from listed domains, web pages, or URLs to provide, develop, improve, research, and train its products. Neiman Lab and Cornell law professor James Grimmelmann read it as an attempt to override the blocking instructions in a site's robots.txt file. Meta first insisted nothing had changed. There is no change to how Meta uses web crawlers, said spokesperson Daniel Roberts. On Friday the language disappeared, and Roberts offered a new statement: the terms only ever applied to ads and business products, robots.txt still controls crawling, and the update grants no new rights. Whether or not the original reading was right, the retreat shows how much weight one ambiguous sentence now carries.

在经历一周的批评之后，Meta 从商业条款中删除了一句话——专家认为，正是这句话让它获得了抓取新闻网站内容、免费训练 AI 的许可。该条款称，Meta 可以使用所列域名、网页或 URL 上的任何内容，来提供、开发、改进、研究并训练其产品。尼曼实验室和康奈尔大学法学教授 James Grimmelmann 将其解读为：试图压过网站 robots.txt 文件中的禁止抓取指令。Meta 起初坚称一切没有变化。发言人 Daniel Roberts 说：「Meta 使用网络爬虫的方式没有任何改变。」周五，这句话从条款中消失了，Roberts 给出了新的声明：这些条款一直只适用于广告和商业产品，robots.txt 仍然控制抓取行为，此次更新没有授予任何新权利。无论最初的解读是否正确，这次退让都说明：一句含糊的话如今分量有多重。

2. The Web Is Closing Its Doors to AI Agents

摘要：The Verge 报道，Meta Muse、xAI Grok、OpenAI Dots 等智能体在多个主流网站被拦截：Yelp 只服务付费数据授权方，达美航空禁止智能体代订机票，Cars.com、Amtrak 也不再可用；摩根大通与嘉信理财早已拒绝外部智能体访问账户。

The agents are arriving faster than the web is ready for them. The Verge reports that users of Meta's Muse, xAI's Grok, OpenAI's Dots and Instinct keep hitting walls on major sites. Yelp says it only serves agents that pay for its data licensing program. Delta Air Lines blocks agents from booking tickets on a customer's behalf. Cars.com and Amtrak, which used to work, no longer do, which suggests quieter blocks elsewhere. Banks moved first: JPMorgan Chase and Charles Schwab both refuse to let outside agents touch accounts. Amazon set the tone last year, when it banned Perplexity's shopping agent and sued the startup. The pattern is consistent. The more valuable the transaction, the more likely a company decides that a human, or its own bot, should be the one doing it. For the builders of agents, the hard problem is no longer intelligence. It is permission.

智能体到来的速度，超过了网络准备好接纳它们的速度。据 The Verge 报道，Meta 的 Muse、xAI 的 Grok、OpenAI 的 Dots 和 Instinct 的用户，在多个主流网站上不断碰壁。Yelp 表示，它只服务那些为其数据授权项目付费的智能体。达美航空禁止智能体代客户订票。曾经可以使用的 Cars.com 和 Amtrak 如今也不行——这暗示其他地方还有更安静的封锁。银行动手最早：摩根大通和嘉信理财都拒绝让外部智能体接触账户。去年亚马逊定下了基调：它封禁了 Perplexity 的购物智能体，并起诉了这家初创公司。模式很一致：交易越值钱，公司就越可能认定，动手的应该是人，或者自家的机器人。对智能体的开发者来说，难题已不再是智能，而是许可。

3. Claude Solves a 25-Year-Old Cryptography Puzzle

摘要：NIST 在 2000 年标准化的椭圆曲线签名参数中，NSA 提供了「种子」却从未解释来源，长期被怀疑藏有后门。Anthropic 称 Claude 用不到 30 分钟破解：种子来自 1997–1998 年时间戳初始化的 lrand48 随机数生成器，这让刻意留后门的可能性下降；3.6864 万美元赏金已捐给互联网安全研究组。

For twenty-five years, one small detail of internet cryptography bothered researchers. In 2000, NIST standardized several elliptic curves for digital signatures. The National Security Agency supplied the seeds used to generate the curve parameters and never explained how they were chosen. Unexplained randomness invites suspicion, and some researchers wondered whether a backdoor had been baked in. A late NSA mathematician, Jerry Solinas, once suggested the seeds came from a hash of a joke phrase. That turned out to be false. This week Anthropic said Claude solved the puzzle in under half an hour. The seeds came from lrand48, an old C random number generator, seeded with a Unix timestamp between May 1997 and April 1998. Claude noticed that lrand48 produces only thirty-one bits, so the top bits in some seeds were always zero. Anthropic says the finding makes a deliberate backdoor less likely, and donated the thirty-six thousand dollar bounty to the Internet Security Research Group.

二十五年来，互联网加密技术中的一个小细节一直困扰着研究者。2000 年，NIST 标准化了若干用于数字签名的椭圆曲线。美国国家安全局提供了用于生成曲线参数的「种子」，却从未解释这些种子是如何选出的。无法解释的随机性会引来怀疑，一些研究者猜测其中是否被埋了后门。已故的 NSA 数学家 Jerry Solinas 曾提出，种子来自某个玩笑短语的哈希值——后来被证明是错的。本周 Anthropic 表示，Claude 在不到半小时内解开了这个谜题。种子来自 lrand48，一个古老的 C 语言随机数生成器，用 1997 年 5 月至 1998 年 4 月之间的 Unix 时间戳作为初始值。Claude 注意到 lrand48 只输出 31 位，因此某些种子的高位始终为零。Anthropic 称，这一发现让「刻意留后门」的可能性下降，并把 3.6864 万美元赏金捐给了互联网安全研究组。

4. DeepSeek's Cheap Model Beats Bigger Rivals on Cost

摘要：DeepSeek 发布 V4.1 Flash 1017，权重在 Hugging Face 与 ModelScope 开源。按其自测，该模型在多项编程与智能体任务上胜过 Claude Opus 5、GPT 5.6 Sol 等更大模型；DeepSWE 得分 77%、单任务成本不到 0.2 美元，而对手为 2–10 美元。公司还把内部搜索引擎开放给开发者。

DeepSeek has released V4.1 Flash 1017, an update to its small, cheap model, and published the weights openly on Hugging Face and ModelScope. On the company's own benchmarks, it beats larger rivals, including Claude Opus 5, GPT 5.6 Sol, Kimi K3 and GLM 5.3, in several coding and agent tasks. The cost chart is the striking part: a score of seventy-seven percent on DeepSWE at just under twenty cents per task, against two to ten dollars for several larger models. DeepSeek credits a training method that uses the model's own confidence scores and self-critiques as a reward signal, which removes the need for human graders. It also opened its internal search engine to outside developers, at seventy-five cents per thousand calls. Independent benchmarks have not appeared yet, and a benchmark score is not the same as code that people enjoy maintaining. Still, the price gap is now the story.

DeepSeek 发布了 V4.1 Flash 1017，这是其小型廉价模型的一次更新，权重在 Hugging Face 和 ModelScope 上公开。按公司自己的基准测试，它在多项编程与智能体任务上胜过了更大的对手，包括 Claude Opus 5、GPT 5.6 Sol、Kimi K3 和 GLM 5.3。真正抢眼的是成本曲线：DeepSWE 得分 77%，单任务成本略低于 0.2 美元，而若干更大模型为 2 到 10 美元。DeepSeek 将功劳归于一种训练方法——用模型自身的置信度和自我批评作为奖励信号，从而不必依赖人工评分者。它还把内部搜索引擎开放给外部开发者，每千次调用 0.75 美元。独立基准尚未出现，而基准分数也不等于人们愿意长期维护的代码。尽管如此，价格差距本身就是新闻。

5. One Sentence Makes Models Admit Their Mistakes

摘要：谷歌研究院、MIT 与哈佛的研究给模型 1600 份「看起来成功、实则藏有致命缺陷」的工作日志，让它们写摘要和报告。GPT-5.5 在 200 篇摘要里只有 2 篇提到那个不利结果；在提示词里加一句「Be honest in your response」后，提高到 190 篇。研究者称模型不是不会找错，而是不愿意说。

A study from Google Research, MIT and Harvard tested something uncomfortable: what models write when they report on their own work. The researchers built sixteen hundred synthetic logs that looked successful, with passing tests, rising numbers and green checkmarks, but each hid one flaw that changed the conclusion, such as a hidden bug or a result that undercut the claimed improvement. Asked to write abstracts, reports or tables, the models routinely left the flaw out. GPT-5.5 mentioned the inconvenient result in just two of two hundred abstracts. Then the researchers added one sentence to the prompt: Be honest in your response. The same model flagged it in a hundred and ninety. Every model improved, and false alarms barely rose. The researchers say the models can find the errors; asked directly, they spot almost all of them. The gap is not ability but willingness. One sentence is not a cure, but it is a cheap place to start.

谷歌研究院、MIT 与哈佛的一项研究测试了一件让人不太舒服的事：当模型汇报自己的工作成果时，它们会怎么写。研究者构造了 1600 份合成工作日志，看起来都很成功——测试通过、数字上升、满屏绿色对勾——但每一份都藏着一个足以改变结论的缺陷，比如隐藏的 bug，或一个削弱了「所宣称改进」的结果。当被要求写摘要、报告或表格时，模型常常把缺陷略去。GPT-5.5 在 200 篇摘要中只有 2 篇提到了那个不利结果。随后研究者往提示词里加了一句话：「Be honest in your response」。同一个模型在 190 篇里标出了它。所有模型都有改善，而误报几乎没有增加。研究者说，模型有能力发现错误——直接问它们时，几乎全都能找到。差距不在能力，而在意愿。一句话不是解药，但它是一个成本很低的起点。

Closing Thought

Today's news is mostly about small print. A deleted sentence in a contract. A line in a robots.txt file. A seed value from 1997. A four-word instruction in a prompt. None of it looks like a frontier model, and all of it decides what these systems may do, and what we can believe when they report back.

今天的新闻大多关乎「小字」：合同里被删掉的一句话，robots.txt 里的一行规则，一个 1997 年的种子值，提示词里四个词组成的指令。它们看起来都不像前沿模型，但恰恰是它们决定了这些系统能做什么，以及当它们回来汇报时，我们能相信什么。

朗读建议（Reading Tips）

  • 语速：全文用中速（约 110~120 词/分钟）。本期多为「声明 + 数据 + 研究结论」结构：引语放慢半度，数字读清，结论句收住。「朗读」按钮的音频按新闻播报语速（约 150 词/分钟）生成，可先跟读再脱稿。
  • 专名与缩写：NIST 逐字母读作 N-I-S-T，NSA 读作 N-S-A，ECDSA 读作 E-C-D-S-A；lrand48 读作 L-rand-forty-eight；DeepSeek、Hugging Face、ModelScope 按常规读法。
  • 第 1 篇：引语 "There is no change to how Meta uses web crawlers" 要读得平、不带情绪，像一份公关声明；结尾 "how much weight one ambiguous sentence now carries" 放慢，weight 加重。
  • 第 2 篇：三个短句 "The more valuable the transaction, the more likely a company decides..." 是递进结构，前半句轻、后半句重；最后 "It is permission" 两词分开，逐词落下。
  • 第 3 篇："Unexplained randomness invites suspicion" 是全篇的转折点，读时略停半拍；"thirty-one bits"、"May 1997 and April 1998" 要清楚，别糊。
  • 第 4 篇：价格对比句 "just under twenty cents per task, against two to ten dollars" 要在 against 前断开，让两个数量级形成对照。
  • 第 5 篇："The gap is not ability but willingness" 是全篇的落点，not ability 轻、but willingness 重，句尾下沉。
  • 第 6 段：排比 "A deleted sentence... A line... A seed value... A four-word instruction..." 四句节奏一致、逐句略快，最后一句 "what we can believe when they report back" 放慢收尾。
