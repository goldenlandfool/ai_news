1. A Claimed Proof, Waiting for Checks

摘要：Anthropic 数学家 Levent Alpöge 11 月 20 日称借 Claude 证明黎曼猜想，并以 Lean 形式化、声称方法可推广到广义版本；证明仅发布在个人网站，Anthropic 未置评，外部专家尚未验证。按千年大奖规则，解法须公开并经受两年审查才可能被承认。

Levent Alpoge, a mathematician employed by Anthropic, said on Friday that he had proved the Riemann hypothesis with help from Claude. The question, posed in eighteen fifty-nine, concerns how prime numbers are distributed, and a verified solution carries a one million dollar prize. Alpoge says he wrote the argument in Lean, a language that lets a computer check each logical step, and that the method extends to a more general version. He posted it on his own website; Anthropic has not commented, and outside experts have not verified it. Under the prize's rules, a solution must be published and withstand two years of scrutiny before it can be recognized. The day's biggest claim, in other words, is still only a claim.

受雇于 Anthropic 的数学家 Levent Alpöge 周五称，他在 Claude 的帮助下证明了黎曼猜想。这个问题提出于 1859 年，关乎素数的分布，经过验证的解法可获得一百万美元奖金。Alpöge 说他用 Lean 写成了论证——这是一种让计算机逐步检查逻辑的语言——并称该方法可推广到更一般的版本。他把成果发布在个人网站上；Anthropic 未予置评，外部专家也尚未验证。按该奖项规则，解法必须公开并经受两年审查才可能获承认。换句话说，当天最大胆的宣称，目前仍只是一个宣称。

2. OpenAI Maps Its Defenses Against Distillation

摘要：OpenAI 11 月 20 日发文详述如何防范对抗性蒸馏——未经许可用前沿模型输出训练第二个模型、继承能力却丢掉防护。公司特别保护「隐藏推理」；内部红队测试中，升级后模型从早前请求恢复思维链的成功率由 54% 降至约 0.2%。这些数字来自 OpenAI 自家红队，非外部复现。

OpenAI described how it tries to stop adversarial distillation, training a second model on a frontier model's outputs without permission. The worry is that the copy inherits the capability but not the safeguards. The company focuses on hidden reasoning, the intermediate steps a final answer leaves out. In an internal test, an updated model cut the rate at which attackers recovered that reasoning from an earlier request from fifty-four percent to about zero point two percent. Those figures come from OpenAI's own red team, not outside researchers. Protected reasoning is now tied to the conversation that produced it, so rewriting an earlier message drops the reasoning that followed. It is a reminder that safety depends partly on things users never see.

OpenAI 发布长文，详述它如何试图阻止对抗性蒸馏——即未经许可用一个前沿模型的输出去训练第二个模型，让副本继承能力却丢掉防护。公司尤其关注模型的「隐藏推理」，即最终答案略去的中间步骤。在一项内部测试中，升级后的模型把攻击者从较早请求中恢复该推理的成功率，从 54% 降到约 0.2%。这些数字来自 OpenAI 自家红队，而非外部研究者。公司还说，受保护的推理现在与产生它的对话绑定，因此改写早前消息会丢弃其后的推理。这篇文章提醒人们，模型的安全性部分取决于用户永远看不到的东西。

3. Google Puts AI Buttons Where Search Used to Be

摘要：Google 经过数周测试后正式推出重新设计的桌面搜索主页：熟悉的「Google 搜索」按钮被移除，搜索框下改为图片生成、文件分析与 Gemini 脑暴等 AI 按钮，「手气不错」保留；登录用户可见最近 AI 模式对话侧栏。Google 搜索副总裁 Robby Stein 在 X 上确认发布。

Google rolled out a redesigned desktop search homepage after several weeks of testing. The familiar Google Search button is gone. In its place, under the search bar, sit buttons for generating images, analyzing files and brainstorming with Gemini, while the lucky button remains. Signed-in users get an expandable sidebar listing recent AI Mode threads, which also appears on results pages. Google's search vice president, Robby Stein, confirmed the rollout on X, writing that the page was going out after a successful test. Ars Technica noted that the autocomplete list covers the old button position as you type, so most people simply press Enter and lose little. The change looks small on screen but carries a large signal: AI features move to the front, and classic search quietly steps back.

Google 经过数周测试后，正式推出重新设计的桌面搜索主页。熟悉的「Google 搜索」按钮消失了。取而代之的是搜索框下方的几个按钮：生成图片、分析文件、与 Gemini 一起头脑风暴，而「手气不错」按钮仍保留。登录用户会看到一个可展开的侧栏，列出最近的 AI 模式对话，结果页上也会出现。Google 搜索副总裁 Robby Stein 在 X 上确认了这次发布，他在这个社交平台上写道，页面在测试成功后开始推送。Ars Technica 指出，输入时自动补全列表会盖住旧按钮的位置，所以大多数人直接按回车，几乎没什么损失。这个改动在屏幕上看很小，在信号上却很大：AI 功能被推到最前，传统搜索则悄悄后退。

4. Nvidia Moves on Diffusion Language Models

摘要：据 The Information，英伟达接近收购成立两年的 Inception，交易额至少 5 亿美元，含 30 多名员工奖金，三位教授创始人预计全职加入。Inception 用扩散方法构建语言模型——逐层精修整段答案而非逐个 token 生成，最新模型名为 Mercury 3。双方均未公开置评；英伟达意在强化开源 Nemotron 与机器人物理 AI 模型。

Nvidia is close to acquiring Inception, a two-year-old startup that builds language models using diffusion, according to The Information. Unlike ChatGPT and Claude, which write an answer one token at a time, a diffusion model refines the whole answer step by step, a method borrowed from image generation. Inception's latest model is called Mercury Three. The reported deal is worth at least five hundred million dollars and includes bonuses for more than thirty employees; the three professor founders are expected to join Nvidia full time. Neither company has commented publicly. Nvidia reportedly wants the technology to strengthen its open Nemotron models and its physical AI models for robots. The move shows a chip maker buying not just capacity, but a different way for models to think.

据 The Information 报道，英伟达接近收购成立仅两年的初创公司 Inception，该公司用扩散方法构建语言模型。与 ChatGPT 和 Claude 逐个 token 写出答案不同，扩散模型借用了图像生成的方法，一步步精修整个答案。Inception 最新的模型叫 Mercury 3。据报道，这笔交易价值至少五亿美元，包括三十多名员工的奖金，三位教授创始人预计将全职加入英伟达。两家公司都未公开置评。据报道，英伟达希望用这项技术强化其开源的 Nemotron 模型和面向机器人的物理 AI 模型。这一举动显示，一家芯片制造商买下的不只是算力，而是一种让模型思考的不同方式。

5. DeepMind Looks Past AGI

摘要：Google DeepMind 发布《AGI 之后是什么？》文章，认为通用人工智能只是连续谱上的一个点而非终点，并勾勒通向「人工超级智能」的四条路径——若一条受阻，其他路径仍可推进进展。文章由联合创始人兼首席 AGI 科学家 Shane Legg 在 X 上发布，属研究观点而非 Google 官方立场。

Google DeepMind published an essay titled What Comes After AGI, arguing that artificial general intelligence is a point on a continuum rather than a destination. The piece was announced on X by Shane Legg, DeepMind's co-founder. It describes four paths toward what the authors call artificial superintelligence, systems that would outperform teams of human experts on hard tasks while using fewer resources. The core claim is that if one route stalls, the others can still carry progress forward; even if single models improve slowly, many agents working together can raise total output. The authors say timing is hard to predict and urge preparation now. It is a research essay, not a policy statement, but it shows how seriously the lab takes a world beyond human-level systems.

Google DeepMind 发布了一篇题为《AGI 之后是什么？》的文章，主张通用人工智能只是连续谱上的一个点，而非终点。这篇文章由 DeepMind 联合创始人兼首席 AGI 科学家 Shane Legg 在 X 上发布。文中描述了通向作者所称「人工超级智能」的四条路径——这类系统能在困难、耗时的任务上胜过人类专家大团队，同时消耗更少资源。核心论点是：如果一条路径受阻，其他路径仍能推动进展；即便单个模型提升缓慢，多个智能体协同也能提高总产出。作者说时机难以预测，敦促现在就开始研究和准备。这是一篇研究文章，并非 Google 的政策声明，但它显示出该实验室对超越人类水平系统的世界有多认真。

6. The Evaluation Gets Harder

摘要：MIT Technology Review 报道，非营利机构 METR 更新了「时间视野」能力图：旧图显示模型可完成的任务长度约每四个月翻倍，新版加入更长、更开放的任务。一项新任务中，模型需为 METR 研究设计 AI 评测，Anthropic 最新模型勉强越线，一年前所有模型都明显不及；但在判断类任务上仍落后人类。

MIT Technology Review reported on an update to a capability chart kept by METR, a nonprofit that tests frontier models. The old chart tracked how the length of tasks models could complete roughly doubled every four months. The new version adds longer, more open-ended work. On one fresh task, a model was asked to design an AI evaluation for METR's research; Anthropic's latest model landed just above the bar, where a year ago all models were clearly below it. Yet on tasks that need judgment, such as grading philosophical arguments, models still fell short of people. OpenAI said its models do worse on real staff work than on easy-to-check tasks. METR cautions that clean trends for judgment-heavy work are hard to establish. Capability rises fastest where answers are easy to verify.

MIT Technology Review 报道了非营利机构 METR 所维护能力图的一次更新。该机构负责测试前沿模型。旧图追踪的是：模型能完成的任务长度大约每四个月翻一番。新版加入了更长、更开放的任务。在一项新任务里，模型被要求为 METR 自己的研究设计一项 AI 评测；Anthropic 最新模型刚好越过门槛，而一年前所有模型都明显低于门槛。然而在需要判断的任务上，比如给哲学论证评分，模型仍不及人类。OpenAI 另称，其模型在员工指派的真实工作上，表现比原始测试集中易于核查的任务更差。METR 提醒，对判断密集型工作很难得出干净的趋势。图景并不均衡：在答案容易验证的地方，能力上升最快。

朗读建议（Reading Tips）

  • **语速**：全文用中速（约 110~120 词/分钟），小标题稍放慢、加重音；配套音频按新闻播报语速（约 150 词/分钟）生成。
  • In Brief 摘要段可作开嗓热身：六条各一句，末尾 still only a claim 前后各停半秒，点出「宣称跑在验证前面」这条主线。
  • 第 1 篇 eighteen fifty-nine、one million dollar prize、two years of scrutiny 放慢读清；Riemann hypothesis 读清每个音节。
  • 第 2 篇 adversarial distillation 读清重音；fifty-four percent 到 zero point two percent 的落差处稍作停顿。
  • 第 3 篇 Robby Stein 后作半秒停顿交代身份；rolled out、autocomplete、sidebar 连读，末句经典搜索后退平稳收束。

  • 第 5 篇 What Comes After AGI 作书名读；Shane Legg 后停顿；artificial superintelligence 逐音节读清。
  • 第 6 篇 METR 读作 em-ee-tee-ar；open-ended、judgment、easy to verify 是关键短语，末句平稳收束。
  • **节奏**：每篇小标题后停顿 1 秒再开始正文；段落之间停顿 2 秒。全文没有结尾总结段，读完第 6 篇即可收束。
