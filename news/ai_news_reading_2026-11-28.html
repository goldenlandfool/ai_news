1. Google's Agent Writes Six Math Papers

摘要：Google Research 在 GitHub 一次公开 6 篇由 Gemini 4 驱动的多智能体系统 Cogentic 写成的数学论文，共约 274 页；宣称在整数分解上取得 1992 年以来首个无条件亚指数算法，但 Lean 4 证明尚未公开、未经同行评审。

Google Research added six mathematics papers to a public GitHub folder this week, all written by a multi-agent system called Cogentic that runs on Gemini 4. They cover integer factoring, discrete logarithms, game theory and graph theory, roughly 274 pages in all. The repository says each paper and its Lean 4 formalization were produced after one initial prompt, with no further human interaction. The headline result claims a zero-error algorithm that factors any integer in sub-exponential time without unproven assumptions, a barrier untouched since 1992. The constant is large, so existing encryption is not at risk. The caveat matters more: the Lean files are not yet published, the papers are not peer reviewed, and the folder carries a disclaimer. Research is now written faster than people can check it.

Google Research 本周向一个公开 GitHub 文件夹添加了六篇数学论文，全部由一个名为 Cogentic、运行在 Gemini 4 上的多智能体系统写成。论文涵盖整数分解、离散对数、博弈论和图论，共约 274 页。仓库称，每篇论文及其 Lean 4 形式化证明都是在一条初始提示后生成的，没有进一步的人工互动。头条成果宣称给出一个零错误算法，在不依赖未证假设的情况下，以亚指数时间分解任意整数，打破了自 1992 年以来停滞的理论壁垒。常数很大，因此现有加密并无风险。提醒比结论更重要：Lean 文件尚未发布，论文未经同行评审，文件夹带有免责声明。智能体写研究的速度，已快过人们核查的速度。

2. Volvo Puts a Humanoid on the Line

摘要：沃尔沃把 Hexagon 的 AEON 人形机器人送进比利时根特工厂，用模仿学习训练其高精度放置零件；机器人轮式移动、载重 10 公斤，计划 2027 上半年试点、年中投入实际生产，目的是分担重体力活而非裁员。

Volvo Cars said this week it has moved AEON humanoid robots into its Ghent plant in Belgium. AEON is 1.65 metres tall, weighs 60 kilograms and rolls on wheels rather than legs, reaching 2.4 metres per second and carrying up to 10 kilograms. Volvo's team is training the robots with imitation learning: the machine copies a task by watching a person do it. The first job is placing components into fixtures with high precision. As each step is proven, tasks get harder, including handling several parts and moving around the floor. A pilot is planned for the first half of 2027, with production from mid-2027. Volvo says the point is not to replace staff but to take the most demanding tasks and make work more ergonomic.

沃尔沃汽车本周表示，已把 AEON 人形机器人送进比利时根特的工厂，这是沃尔沃首家试用此类机器人的工厂。AEON 高 1.65 米、重 60 公斤，靠轮子而非双腿移动，速度可达每秒 2.4 米，载重最多 10 公斤。沃尔沃自己的团队用模仿学习训练机器人：机器通过观看人的动作来模仿任务。第一项工作是把零件高精度地放进夹具。每一步得到验证后，任务难度会提高，包括处理多个零件、在车间内移动。试点计划于 2027 年上半年进行，2027 年中投入实际生产。沃尔沃说，目的不是取代员工，而是接手体力消耗最大的任务，让工作更符合人体工学。

3. Google Gives Workspace Agents a Deadline

摘要：谷歌定下 2027 年 2 月 1 日为期限，Workspace 智能体须通过 MCP 服务器与命令行工具访问 Gmail、Drive 等，不能直调旧 REST API；OAuth 客户端须声明智能体身份，且 Gmail 的 MCP 服务器目前不能发邮件。

Google has set a deadline for AI agents inside Workspace. From 1 February 2027, agents must reach Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Chat and Tasks through MCP servers and a Workspace command-line tool, rather than calling REST APIs directly. Billing for scaled use begins on 1 April. Agent OAuth clients must now declare that they are agents, so their traffic can be told apart from ordinary users. MCP, the Model Context Protocol, is an open standard for connecting models to tools. One limitation has drawn attention: Google's Gmail MCP server cannot send email, so developers who assumed that capability must redesign their workflows. Agent plumbing is becoming a platform with rules, dates and prices.

谷歌为 Workspace 中的 AI 智能体定下了日期。自 2027 年 2 月 1 日起，智能体必须通过 MCP 服务器和一个 Workspace 命令行工具访问 Gmail、Drive、Docs、Sheets、Slides、Calendar、Chat 和 Tasks，而不能再直接调用较旧的 REST 接口。规模化使用的计费从 4 月 1 日开始。智能体的 OAuth 客户端现在必须声明自己是智能体，使其流量能与普通用户区分开。MCP 即模型上下文协议，是把模型连接到工具的开放标准。一个限制引发关注：谷歌的 Gmail MCP 服务器不能发送邮件，因此原本假定具备该能力的开发者必须重新设计工作流。信号很明确：智能体的管线正变成一个带规则、日期和价格的平台。

4. Nvidia Pauses Shipments to Four Markets

摘要：《华尔街日报》援引报道称，英伟达要求戴尔、超微等伙伴暂停向马来西亚、新加坡、泰国、印尼四国出货服务器以核查出口合规；远程租用中国公司算力被视为漏洞，新加坡称暂停传闻未获证实。

Nvidia has told partners, including Dell and Super Micro, to pause server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia, according to The Wall Street Journal, as reported by TechCrunch. The pause has no stated end date. Nvidia is reviewing whether regional partners meet United States export requirements. The four markets drew attention because Chinese companies had been using Nvidia chips in remote data centres there, a route Washington moved to close earlier this month. A think tank estimates such sites supply about 24 percent of China's AI computing power. Cloud providers have ninety days to obtain a licence and show no restricted customers among their users. Singapore said reports of a halt were unconfirmed. The focus is shifting from where chips are sold to who uses the compute.

据《华尔街日报》报道、《TechCrunch》转述，英伟达已告知包括戴尔、超微在内的制造伙伴，暂停向马来西亚、新加坡、泰国和印尼出货服务器，且未说明期限。英伟达正在核查区域伙伴是否符合美国出口要求。这四个市场受到关注，是因为中国公司一直在从当地数据中心远程使用英伟达芯片，而华盛顿本月早些时候已着手封堵这条路径。一家智库估计，这类远程数据中心提供了中国约 24% 的 AI 算力。云服务商有 90 天时间取得许可，并证明受限客户不在其用户之列。新加坡称有关暂停的报道尚未得到证实。变化在于：监管的重点正从芯片卖到哪里，转向最终是谁在用这些算力。

5. An Agent That Reads Mass Spectra

摘要：Google DeepMind 发布 Mass Spec Skills，让 Antigravity 智能体调用公开化学工具与数据库，从串联质谱识别分子；在 100 个基准化合物上准确率约 97%（无技能时不足 4%），但仍无法区分镜像分子，结果需化学家复核。

Google DeepMind released Mass Spec Skills for its Antigravity platform, a set of skills that lets an agent run established chemistry tools and query public databases to identify molecules from tandem mass spectra. Standard library searches can name only about five percent of measured spectra. The agent preprocesses the spectrum, searches reference libraries, infers a formula, pulls candidate structures from PubChem and tests each against the fragments, then lays out the evidence for a chemist to review. On a hundred benchmark compounds, DeepMind reports about 97 percent accuracy across three Gemini models, including the lightweight one; without the skills, accuracy stayed below four percent. The agent still cannot separate mirror-image molecules or confirm structures absent from databases. Results are a starting point, not a verdict.

Google DeepMind 为其 Antigravity 平台发布了 Mass Spec Skills，这是一套技能，让智能体能够调用成熟的化学工具、查询公开数据库，从串联质谱中识别分子。常规数据库检索只能为约 5% 的实测谱图命名，这套技能试图缩小缺口。智能体先对谱图预处理，再检索参考库、推断分子式、从 PubChem 调取候选结构，并用观测到的碎片逐一验证，最后把证据摆出来交给化学家审核。在 100 个基准化合物上，DeepMind 报告三个 Gemini 模型（包括轻量版）的准确率约为 97%；没有这些技能时，准确率不到 4%。该智能体仍无法区分镜像分子，也无法确认数据库中不存在的结构。结果只是起点，不是定论。

6. A Cheaper King of Video

摘要：Artificial Analysis 最新榜单中，Sand AI 的 MAGI-2 登顶图生视频，可生成 30 秒 1080p 有声视频，成本约每分钟 4.8 美元，不到 Seedance 2.5 的七分之一；视频生成的竞争开始从画质转向单位成本。

Artificial Analysis put a new model at the top of its image-to-video leaderboard this week. MAGI-2, from Sand AI, can generate thirty-second clips at 1080p with sound, and the evaluation puts the cost at about 4.8 dollars per minute of video. That is less than one seventh the price of Seedance 2.5, which many developers had used as a reference. The ranking matters because video generation has been judged mostly on visual quality; price per finished minute is now a headline number, the way cost per token became one for text models. For studios and marketers generating many variations, a sevenfold gap changes which experiments are worth running. Quality still counts, but the economics of video are being rewritten.

Artificial Analysis 本周把一款新模型放到其图生视频榜首。Sand AI 的 MAGI-2 能生成 30 秒、1080p、带声音的视频片段，评测给出的成本约为每分钟视频 4.8 美元。这不到 Seedance 2.5 价格的七分之一，而后者是许多开发者的参照基准。这一排名很重要，因为视频生成此前主要按画质评判；如今每分钟成品价格成了头条数字，就像文本模型的每词元成本一样。对于要生成大量不同版本的制片厂和营销人员，七倍的价差会改变哪些实验值得去做。画质依然重要，但视频的经济账正在被重写。

朗读建议（Reading Tips）

  • 语速：全文用中速（约 110~120 词/分钟）朗读；配套音频按约 150 词/分钟生成，两种节奏可分别练习。
  • In Brief 摘要段可作开嗓热身：六个主题各一句，句与句之间停半秒，末句 The theme is infrastructure 放慢收住。
  • 第 1 篇术语密集：multi-agent、Lean 4、sub-exponential、peer reviewed，逐个读清；末句 faster than people can check it 略降速。
  • 第 2 篇数字较多：1.65 metres、60 kilograms、2.4 metres per second、10 kilograms，每个数字后停半秒；读准 imitation learning。
  • 第 3 篇日期读清楚：1 February 2027、1 April；Gmail、Drive、Docs 一串并列名词用同一语调，别越读越快。
  • 第 4 篇国名连读：Malaysia、Singapore、Thailand and Indonesia；twenty-four percent、ninety days 重读，末句 who ends up using the compute 收住。
  • 第 5 篇 five percent、97 percent、below four percent 三处对比重读；mass spectra、PubChem 读清，末句 not a verdict 停住。
  • 第 6 篇 4.8 dollars、one seventh、sevenfold 放慢读准；末句 the economics of video are being rewritten 略降速，读出结论。
  • 节奏：每篇小标题后停 1 秒再开始正文，段落之间停 2 秒。全文没有结尾总结段，读完第 6 篇即可收束。
