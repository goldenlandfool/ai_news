1. OpenAI and Anthropic Say Their Models Cracked Three Famous Problems

摘要：OpenAI 周一宣布三项由模型产出的科学成果：解决 Kakeya 猜想、反驳「指数时间假设」（ETH）、为一类量子场论给出严格存在性证明；数小时后 Anthropic 也发布了自己的 ETH 反驳，并指 OpenAI 的版本依托前人工作——目前均属「主张」而非公认结果。

OpenAI claims its models have helped crack three of the hardest open problems in science. On Monday the lab announced results in mathematics, computer science, and physics. The mathematics result resolves the Kakeya conjecture, which asks how little space is needed to rotate a needle in every direction. The computer science result claims to refute the Exponential Time Hypothesis, a thirty-year-old assumption about which problems can never be solved quickly. The physics result is a rigorous existence proof for a class of quantum field theories. Within hours, Anthropic released its own refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis, and one of its mathematicians suggested OpenAI's version leaned on earlier work. These are claims, not yet accepted results. But the race between the labs is now being run on exam questions that humans could not answer.

OpenAI 声称其模型帮助攻克了科学界三个最难的未解问题。周一，这家实验室公布了数学、计算机科学和物理三个领域的成果。数学成果解决了 Kakeya 猜想——这个问题问的是，把一根针转向所有方向，最少需要多大的空间。计算机科学成果声称反驳了「指数时间假设」，这是一个有 30 年历史的假设，关乎哪些问题永远无法被快速求解。物理成果则为一类量子场论给出了严格的存在性证明。数小时后，Anthropic 发布了它自己对指数时间假设的反驳，其一位数学家还暗示 OpenAI 的版本依托了前人的工作。这些目前都是主张，还不是公认的结果。但实验室之间的竞赛，如今已经跑在了人类自己答不出的考题上。

2. Researchers Push Back on How OpenAI Announces Breakthroughs

摘要：OpenAI 这次按「领域」区别发布：数学结果直接公开、计算机科学结果交给外部团队解释、物理结果完全扣住待「shepherd」定夺；学界不买账——AGMAI 提前一天警告过不要内定读者，MIT 的 Ryan Williams 称证明里有「两三个看起来像外星人的部分」，呼吁全社区一起验证。

The reaction was almost as interesting as the results. OpenAI released the mathematics output openly, assembled outside experts to explain the computer science finding, and withheld the physics result entirely, deferring to a group it calls shepherds. Researchers told The Verge they are tired of the arrangement. An advisory group called AGMAI had warned the lab, one day earlier, against choosing who reads a result first. MIT professor Ryan Williams, who once called an earlier release a DDOS attack on mathematicians, agreed to help interpret the new work. Some parts, he said, are routine, but there are two or three alien-looking parts, and the whole community needs to work on them together. Science is starting to ship like software, and the scientific community is negotiating its review process in public.

学术界的反应，几乎和成果本身一样有意思。OpenAI 这次把数学结果直接公开，请外部专家来解释计算机科学成果，而物理结果则完全扣住不发，交给一个它称为「shepherd（领路人）」的团队定夺。接受 The Verge 采访的研究者表示，他们已经厌倦了这种安排。一个名为 AGMAI 的顾问团体提前一天就警告过这家实验室：不要由你来决定谁先看到结果。MIT 教授 Ryan Williams 曾把上一次成果投放称为「对数学家的 DDOS 攻击」，这次同意帮忙解读新工作。他说，有些部分是常规的，但有两三个部分「看起来像外星来的」，整个社区需要一起研究它们。科学正在像软件一样「发版」，而科学共同体正在公开场合讨价还价自己的评审流程。

3. Google DeepMind's Genie 4 Generates Worlds You Can Steer in Real Time

摘要：DeepMind 发布新一代世界模型 Genie 4：可实时生成并修改可交互虚拟环境（改天气、移动镜头、添加角色），首次原生生成音频（音乐/环境音/对话），会话时长约 2 分钟翻倍，由 Gemini 3.8 Flash 驱动，研究预览阶段；官方承认模拟越长细节越易漂移。

Google DeepMind unveiled Genie 4, the latest version of its world model. A world model generates an interactive environment rather than a static picture. You can steer through it with a keyboard, change the weather, move the camera, or add characters, and watch the world respond in real time. Genie 4 adds native audio, so music, ambient sound, and dialogue are generated alongside the scene, and sessions now run about two minutes, double the previous model. It is powered by Gemini 3.8 Flash, and it launches as a research preview for trusted testers, with wider access planned for AI Ultra subscribers. Google points to film pre-visualization, game prototyping, and robot training as uses. The company also admits a limit: as simulations run longer, details can drift. Persistent worlds remain hard.

Google DeepMind 发布了其世界模型的最新版本 Genie 4。所谓世界模型，生成的不是一个静态画面，而是一个可交互的环境。你可以用键盘在其中穿行，改变天气、移动镜头、添加角色，然后看着这个世界实时做出反应。Genie 4 首次原生支持音频，音乐、环境音和对话会与场景同步生成；单次会话时长约 2 分钟，是上一代的两倍。它由 Gemini 3.8 Flash 驱动，先以研究预览的形式向受邀测试者开放，之后计划向 AI Ultra 订阅用户扩大范围。Google 给出的用途包括电影预演、游戏原型和机器人训练。公司也承认一个局限：模拟运行的时间越长，细节越容易出现漂移。持久稳定的世界，依然很难。

4. Google's Chips Are Now Running Inside Oracle's Cloud

摘要：Oracle AI World 上，甲骨文宣布把 ChatGPT 和 Codex 集成进自家企业应用，Altman 登台站台；更大新闻在硬件——谷歌 TPU 首次在另一家超大规模云上运行，与 OpenAI 自研 Jalapeño 芯片、Cerebras 一起进入 OCI；英伟达同期把 Vera Rubin 扩展到 OCI 多吉瓦规模。

Oracle opened its cloud even wider. At Oracle AI World in Las Vegas, the company announced it will embed ChatGPT and Codex directly into its business applications, and OpenAI's Sam Altman joined Oracle's co-chief executive on stage. The bigger news may be hardware. Oracle said Google's TPU chips will now run on Oracle Cloud, the first time Google's custom silicon has operated inside another major cloud provider, alongside OpenAI's own Jalapeno chip and Cerebras processors. Oracle's president was careful to add that Nvidia chips remain the best and most general option. Nvidia, meanwhile, expanded its Vera Rubin systems on Oracle's infrastructure to multi-gigawatt scale. The infrastructure race is no longer a single-vendor story. The big clouds are increasingly hosting each other's chips.

甲骨文把自己的云开得更大了。在拉斯维加斯的 Oracle AI World 大会上，公司宣布将把 ChatGPT 和 Codex 直接集成到它的企业应用中，OpenAI 的 Sam Altman 与甲骨文联席 CEO 同台亮相。更大的新闻可能在硬件上。甲骨文表示，谷歌的 TPU 芯片将开始在甲骨文云上运行——这是谷歌自研芯片第一次进入另一家大型云服务商的地盘，同场还有 OpenAI 自研的 Jalapeño 芯片和 Cerebras 处理器。甲骨文总裁谨慎地补充说，英伟达芯片仍然是最好、最通用的选择。与此同时，英伟达把其 Vera Rubin 系统在甲骨文基础设施上的部署扩展到了多吉瓦规模。基础设施竞赛已不再是一家厂商的故事。大型云厂商之间，正越来越多地互相托管对方的芯片。

5. Wall Street Puts Up 63 Billion Dollars for Anthropic's Chips

摘要：美银、黑石、花旗、摩根士丹利将提供 630 亿美元融资，为 Anthropic 采购博通 AI 加速器与网络设备——新设 SPV 购买硬件再租给 Anthropic，2027 年起交付，博通为优先级贷款提供「残值支持」；这是与 AI 基建相关的最大债务交易之一（Reuters）。

The financing behind all this silicon keeps growing. Bank of America, Blackstone, Citigroup, and Morgan Stanley have agreed to provide sixty-three billion dollars to finance Broadcom chips for Anthropic, in one of the largest debt deals tied to the AI buildout, Reuters reported. A newly formed special purpose vehicle will purchase Broadcom's AI accelerators and networking equipment and lease them to the maker of Claude, with deliveries beginning in 2027. Broadcom is providing residual value support, effectively guaranteeing the senior lenders against falling chip prices. It echoes the forty billion dollar lease SpaceX signed with Apollo, disclosed just a day earlier. Frontier labs are starting to look less like software startups and more like airlines: they fly enormous, borrowed fleets, and the business depends on keeping every accelerator full.

这一切硅片背后的融资，还在继续膨胀。据路透社报道，美国银行、黑石、花旗和摩根士丹利已同意提供 630 亿美元，为 Anthropic 采购博通芯片提供融资——这是与 AI 基建相关的最大债务交易之一。一家新设的特殊目的载体将购买博通的 AI 加速器和网络设备，再把它们租给 Claude 的开发者，2027 年开始交付。博通提供「残值支持」，实际上是为优先级贷款人担保芯片跌价的风险。这与前一天刚披露的 SpaceX 与 Apollo 签下的 400 亿美元租赁如出一辙。前沿实验室看起来越来越不像软件创业公司，而更像航空公司：它们驾驶着庞大而借来的机队，生意的好坏取决于每一台加速器是否坐满。

6. Musk Says Robots Will Out-Consume the World's Computers on Memory

摘要：马斯克 10/27 在 X 回应网友称：机器人对内存的需求将超过地球上其他一切，AI 从处理信息走向物理现实后机器人内存消耗将碾压所有现有计算设备，且机器人用的是低功耗 DRAM 而非 HBM；受此带动，西部数据收涨约 7%，美光、希捷跟涨（腾讯新闻/每经）。

Finally, a social media post moved markets. On X, responding to a question about robots and memory, Elon Musk wrote that robots will need more memory than anything else on Earth. As artificial intelligence moves from processing information into physical reality, he argued, robots will consume more memory than all existing computing devices combined. He added that robots use low-power DRAM rather than high-bandwidth memory, so chipmakers could see a much larger market as AI spreads beyond data centers. Storage stocks rallied on the comment. Western Digital closed up about seven percent, and Micron and Seagate also gained. One post from one account repriced an entire sector, for at least one afternoon.

最后，是一条撼动了市场的社交媒体帖子。在 X 上回应一个关于机器人与内存的提问时，马斯克写道：机器人对内存的需求，将超过地球上的任何其他事物。他主张，当人工智能从处理信息走向物理现实，机器人消耗的内存将超过所有现有计算设备的总和。他还补充说，机器人使用的是低功耗 DRAM 而不是高带宽内存，因此随着 AI 扩展到数据中心之外，芯片制造商可能迎来一个大得多的市场。存储类股票应声上涨。西部数据收盘涨约 7%，美光和希捷也纷纷走高。来自一个账号的一条帖子，为一个整个板块重新定了价——至少定了一个下午。

Closing Thought

Today's stories share one quiet theme: verification. Three impossible-sounding proofs now wait for human referees. A world model's worlds start to drift, and Google says so itself. Chips are leased rather than bought, because nobody can pay cash for the future. And a single post can move billions, with no fact-checker in sight. The models keep getting faster at producing claims. The bottleneck is the slower part: who checks them, how long it takes, and what it costs. That part is still done by us.

今天的新闻共享一个安静的主题：验证。三个听起来不可能的证明，现在正等待人类评审员。世界模型生成的世界会漂移，Google 自己也这么说。芯片靠租而不是买，因为没人付得起未来的现金。而一条帖子就能撬动几十亿资金，身边却没有事实核查员。模型 producing claims 的速度越来越快。瓶颈在于更慢的那部分：谁来核查、要花多久、成本几何。这部分，仍然由我们亲手完成。

朗读建议（Reading Tips）

  • 全文用中速朗读（约 110~120 词/分钟）；「朗读」按钮的音频按新闻播报语速（约 150 词/分钟）生成，可先跟读再脱稿。
  • 第 1 篇 "These are claims, not yet accepted results" 是本篇的态度句，claims 重读，逗号处停半拍
  • 第 2 篇引语 "two or three alien-looking parts" 语速放慢，alien-looking 可拆读为 alien / looking
  • 第 3 篇结尾 "details can drift" 句末降调，给「漂移」这个词留一点余味
  • 第 4 篇 "the best and most general option" 三个词逐个咬清，这是甲骨文对英伟达的谨慎评价
  • 第 5 篇比喻句 "they fly enormous, borrowed fleets" 放慢，borrowed 是全句重心
  • 第 6 篇引语 "more memory than anything else on Earth" 重读 more，句末下沉
  • 结尾 "That part is still done by us." 是全篇落点，放慢并降调，读完停一拍再结束
