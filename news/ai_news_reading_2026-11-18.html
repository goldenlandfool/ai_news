1. OpenAI Ships Astra and Sol

摘要：OpenAI 于 11 月 17 日发布 GPT-6.2 Astra（复杂专业任务）与 Sol（日常编程写作）；Astra 基准创新高且成本更低，API 输入/输出降价 20%、缓存输入降价 60%；官方 X 账号发帖宣布，Astra 成为首个在自我改进维度达高风险等级的公开模型，安全限制同步收紧。

OpenAI released two models on Tuesday: GPT-6.2 Astra for complex professional work, and GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday coding and writing. Astra set new highs on tests of office and research tasks, the company says, while using fewer output tokens and costing less per job. Its application programming interface cuts input and output prices by twenty percent, and cached-input prices by sixty percent. Sol keeps its old price but lets users finish more tasks on the same plan. Both are available to Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Education users. On X, the official account announced the release. OpenAI also warned that Astra is its first public model to reach a high rating for helping improve AI itself, so it added stricter safety limits.

OpenAI 于周二发布了两款模型：面向复杂专业工作的 GPT-6.2 Astra，以及面向日常编程与写作的 GPT-6.2 Sol。公司称，Astra 在办公与科研任务测试中创下新高，同时输出 token 更少、单项任务成本更低。其应用程序编程接口将输入与输出价格下调 20%，缓存输入价格下调 60%。Sol 维持原价，但让用户在同一套餐内完成更多任务。两款模型面向 Plus、Pro、Enterprise 和 Education 用户开放。在 X 上，官方账号发布了这一消息。OpenAI 还警告说，Astra 是其首个在帮助改进 AI 自身方面达到高风险等级的公开模型，因此收紧了安全限制。

2. Microsoft's Seven-Model Answer

摘要：微软在 Ignite 2026 大会发布七款自研模型：旗舰编程模型 MAI Code 2 以约 40% 的价格达到 GPT-6.1 Sol 98% 的质量；安全模型 MAI Cyber 2 Flash 以不足 2% 的单任务成本领先 GPT-6 Astra；并推出图像编辑、转录与路由模型，允许可信客户授权使用模型权重。

Microsoft used its Ignite conference on Tuesday to launch seven in-house models, arguing that price and speed matter as much as raw capability. Its flagship coding model, MAI Code 2, reaches ninety-eight percent of the quality of GPT-6.1 Sol at about forty percent of the price, by Microsoft's own measure. A faster version, Code 2 Flash, handles routine edits. For security, MAI Cyber 2 Flash scores ahead of GPT-6 Astra on a security test at under two percent of the cost per task. The company also released an image-editing model, a transcription model, and a routing model that sends work to the right place. Microsoft said it will let trusted customers licence its model weights.

微软在周二的 Ignite 大会上发布了七款自研模型，主张价格与速度和原始能力同样重要。其旗舰编程模型 MAI Code 2，按微软自己的测量，以约 40% 的价格达到 GPT-6.1 Sol 98% 的质量。更快的版本 Code 2 Flash 负责常规修改。在安全方面，MAI Cyber 2 Flash 在一项安全测试中领先 GPT-6 Astra，而单任务成本不足其 2%。公司还发布了图像编辑模型、转录模型，以及一个把任务送往合适模型的路由模型。微软表示，将允许可信客户授权使用其模型权重。

3. A Quarter Above One Hundred Billion

摘要：英伟达 2027 财年第三季度营收 1158 亿美元，同比增 103%，首次单季破千亿；数据中心收入 1076 亿、占比约 93%；Vera Rubin NVL72 以史上最快速度出货；下季指引 1300 亿美元，但未计入中国市场数据中心收入。

Nvidia reported revenue of one hundred fifteen point eight billion dollars for its third fiscal quarter, up one hundred three percent from a year earlier. It was the first time the company passed one hundred billion dollars in a single quarter. Data centre revenue reached one hundred seven point six billion dollars, about ninety-three percent of the total. Its new Vera Rubin NVL72 system had begun shipping at the fastest pace in its history, with racks running at partners including Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure. For the next quarter, Nvidia guided to one hundred thirty billion dollars, but said the figure assumes no data centre revenue from China. Colette Kress, the interim chief executive, said the roadmap is on track.

英伟达公布第三财季营收 1158 亿美元，同比增长 103%，这是公司单季营收首次突破 1000 亿美元。数据中心收入达 1076 亿美元，约占总收入的 93%。其新的 Vera Rubin NVL72 系统已开始出货，速度创公司历史最快，机架已在谷歌云和微软 Azure 等合作伙伴处运行。对于下一季度，英伟达给出 1300 亿美元的指引，但称该数字未计入来自中国的数据中心收入。临时首席执行官科莱特·克雷斯表示，路线图进展顺利。

4. AMD Welcomes Li Fei-Fei

摘要：AMD 完成以约 82 亿美元全股票收购 World Labs，创始人李飞飞出任执行副总裁兼首席科学家，直接向苏姿丰汇报，为 AMD 史上第二大收购；World Labs 主攻空间智能与世界模型；AMD 官方 X 账号晒出二人合影，李飞飞称 AI 是「文明级技术」。

AMD completed its eight point two billion dollar purchase of World Labs, the startup founded by computer scientist Fei-Fei Li. Li, often called the godmother of AI, became executive vice president and chief scientist, reporting directly to chair and chief executive Lisa Su. The all-stock deal, first agreed in late September, is AMD's second largest ever. World Labs works on spatial intelligence and world models, which try to teach machines to reason about the physical world, not just text. Li said AI is a civilisational technology and that she was excited to bring the team to AMD. On X, the official AMD account posted a photo of the two women, writing that a picture is worth a thousand words.

AMD 完成了对 World Labs 的收购，交易价值 82 亿美元，这家初创公司由计算机科学家李飞飞创立。李飞飞常被称为「AI 教母」，出任执行副总裁兼首席科学家，直接向董事长兼首席执行官苏姿丰汇报。这笔全股票交易于 9 月下旬首次达成，是 AMD 史上第二大收购。World Labs 致力于空间智能与世界模型，试图教机器对物理世界而非仅仅是文本进行推理。李飞飞说，AI 是一项文明级技术，她很高兴带领团队加入 AMD。在 X 上，AMD 官方账号发布了两人的合影，配文称一图胜千言。

5. A Billion-Dollar Security Bet

摘要：AI 防御安全初创 Corma 在退出隐身约三个月后估值达 10 亿美元，NEA 领投 1.25 亿美元 A 轮，红杉、Khosla、Coatue 跟投，累计融资 1.85 亿美元；其模型专注日志与审计等防御任务；10 月下旬 PHP、Node.js、LiteLLM 连环漏洞披露后需求激增，但未公布经审计营收。

Corma, a startup that trains AI models to defend computer networks, reached a one-billion-dollar valuation about three months after emerging from silence. The company raised a one hundred twenty-five million dollar funding round led by NEA, with earlier backers Sequoia Capital, Khosla Ventures and Coatue joining again. That brings total funding to one hundred eighty-five million dollars. Corma says its models are built for defence work that has nothing to do with writing code, such as reading logs and audits. Interest surged after a day in late October when serious flaws in PHP, Node.js and the tool LiteLLM were disclosed at once. The company, based in Tel Aviv and San Francisco, has not published audited revenue figures.

Corma 是一家训练 AI 模型用于防御计算机网络的初创公司，在退出隐身状态约三个月后，估值达到 10 亿美元。该公司完成了由 NEA 领投的 1.25 亿美元融资，早期投资方红杉资本、Khosla Ventures 和 Coatue 再次参投，累计融资达 1.85 亿美元。Corma 表示，其模型专用于与编写代码无关的防御工作，例如阅读日志和审计。10 月下旬一天之内，PHP、Node.js 和 LiteLLM 工具的严重漏洞集中披露，市场对它的兴趣随之激增。这家公司总部位于特拉维夫和旧金山，尚未公布经审计的营收数据。

6. Fifty-Three Billion in a Chat Window

摘要：PayPal 资金团队用对话式 AI 智能体管理约 530 亿美元公司与客户资金；据德意志银行案例研究，系统取代 90 张表格、覆盖约 1000 个账户，现金可见度从 78% 升至近 100%，支持 160 个货币对、现金定位准确率超 99%，每年节省约 1700 小时。

PayPal's treasury team can now ask a chat window where the company's cash sits, using a conversational agent that covers about fifty-three billion dollars in corporate and customer funds. According to a case study by Deutsche Bank, the system replaced ninety spreadsheets and roughly one thousand accounts, lifting cash visibility from seventy-eight percent to nearly one hundred percent. It handles one hundred sixty currency pairs and reports accuracy above ninety-nine percent in cash positioning, saving around one thousand seven hundred hours a year. The funds include about thirteen point five billion of company cash and thirty-nine point five billion of customer money. PayPal has not said how much of that saved time becomes real cost savings.

PayPal 的资金团队如今可以通过一个聊天窗口询问公司现金的所在，使用的是一个覆盖约 530 亿美元公司与客户资金的对话式智能体。根据德意志银行的一份案例研究，该系统取代了 90 张电子表格和约 1000 个账户，将现金可见度从 78% 提升至接近 100%。它支持 160 个货币对，现金定位准确率超过 99%，每年节省约 1700 小时。这些资金包括约 135 亿美元的公司现金和 395 亿美元的客户资金。PayPal 尚未说明节省的时间中有多少转化为实际成本节约。
