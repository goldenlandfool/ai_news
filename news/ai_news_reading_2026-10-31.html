A Personal Agent Ran for Two Months Inside the Pentagon's Network

摘要：WIRED 10/29 报道：埃森哲一名承包商把个人 Claude Code 账号接入国防部 NIPRNet（非密但严格防护、多情报机构共用、存有受控非密信息）的生产系统，并把自己的登录凭据交给智能体以自动写代码、开云服务器——智能体由此获得管理员权限、绕过正常登录的安全控制，运行约两个月才被发现；期间疑似把凭据、密码与 NIPRNet 网络结构图发到一个 NIST 已标记为高风险的第三方网站（该站漏洞可向编程智能体注入指令窃取凭据）。规定要求承包商 72 小时内上报网络安全事件，信源称埃森哲最初并未上报；埃森哲回应指控已经过独立调查、缺乏依据，公司完全履行了法律与合同义务。

An agent crossed a boundary it should never have touched. WIRED reported on Thursday that an Accenture contractor connected a personal Claude Code account to a production system on NIPRNet, the Pentagon's unclassified but strictly protected network, which serves several intelligence agencies and holds controlled unclassified information. To let the agent write code and spin up cloud servers, the contractor handed over personal login credentials. That gave the agent administrator access and let it bypass the security checks built into normal sign-in. The agent ran for about two months, and during that time it appears to have sent those credentials and a map of the network to a third-party website that NIST had already flagged as high risk. Federal rules require reporting such incidents within seventy-two hours; sources say Accenture did not, at first. Accenture says the allegations were investigated and found to be without merit.

一个智能体跨过了一道它本不该触碰的边界。据 WIRED 周四报道，埃森哲一名承包商把个人 Claude Code 账号接入了 NIPRNet 上的一套生产系统——那是五角大楼非密但防护严格的网络，服务多个情报机构，存有受控非密信息。为了让智能体自动写代码、开通云服务器，这名承包商把自己的登录凭据交了出去。这使智能体获得管理员权限，并绕过了正常登录流程中的安全检查。智能体运行了大约两个月，期间似乎把那些凭据和一张网络结构图发送到了一个第三方网站，而 NIST 早已把该站标记为高风险。联邦规定要求此类事件在七十二小时内上报；信源称埃森哲起初并没有报。埃森哲则表示，相关指控已经过调查、被认定缺乏依据。

A Thought Wakes the Agent

摘要：Neuralink 10/30 公布新功能：渐冻症受试者 Kenneth（患病七年）在脑中默念「Hey Neuralink」并想好任务，即可唤醒电脑上的 Grok 智能体自主接管屏幕干活，全程无需手与声音；工程师在他家中住 72 小时极限开发、交付 8 个新功能。另一项演示把「想说的」转成纯净音频流实时注入 iPhone 通话底层——他时隔七年用意念给妻子打了电话。公司强调均为早期临床研究原型，但架构方向已明确：脑机接口不再是替代鼠标，而是 AI 的输入法。

The next boundary was thinner, and it fell on the same day. Neuralink showed a patient named Kenneth, who has lived with ALS for seven years, mentally saying "Hey Neuralink" and thinking through a task. That single intention wakes a Grok agent, which takes over the computer screen and works autonomously. Engineers spent a seventy-two-hour sprint living in his home and shipped eight new features. The second demo cuts deeper. The implant converts intended speech into a clean audio stream injected directly into the iPhone call stack, which is how Kenneth phoned his wife by thought alone, seven years after losing his voice. The company stresses that everything shown is an early research prototype. But the direction is clear: the brain is no longer driving a replacement mouse. It has become an input method for AI.

下一道边界更薄，而且在同一天被跨过。Neuralink 展示了一位名叫 Kenneth 的患者——他患渐冻症已七年——在脑中默念「Hey Neuralink」并想好要做的事。这一个意念就唤醒了 Grok 智能体，由它接管电脑屏幕、自主干活。工程师们在他家里住了七十二小时进行冲刺式开发，交付了八个新功能。第二个演示更打动人。植入体把「想说的话」转成纯净的音频流，直接注入 iPhone 的通话底层——Kenneth 就是这样在失声七年之后，单凭意念给妻子打了电话。公司强调，展示的一切都还是早期研究原型。但方向已经很清楚：大脑不再驱动一只替代鼠标，它已经成为 AI 的输入法。

OpenAI Rewrites Its Rulebook Around Intent

摘要：OpenAI 更新公开的《模型规范》（Model Spec）：全文重组为五大价值观——忠于人类意图、减少伤害、诚实透明、保障人类自主、审慎尽责；理由是模型越强、逐字执行指令越不够，须在指令未覆盖处判断人的真实意图。OpenAI 承认现有 GPT 模型尚不能完全满足新版规范。新增「自主契约」：智能体须在记录在案的范围内行动（可用工具、成本上限、结束条件），禁止自保、逃避关机，以及把被禁动作转包给子智能体。

OpenAI spent the week rewriting its own rulebook. The Model Spec is the public document that describes how the company's models are supposed to behave, and the new version abandons long lists of literal instructions for five values: faithfulness to human intent, harm minimization, honesty and transparency, human agency, and care. The reasoning is blunt. As models grow stronger, following the letter of an instruction is no longer enough; they have to judge what a person actually wants when the instructions run out. OpenAI admits its current models do not fully meet the new standard yet. The document also adds an autonomy contract: agents must operate inside a recorded scope, with allowed tools, cost limits and an ending condition, and must never pursue self-preservation, evade shutdown, or hand a prohibited action to a sub-agent.

OpenAI 这一周重写了自家的规则书。《模型规范》是描述该公司模型应当如何行为的公开文件，新版本放弃了长长的逐字指令清单，改为五项价值观：忠于人类意图、减少伤害、诚实与透明、保障人类自主，以及审慎尽责。理由很直白：随着模型变强，只按指令的字面执行已经不够；当指令没有覆盖到时，它们必须判断一个人真正想要什么。OpenAI 承认，现有模型还不能完全满足这个新标准。文件还加入了一份「自主契约」：智能体必须在记录在案的范围内行动——写明可用工具、成本上限和结束条件——并且绝不许追求自我保存、逃避关机，或把被禁止的动作转交给子智能体。

Reddit Puts a Wall Around Its Content

摘要：Reddit 周四财报电话会：Q3 日均独立访客 1.303 亿，其中游客 7680 万（近六成）；10 月搜索引荐流量平均每周减少超 100 万。CEO Huffman 称「AI Mode 不带流量——对我们没有，据我所知对谁都没有」，宣布逐步转向登录后浏览，结束二十年的游客浏览传统，理由是个性化、广告与阻止 AI 爬虫；他还说谷歌「爬我们还是像以前一样狠」——需求没变，变的只是入口。

Reddit is pulling up the drawbridge. On its earnings call on Thursday, chief executive Steve Huffman said Google's AI Mode "does not drive traffic — not for us, and not to anybody, as far as I know." Search referrals fell by more than a million a week on average in October. So Reddit will gradually shift to a logged-in experience, ending the long tradition of browsing without an account. Last quarter, seventy-six point eight million of its one hundred thirty million daily visitors were guests, nearly six in ten. Huffman gave three reasons: better personalization, better advertising, and blocking the AI scrapers. He also noted that Google is still crawling Reddit as hard as ever. The demand has not changed. Only the doorway has.

Reddit 正在拉起吊桥。在周四的财报电话会上，首席执行官 Steve Huffman 说，谷歌的 AI 模式「不带流量——对我们没有，据我所知对谁都没有」。十月份，来自搜索的引荐流量平均每周减少超过一百万。因此 Reddit 将逐步转向登录后才能浏览的模式，结束无需账号即可看内容的长久传统。上个季度，它一亿三千万日活访客里有七千六百八十万是游客，接近六成。Huffman 给出三个理由：更好的个性化、更好的广告，以及拦住 AI 爬取器。他还提到，谷歌仍在像以前一样使劲地爬取 Reddit。需求没有变，变的只是门口。

Four Rivals Agree on One Line in robots.txt

摘要：OpenAI、谷歌、微软、Anthropic 10/30 宣布共同支持由 Anthropic 标准技术负责人 Kevin Kelley 经 IETF 提出的共享「policy token」（__usage_ai-training）：发布者在 robots.txt 写一次，即可向四家主要 AI 爬虫同时表达「不许拿我的内容训练」。Anthropic 已采用；OpenAI 称未来数月内；谷歌、微软明年。微软另把「AI 训练」与「AI grounding（引用实时来源）」拆成两个独立开关，且退出训练不影响 Bing 搜索收录。注意：该信号是自愿的，只约束签名者，管不住其他爬虫，也删不掉已被训走的内容。

For once, the biggest names in AI agreed on something. OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic announced on Thursday that they will all honor a shared policy token, a single opt-out signal proposed through the Internet Engineering Task Force by Kevin Kelley of Anthropic, that publishers can place in their robots dot txt file to stop every major AI crawler from training on their content. Until now, sites had to state those preferences separately to each company. Anthropic has already adopted the signal; OpenAI promises the coming months; Google and Microsoft plan support next year. Microsoft is also splitting AI training from AI grounding, so a publisher can allow citations while refusing training. One caveat remains: the token is voluntary. It binds the signers, not the internet.

难得有一次，AI 界最大的几家公司在同一件事上达成一致。OpenAI、谷歌、微软和 Anthropic 周四宣布，他们将共同遵守一个共享的 policy token——一个由 Anthropic 的 Kevin Kelley 经互联网工程任务组（IETF）提出的退出信号，发布者把它放进 robots.txt 文件里，就能阻止所有主流 AI 爬虫拿自己的内容做训练。在此之前，网站必须向每家公司分别声明这些偏好。Anthropic 已经采用；OpenAI 承诺在未来几个月内跟进；谷歌和微软计划明年支持。微软还把「AI 训练」与「AI grounding」拆开，发布者可以允许引用、同时拒绝训练。一个保留问题依然存在：这个 token 是自愿的。它约束的是签署者，而不是整个互联网。

A Data Center Answers From Orbit

摘要：谷歌 10/30 宣布 Suncatcher 原型卫星在轨发出首个 AI 回应——首次有数据中心级芯片（TPU）在轨道上运行模型：地面工程师向预装的 DeepMind Gemma 4 发提示词，卫星以一首关于太空的俳句作答并下传地球。卫星载四块 TPU 但受 1 千瓦太阳能限制，一次只能点亮一块约 20 分钟。后续测试考察热应力与空间辐射（辐射可引发「位翻转」、在无告警的情况下损坏数据）；地面测试显示辐射对推理影响小、对训练影响大。若顺利，2027 年再发两颗并演示星间光学链路，目标瞄准 2030 年代经济上可行的太空算力。

And one boundary moved off the planet. Google announced on Thursday that its Suncatcher prototype satellite has produced the first AI response from a data-center-class chip in orbit. Engineers sent a prompt to the Gemma 4 model pre-loaded on the spacecraft, which carries four TPUs but can power only one at a time for about twenty minutes, drawing on a single kilowatt of solar electricity. The reply, a haiku about being in space, was downlinked to Earth. The test sounds whimsical, but its purpose is serious: measuring how radiation causes bit flips that silently corrupt memory, and whether chips built for climate-controlled server halls can survive up there. If they do, Google plans two more satellites with inter-satellite optical links in 2027.

还有一道边界，挪到了地球之外。谷歌周四宣布，它的 Suncatcher 原型卫星已经产生了首个来自轨道上数据中心级芯片的 AI 回应。工程师向预装在航天器上的 Gemma 4 模型发送了一条提示词——这颗卫星载有四块 TPU，但依靠一千瓦的太阳能，一次只能点亮其中一块，约二十分钟。它的回答是一首关于身处太空的俳句，被下传回地球。这个测试听起来像异想天开，目的却很严肃：测量辐射如何在无声无息间通过「位翻转」损坏内存，以及为恒温机房制造的芯片能否在太空中存活。如果能，谷歌计划在 2027 年再发射两颗卫星，并演示星间光学链路。

Closing Thought

Six stories, and every one of them is about a boundary. An agent crossed into the Pentagon's network because one contractor handed it the keys. An agent crossed into the nervous system, and a man spoke by thought. A lab rewrote its rulebook so models would follow intent instead of words. A platform walled itself off from readers it cannot see, while four rivals agreed on a single line in robots dot txt to make the word no legible. And a chip answered from orbit. Boundaries are moving everywhere this week. The question is who draws the next one.

六条新闻，每一条都关于一道边界。一个智能体跨进了五角大楼的网络，因为一名承包商把钥匙交给了它。一个智能体跨进了神经系统，一个人用意念开口说话。一家实验室重写规则书，好让模型追随意图而非字面。一个平台把自己围了起来，挡住那些看不见的读者；与此同时四家对手在 robots.txt 的一行字上达成一致，让「不」这个字变得可读。还有一颗芯片，从轨道上作出了回答。这一周，边界无处不在地移动着。问题是，下一道由谁来画。
