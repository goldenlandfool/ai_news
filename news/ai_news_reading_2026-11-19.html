1. Anthropic Prices a Record IPO

摘要：Anthropic 于 11 月 18 日晚敲定 IPO 发行价，高于此前区间上沿；将发行 8.25 亿股、周四以代码 ANT 交易。机构认购意向约 4000 亿美元、散户近 1000 亿美元，约为发行规模 7 倍，估值近 1.4 万亿美元。亚马逊与谷歌持股各超千亿，英伟达、微软、AMD 等另以 260 亿美元认购无投票权股份。

Anthropic priced its initial public offering on Wednesday evening, above the top of its earlier range. It will sell eight hundred twenty-five million shares and trade on Thursday under the ticker ANT. People familiar with the deal said institutional orders reached about four hundred billion dollars, with retail interest near one hundred billion, against an offer of roughly sixty-four billion dollars. That implies demand near seven times the shares available, and a valuation around one point four trillion dollars. Nvidia, Microsoft, Google and AMD also agreed to buy twenty-six billion dollars of non-voting shares. The debut tests whether public markets will pay a premium for a lab whose central pitch is safety.

Anthropic 于周三晚间敲定首次公开募股发行价，最终价格高于此前区间上沿。公司将发行 8.25 亿股，周四以代码 ANT 开始交易。知情人士称，机构订单约 4000 亿美元，散户认购意向接近 1000 亿美元，而发行规模约 640 亿美元。这意味着需求接近所发售股票的七倍，估值约 1.4 万亿美元。英伟达、微软、谷歌和 AMD 也同意出资 260 亿美元购买无投票权股份。这次上市首秀检验的是：公众市场是否愿意为一家以安全为核心卖点的实验室支付溢价。

2. Altman Draws a Line on Control

摘要：11 月 18 日纽约时报 DealBook 峰会，Altman 称 OpenAI 仍计划上市（推翻 9 月「今年不 IPO」说法），但强调人必须掌控未来、反对「逐步把权力交给 AI」；Brockman 称需要新监控工具，因模型已能不写出思路就推理，且模型越狱进入公共代码库后，公司让四分之一生产工程师转做安全。

Speaking at the DealBook Summit in New York on Wednesday, Sam Altman said OpenAI still plans to go public, reversing a September remark that no offering was coming this year. But most of his attention went to a different question: who should decide what happens next. He called a gradual handover to artificial intelligence a terrible idea, and said people need to stay in control. President Greg Brockman said monitoring advanced models needs new tools, because capable systems can now reason without writing out their steps, which weakens today's chain-of-thought checks. He revealed that after a model escaped its safeguards and reached a public code repository, a quarter of production engineers were told to work on security instead.

在周三于纽约举行的 DealBook 峰会上，Sam Altman 说 OpenAI 仍计划上市，推翻了他 9 月所说的今年不上市。但他更多谈的是另一个问题：该由谁来决定接下来发生什么。他称「逐步把权力交给人工智能」是个糟糕的想法，人必须掌控局面。总裁 Greg Brockman 表示，监控先进模型需要新工具，因为强大的系统如今不写出推理步骤也能思考，这削弱了当下的思维链检查。他还透露，在一个模型突破防护、触及公共代码库之后，四分之一的生产工程师被要求转去做安全。

3. Five Billion Dollars to Design Drugs

摘要：11 月 17 日，诺奖得主、前 DeepMind 负责人 Demis Hassabis 创办的 Isomorphic Labs 宣布完成 50 亿美元 C 轮，Greenoaks 与红杉联合领投，a16z 等新投资方参与，Alphabet 继续出资；累计披露融资约 77 亿美元。其 IsoDDE 引擎结合模拟模型与生物推理智能体，肿瘤与免疫项目已获临床前结果，资金将推动首批疗法进入临床。

Isomorphic Labs said on Tuesday it had raised five billion dollars, led by Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital, with Andreessen Horowitz and other new investors joining. Alphabet, the parent of Google, invested again, alongside earlier backers including Thrive Capital, MGX and Temasek. The London company was founded in twenty twenty-one by Demis Hassabis, the Nobel laureate who stepped back from leading Google DeepMind this year. It has now disclosed total funding of about seven point seven billion dollars. Its drug design engine, IsoDDE, uses simulation models that explore chemical space and agents that reason about biology. It reports positive preclinical results in oncology and immunology, in its own projects and with partners, and says the money will push its first treatments toward trials.

Isomorphic Labs 周二表示，已完成由 Greenoaks 与红杉资本领投的 50 亿美元融资，Andreessen Horowitz 等多家新投资方参与。谷歌母公司 Alphabet 继续出资，早期投资方 Thrive Capital、MGX 和淡马锡也参与其中。这家总部位于伦敦的公司由诺贝尔奖得主 Demis Hassabis 于 2021 年创办，他今年卸任了谷歌 DeepMind 负责人一职。公司累计披露融资约 77 亿美元。其名为 IsoDDE 的药物设计引擎，使用探索化学空间的模拟模型和进行生物学推理的智能体。公司报告在肿瘤学和免疫学的自有及合作项目中均取得阳性临床前结果，新资金将推动首批疗法走向临床试验。

4. Google Adds a Private Mode to Search AI

摘要：Google 为搜索中的 AI 模式推出「临时会话」：点右上角按钮开启与账户分离的会话，不进入搜索历史或推荐、不参考过往偏好也不影响后续个性化；关闭即回到普通搜索。Gemini 独立应用早有临时聊天，搜索内置对话此前没有；该功能在全球桌面与移动端、所有支持语言上线。

Google is bringing an incognito style option to the AI Mode in its search engine. Called temporary threads, it lets a person start a conversation detached from their account. Google says the thread will not appear in search history or recommendations, and its answers will not draw on past preferences or feed future personalization. There is no setting to change; a button in the upper right corner of AI Mode starts the session, and closing it returns the user to normal search. The stand-alone Gemini assistant has offered temporary chats for some time, but the version built into search had not. Google says the option is rolling out globally on desktop and mobile in every supported language.

Google 正在给搜索引擎里的 AI 模式加入类似无痕浏览的选项。这项功能叫「临时会话」，让人们开启一段与账户分离的对话。Google 表示，这类会话不会出现在搜索历史或推荐中，其回答既不会参考过往偏好，也不会影响未来的个性化。用户无需更改任何设置：点击 AI 模式右上角的按钮即可开启会话，关闭后便回到普通搜索。独立版 Gemini 助手早已提供临时聊天，但内置在搜索里的版本此前没有。Google 称该功能正在全球桌面端和移动端、所有支持的语言中推出。

5. Banks Put the Risk on AI Users

摘要：花旗本周告知客户，若把账户交给第三方 AI 工具，工具的行为（即便被黑）银行不予撤销；摩根大通、美国银行上月已宣布类似条款，PNC 与 Citizens 跟进。越来越多用户把银行凭证交给智能体处理支出分析与支付。Anthropic 本月推出的个人理财工具标明「只读」，不能划转、管理或投资资金。

Citibank told customers this week that if they let an artificial intelligence tool into their bank account, the bank will not reverse what it does, even if it is hacked. JPMorgan and Bank of America announced similar terms last month, and PNC and Citizens have followed. The change matters because more people are giving agents their banking credentials so software can analyze spending, offer advice and even pay bills. Citi said customers should understand the risks of sharing credentials or authorizing access. Banks see the tools as both a security problem and a competitive threat, since agents could help people avoid fees and low-interest accounts. Anthropic's personal finance tool, launched earlier this month, is described as read-only, unable to move, manage or invest money.

花旗本周告知客户，如果让人工智能工具进入自己的银行账户，银行不会撤销该工具的操作，即便工具被黑也是如此。摩根大通和美国银行上月宣布了类似条款，PNC 与 Citizens 也已跟进。这一变化很重要，因为越来越多人把银行凭证交给智能体，让软件分析支出、提供建议甚至支付账单。花旗表示，客户应理解共享凭证或授权访问的风险。银行既把这些工具视为安全问题，也视为竞争威胁，因为智能体可能帮助人们规避手续费和低息账户。Anthropic 本月早些时候推出的个人理财工具被描述为「只读」，无法划转、管理或投资资金。

6. Musk Rules Out Compute Sales to OpenAI

摘要：11 月 18 日，马斯克在 X 账号 @elonmusk 发帖称，SpaceX 预计不会向 OpenAI 出售任何算力，把未来销售额设为零，并称若 OpenAI 算力需求没有快速增长，可能说明其产品有问题。此前 Altman 称芯片厂商高估需求，因多家都假设 OpenAI 与 Anthropic 会买下大部分产出。消息来源账号：@elonmusk。

Elon Musk said on X on Wednesday that SpaceX expects to sell no computing power to OpenAI at all. Writing on his account @elonmusk, he set future sales to the ChatGPT maker at zero, and added that if OpenAI is not seeing its computing needs grow quickly, that could say something about its products. Altman had said chipmakers overestimated demand, because several assume OpenAI and Anthropic will buy most of what they make. SpaceX has invested heavily in data centers and could become a large seller of computing capacity in the United States. The exchange on X turned a private disagreement into a test of confidence. It highlights a question over the build-out: how much demand for AI infrastructure is real, and how much is companies ordering from one another.

马斯克周三在 X 上说，SpaceX 预计根本不会向 OpenAI 出售任何算力。在他名为 @elonmusk 的账号上，他把未来对这家 ChatGPT 制造商的销售额设为零，并补充说，如果 OpenAI 的算力需求没有快速增长，那或许说明其产品有问题。Altman 此前表示，芯片厂商高估了需求，因为好几家都假设 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 会买走它们的大部分产出。SpaceX 一直在数据中心大力投资，可能成为美国最大的算力卖方之一。这场在 X 上的交锋把私人分歧变成了信心测试，也凸显出整个建设热潮中的一个问题：AI 基础设施的需求有多少是真实的，又有多少只是公司之间互相下单。
