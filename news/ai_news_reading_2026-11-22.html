1. Signal Draws a Line at the Screen

摘要：Signal 宣布 iPhone 默认屏蔽截图，Android 检测到 AI 助手读屏时也屏蔽；总裁 Meredith Whittaker 称系统级助手对隐私构成「生存威胁」，并援引 Meta Muse 内部测试读取 Signal 消息的报道。用户可关闭保护但会更不安全；Mac 端因缺少工具无法提供同等防护。

Signal said it will block screenshots by default on iPhones, and on Android when it detects an AI assistant trying to capture the screen. The encrypted messaging app says assistants built deep into an operating system, such as Apple's Siri and Google's Gemini, can pull a private conversation off the display and send it to a remote server. President Meredith Whittaker called the reach of these agents an existential threat to the privacy the app promises. Signal points to reports that Meta's Muse agent read Signal messages in internal testing. Users can switch the protection off, but the company warns that may expose their chats and their contacts' messages. On Mac, Signal says it lacks the tools to offer the same guard, and it wants Apple, Google and Microsoft to give developers a system-level way to keep agents out of sensitive apps.

Signal 表示，将默认阻止 iPhone 上的截图，在 Android 上检测到 AI 助手试图捕获屏幕时也会阻止。这款加密通讯应用称，深度嵌入操作系统的助手，如苹果的 Siri 和谷歌的 Gemini，可以把私人对话从屏幕上读出并发送到远程服务器。总裁 Meredith Whittaker 称这些助手的触及范围是对该应用所承诺隐私的「生存威胁」。Signal 援引报道称，Meta 的 Muse 智能体在内部测试中读取过 Signal 消息。用户可以关闭这一保护，但公司警告说，这可能暴露自己的聊天以及联系人的消息。在 Mac 上，Signal 说它缺少提供同样保护的工具，并希望苹果、谷歌和微软为开发者提供系统级方法，让智能体远离敏感应用。

2. A Loop Model That Fits on a Laptop

摘要：前 OpenAI 研究主管 Mira Murati 创立的 Thinking Machines Lab 以 Apache 许可开源 Inkling 2 Mini：260 亿参数、每词元激活 34 亿、约 20GB 内存可在笔记本运行；「循环」设计让中间层每词元跑三次以延长推理。官方基准领先同级开放模型，独立排名较温和。

Thinking Machines Lab, founded by former OpenAI research chief Mira Murati, released Inkling 2 Mini with open weights under an Apache license. The model has twenty-six billion parameters but activates only three point four billion per token, and it needs about twenty gigabytes of memory, small enough for a laptop. Its unusual design is a loop: the middle layers run three times for each token, letting the model reason longer without writing that reasoning down. The lab says it helps most on coding and agent tasks. In a demo, a researcher ran it offline on a flight and had it build a playable flight simulator in minutes. Its own benchmarks lead among open models of this size, though independent rankings are more modest.

Thinking Machines Lab——由前 OpenAI 研究主管 Mira Murati 创立的公司——以 Apache 许可发布了开放权重的 Inkling 2 Mini。该模型有 260 亿个参数，但每个词元只激活 34 亿个，且只需约 20GB 内存，小到可以在笔记本上运行。它不同寻常的设计是「循环」：中间层对每个词元运行三次，让模型能思考更久，而不必把推理写出来。实验室说这对编码和智能体任务帮助最大。在一次演示中，一位研究员在飞机上离线运行它，几分钟内就让它构建出一个可玩的飞行模拟器。实验室自己的基准测试在这个规模的开放模型中领先，不过独立排名较为温和。

3. OpenAI Puts a Price on Silence

摘要：OpenAI 在 API 上线全双工语音模型 GPT-Live-1.1，可边听边说，由开发者账号在 X 上宣布。官方称转写更准、口音更稳定，但未公布基准分数；最实质的变化是新增待机模式，把静默时段的费用从每分钟 5 美分降到 2 美分，降幅 60%。

OpenAI released GPT-Live-1.1 in its API, a full-duplex voice model that listens while it speaks. The company's developer account announced it on X. OpenAI says the update transcribes speech more accurately, keeps a steadier accent across languages and handles letters and numbers better, but it published no benchmark scores. The clearest change is the price of silence. A new standby mode pauses inference when a call goes quiet while keeping the connection open, cutting idle time from five cents a minute to two cents, a sixty percent drop. Voice agents spend much of their day waiting, so cheaper waiting may matter more than a smoother voice. Backend reasoning and tools are billed separately.

OpenAI 在其 API 中发布了 GPT-Live-1.1，这是一个能边听边说的全双工语音模型。公司的开发者账号在 X 上宣布了这一消息。OpenAI 说，这次更新让语音转写更准确，跨语言的口音更稳定，字母和数字处理得更好，但没有公布基准分数。最清晰的变化是静默的价格。新的待机模式会在通话安静时暂停推理，同时保持连接不中断，把空闲时间从每分钟五美分降到两美分，降幅达 60%。语音智能体一天中大部分时间都在等待，因此更便宜的等待可能比更动听的声音更重要。后端推理和工具单独计费。

4. Stripe Measures a Demo Against Real Payments

摘要：Stripe 用 69 个真实支付、订阅与市场场景测试 14 种模型与编码智能体组合，平均分 78%，但要求所有必要条件都通过时降到 29%；配上自家插件后 14 种配置全部改善。结论：通用编码分数掩盖差距，特定领域检查更重要。这是 Stripe 自有基准。

Stripe tested fourteen combinations of models and coding agents on sixty-nine real scenarios drawn from payments, subscriptions and marketplaces. The average score was seventy-eight percent. But when Stripe required every necessary criterion to pass, the average fell to twenty-nine percent. All fourteen setups improved when paired with Stripe's own agent plugin. The results come from Stripe's own benchmark, not an outside test, so they should be read with that in mind. The lesson is practical: code that completes a checkout can still miss a failed payment or a subscription change. A generic coding score hides those gaps. For teams shipping agents into production, domain-specific checks turn out to matter more than a headline number.

Stripe 用来自支付、订阅和市场场景的 69 个真实情境，测试了 14 种模型与编码智能体的组合。平均分是 78%。但当 Stripe 要求每一个必要条件都通过时，平均分降到了 29%。14 种配置在配上 Stripe 自家的智能体插件后都有改善。结果来自 Stripe 自己的基准测试，而非外部测试，因此阅读时应记住这一点。教训很实际：能完成结账的代码，仍可能漏掉一笔失败的支付或一次订阅变更。通用的编码分数掩盖了这些差距。对把智能体投入生产环境的团队来说，针对特定领域的检查，比一个亮眼的分数更重要。

5. Google Loads Tools Only When Needed

摘要：Google 在 Gemini Interactions API 中预览「工具检索」：工具名称与简短描述常驻，详细模式只在智能体需要时加载，避免庞大工具库占满提示；支持 Gemini 3.6 及以上与远程 MCP 服务器。官方未公布节省数字，团队需自行衡量。

Google opened a preview of a feature called tool retrieval in its Gemini Interactions API. It keeps the names and short descriptions of tools available at all times, but loads the detailed schema only when an agent needs a specific tool. Large tool libraries can otherwise fill much of a prompt with instructions an agent never uses. The preview supports Gemini 3.6 and later models, including the Gemini 4 family, and works with remote MCP servers. Google notes that standard token charges still apply and published no savings figure, so teams must measure the benefit themselves. It is a quiet fix for a real cost: as agents gain tools, the instructions describing them compete with the work itself.

Google 在其 Gemini Interactions API 中开启了一项名为工具检索的功能预览。做法是让工具的名称和简短描述始终可用，但只在智能体确实需要某个具体工具时，才加载详细的模式定义。否则，庞大的工具库可能会用智能体从不使用的指令占满提示的大部分空间。该预览支持 Gemini 3.6 及更高版本，包括 Gemini 4 系列，并可用于远程 MCP 服务器。Google 指出，标准的词元费用仍然照收，也没有公布节省数字，因此团队必须自行衡量收益。这是对一项真实成本的安静修复：随着智能体获得更多工具，描述这些工具的指令会与工作本身争夺空间。

6. A Cooler Second Day for Anthropic

摘要：Anthropic 股价在上市第二个交易日大幅下跌，此前首日曾短暂跻身全球最有价值公司之列；IPO 定价 83 美元、开盘远高于此，公司募资近 1050 亿美元，七位创始人各套现约 2.8 亿美元。交易员在 X 上以代码 ANT 发布波动图表。

Anthropic's shares fell sharply on their second day of trading, after a debut that had briefly put the company among the most valuable in the world. The stock had priced at eighty-three dollars in the initial public offering and opened well above that. Reports differed on the exact drop, but the fall was steep enough to drag other AI-related shares lower. The company raised nearly one hundred five billion dollars, and its seven founders each cashed out around two hundred eighty million. Traders on X posted charts of the swing under the ticker ANT. A rough second day does not erase a record debut, but it punctures the idea that the market will keep rewarding ever larger numbers without question.

Anthropic 的股价在上市第二个交易日大幅下跌，此前首日曾一度让该公司跻身全球最有价值公司之列。该股在首次公开募股中定价为 83 美元，开盘价远高于此。各家报道对具体跌幅说法不一，但跌幅足以拖累其他 AI 相关股票走低。公司募资近 1050 亿美元，七位创始人每人套现约 2.8 亿美元。交易员们在 X 上以股票代码 ANT 发布了股价波动的图表。坎坷的第二天并不会抹去创纪录的首次亮相，但它戳破了一种想法：市场会不加质疑地持续奖励越来越大的数字。
