1. Anthropic Files for an IPO That Could Be the Largest in History

摘要：Anthropic 周一正式向 SEC 递交 S-1 上市申请，拟以代码 AN 登陆纳斯达克：上半年营收 163 亿美元（去年同期 12 亿）、经调整经营利润 16 亿美元、经营现金流首次转正；807 亿美元净亏损中 724 亿是可转债估值的会计费用；市场预期估值 1.5–2 万亿美元。

Anthropic is going public. On Monday, the company filed its S-1 registration with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, the formal start of what could be the largest stock market debut in history. It plans to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, with a first day of trading expected around Thanksgiving week. The numbers run to extremes in both directions. Revenue reached 16.3 billion dollars in the first half of 2026, up from about 1.2 billion a year earlier. The net loss was 80.7 billion, but 72.4 billion of that is an accounting charge from the rising value of convertible notes, not spent cash. On the company's adjusted measure it earned a 1.6 billion operating profit, and operating cash flow turned positive for the first time. Investors are discussing a valuation between 1.5 and 2 trillion dollars, which would top SpaceX's record. The prospectus promises an age of miracles on one page, then lists existential risks a few pages later.

Anthropic 要上市了。周一，公司向美国证券交易委员会递交了 S-1 注册文件，这可能是史上最大规模股票上市交易的正式起点。它计划以代码 ANT 登陆纳斯达克，首个交易日预计在感恩节前后。文件里的数字向两个极端延伸。2026 年上半年营收达 163 亿美元，是一年前约 12 亿美元的十几倍。净亏损 807 亿美元，但其中 724 亿来自可转换票据升值带来的会计费用，并不是花出去的现金。按公司自己的调整口径，它赚了 16 亿美元经营利润，经营现金流也首次转正。投资者在讨论 1.5 万亿到 2 万亿美元的估值，这将超过 SpaceX 的纪录。招股书在一页里承诺「奇迹时代」，几页之后又列出了存在性风险。

2. SpaceX Locks In 40 Billion Dollars to Lease GPUs from Apollo

摘要：SpaceX 披露与 Apollo 旗下实体签署约 400 亿美元主融资租赁的约束性承诺，用于购置英伟达下一代 Vera Rubin 芯片，号称史上最大 GPU 融资；高盛独家设计结构，贝莱德与 KKR 参与锚定，年底前交割、2027 上半年开始交付。

The money behind the models is getting creative. On Monday, SpaceX disclosed a binding commitment for a master finance lease of about forty billion dollars with an entity managed by Apollo Global Management, to fund NVIDIA's next-generation Vera Rubin chips. The company calls it the largest committed GPU financing ever. Goldman Sachs structured the deal, and funds managed by BlackRock and KKR made anchor commitments. The agreement should close before the end of the year, with chips arriving in the first half of 2027 on five-year leases paid quarterly in advance. Instead of borrowing plain cash, SpaceX is essentially renting the chips themselves. And the company's finance chief says demand is strong enough that payback periods on its intelligence infrastructure run under one year. A rocket company has quietly become one of the biggest names in AI financing.

模型背后的钱，玩法越来越有创意。周一，SpaceX 披露了一项约束性承诺：与 Apollo 全球管理旗下的一家实体签订约 400 亿美元的主融资租赁，用于购置英伟达下一代 Vera Rubin 芯片。公司称这是有史以来承诺金额最大的 GPU 融资。高盛担任结构设计方，贝莱德和 KKR 管理的基金作出了锚定承诺。协议预计年底前完成交割，芯片 2027 年上半年到货，租期约五年、按季度预付。SpaceX 不是借普通现金，而本质上是把芯片本身租进来。公司财务负责人说，需求强劲到其智能基础设施投资的回本周期不到一年。一家火箭公司，已经悄悄成为 AI 融资界最大的名字之一。

3. Microsoft Offers Voluntary Exits to 60,000 Employees as AI Bills Pile Up

摘要：微软推出「自愿离职方案」，约 6 万名员工（占全球 22.3 万人的四分之一以上）符合资格，覆盖美加英印、70 级及以下，12 月前决定、年底离职；这是微软年内第三轮人员调整，背景是今年 1750 亿美元资本开支，周三将发布财报。

Microsoft showed the other side of the same bill. On Monday the company introduced a Voluntary Exit Offer for about sixty thousand employees, more than a quarter of its global workforce of two hundred twenty-three thousand. Eligible staff in the United States, Canada, India and the United Kingdom, at level seventy and below, can leave with financial support if they decide by early December. Teams around Copilot, Windows, Xbox and finance are included; parts of sales, marketing and security are not. It is the company's third workforce adjustment this year, after a retirement program in April and forty-eight hundred layoffs in July. Microsoft has forecast one hundred seventy-five billion dollars in capital spending this year, mostly for AI data centers, and reports earnings on Wednesday. The payroll is one place where that bill gets paid.

微软展示了同一张账单的另一面。周一，公司推出「自愿离职方案」，约 6 万名员工符合资格，超过其全球 22.3 万员工总数的四分之一。美国、加拿大、印度和英国、职级 70 及以下的员工，只要在 12 月初前做出决定，就可以带着财务支持离开。Copilot、Windows、Xbox 和财务等团队在范围内；部分销售、营销与安全团队不在。这是微软年内第三轮人员调整，此前有 4 月的退休计划和 7 月的 4800 人裁员。微软预测今年资本支出 1750 亿美元，主要投向 AI 数据中心，公司周三发布财报。而工资单，正是这笔账单被支付的地方之一。

4. OpenAI Cuts the Price of Computer-Using AI in Half

摘要：OpenAI 发布 GPT-6.1 Terra：API 价格 $1/$5 每百万 token，是前代与 Sol 的一半；官方称计算机使用能力持平旗舰 Astra、单价仅十分之一、任务快 3 倍以上；事实错误率从 53.6% 降到 16.7%（最大推理力度 4.3%）。

OpenAI cut prices on Monday. The new model, GPT-6.1 Terra, is built for computer work: reading documents, filling forms, and operating software on screen. Its standard API price is one dollar per million input tokens and five per million output tokens, half the cost of the previous Terra and of GPT-6.1 Sol. OpenAI says it matches its flagship GPT-6 Astra on computer-use tasks at one tenth of the price per token, and finishes those tasks more than three times faster. Coding improved too: the model now solves just over half of Terminal-Bench 4.0, double its predecessor's score. Most striking is the error rate: on OpenAI's internal set of trick prompts, factual errors fell from about fifty-four percent to under seventeen, and to four percent at maximum effort. Terra sits below Astra and Sol. The pattern is clear: the same capability keeps getting cheaper every month.

OpenAI 在周一砍了价格。新模型 GPT-6.1 Terra 为计算机上的工作而生：读文档、填表格、操作屏幕上的软件。它的标准 API 价格是每百万输入 token 1 美元、输出 5 美元，是上一代 Terra 和 GPT-6.1 Sol 的一半。OpenAI 称，在计算机使用任务上它持平旗舰 GPT-6 Astra，而单价只有后者的十分之一，完成这些任务还快 3 倍以上。编码也提升了：它现在能解出 Terminal-Bench 4.0 刚过半的任务，是前代的两倍。最显眼的是错误率：在 OpenAI 内部一组刁钻提示词上，事实错误从约 54% 降到 17% 以下，最大推理力度时降到 4%。Terra 位于 Astra 和 Sol 之下。规律如今很清楚：同样的能力，每个月都在变得更便宜。

5. Anthropic Hands Its Agent Skills Standard to a Neutral Foundation

摘要：Anthropic 把 Agent Skills 开放标准捐给 Linux 基金会旗下的 Agentic AI Foundation：该标准已被 70 多个平台（含 ChatGPT、Cursor、Copilot）采用、约 100 万个技能、安装 2.8 亿次；这是继 MCP 之后 Anthropic 捐出的第二个标准，同期 OpenAI、Hugging Face、Alibaba 也各捐一个项目。

One quieter story may matter for years. Anthropic has donated its Agent Skills standard to the Agentic AI Foundation, the neutral body under the Linux Foundation that it co-founded with Block and OpenAI last December. A skill is a folder with a SKILL.md file and some scripts that teaches an agent one task. More than seventy platforms have adopted the format, including ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot and VS Code, and about one million skills have been installed roughly two hundred eighty million times. Anthropic handed over the name, logo, and GitHub organization, so no single company now controls the direction. It is the second standard Anthropic has given away, after the Model Context Protocol. In the same round, OpenAI, Hugging Face and Alibaba each contributed a project, bringing the young foundation to ten. Even competitors are learning to share the plumbing.

一条更安静的新闻，也许会影响很多年。Anthropic 把它的 Agent Skills 开放标准捐给了 Agentic AI Foundation——去年 12 月它和 Block、OpenAI 共同在 Linux 基金会下创立的中立机构。所谓技能，就是一个包含 SKILL.md 文件和配套脚本的文件夹，用来教会智能体做好一件事。已有 70 多个平台采用这一格式，包括 ChatGPT、Cursor、GitHub Copilot 和 VS Code，约 100 万个技能被安装了约 2.8 亿次。Anthropic 移交了名称、标识和 GitHub 组织，今后没有任何一家公司能单独控制它的方向。这是 Anthropic 继 MCP 之后捐出的第二个标准。在同一轮捐赠里，OpenAI、Hugging Face 和阿里巴巴各贡献了一个项目，把这家年轻基金会托管的项目带到了十个。连竞争者也在学着共享管道。

6. xAI Releases the First Seven Minutes of a Fully AI-Generated Film

摘要：马斯克旗下 xAI 放出全 AI 生成电影《奥德赛》的前七分钟，无实拍镜头；早期评论认可技术完成度，但认为它「缺乏艺术性、灵感和人的温度」——一年前还不可能有连续七分钟的 AI 电影，能否撑起 90 分钟仍是未知数。

And a glimpse of AI entertainment. xAI, Elon Musk's company, released the first seven minutes of Odyssey, a film generated entirely by its Grok models, with no cameras and no human footage. Early reviewers credited its technical polish, and noted what is missing: as one write-up put it, the film lacks artistry, inspiration, or human interest. That criticism may date quickly. A year ago, a continuous seven-minute AI film would have been impossible, and this short already shows where generative video is heading. Whether audiences will sit through ninety minutes is the open question, and for now, the honest answer is that nobody knows.

最后看一眼 AI 娱乐。马斯克旗下的 xAI 放出了《奥德赛》的前七分钟——一部完全由 Grok 模型生成的电影，没有摄像机，也没有任何实拍镜头。早期的评论者认可它的技术完成度，同时点出了缺失的东西：正如一篇评论所说，这部电影「缺乏艺术性、灵感，或人的温度」。这种批评可能很快就会过时。一年前，连续七分钟的 AI 电影还不可能存在，而这部短片已经展示了生成式视频正在走向哪里。观众能不能坐着看完九十分钟，才是真正悬而未决的问题；就目前而言，诚实的答案是：没人知道。

Closing Thought

Almost every story today is about money. A lab asked the public to fund its future. A rocket company committed forty billion dollars against chips it has not yet received. A software giant offered sixty thousand people the door, to afford the computers behind the door. That same week, the price of using the models fell in half. This industry is no longer only a research story; it is a capital story, and capital, unlike research, keeps score in public.

今天几乎每一条新闻都和钱有关。一家实验室请公众为它的未来买单。一家火箭公司为还没收到的芯片承诺了 400 亿美元。一家软件巨头给 6 万人递上了门，为了养得起门后那些计算机。同一周里，使用模型的价格降了一半。这个行业已经不只是一个研究故事，而变成了一个资本故事——而资本和研究不同，它是在公开场合计分的。

朗读建议（Reading Tips）

  • 全文用中速朗读（约 110~120 词/分钟）；「朗读」按钮的音频按新闻播报语速（约 150 词/分钟）生成，可先跟读再脱稿。
  • 第 1 篇的金额与数字要读清楚并稍作停顿："16.3 billion dollars"、"80.7 billion"、"1.5 and 2 trillion dollars"
  • 第 1 篇结尾 "an age of miracles on one page, then lists existential risks a few pages later" 是点睛句，两个分句间停顿一拍
  • 第 2 篇 "the largest committed GPU financing ever" 重读 largest，这是本条新闻的核心定性
  • 第 3 篇 "more than a quarter of its global workforce" 稍放慢，让比例的含义落下来
  • 第 4 篇的百分比要清楚："about fifty-four percent to under seventeen"、"four percent at maximum effort"
  • 第 5 篇 "Even competitors are learning to share the plumbing" 语速放慢、句末降调，这是本篇的结论
  • 第 6 篇引语 "lacks artistry, inspiration, or human interest" 三个并列词逐个咬清，节奏均匀
  • 结尾 "capital, unlike research, keeps score in public" 是全篇落点，放慢并降调，留一点空白再结束
