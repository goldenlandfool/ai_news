Two Labs Open Election War Rooms

摘要：11 月 3 日美国中期选举投票，多家媒体称这是「美国史上首场有相当数量选民使用 AI 的选举」。OpenAI 在投票前一周启动「选举运营中心」，持续运行到所有选票统计完毕，并对投票站排队等敏感图像请求返回卡通化结果而非逼真照片；Anthropic 首次设立「作战室」，由发言人对 Semafor 确认团队全年强化选举防护、投票前夕专职监控滥用并准备快速响应。布伦南司法中心 10/22 报告显示，只有 OpenAI 明显收紧了防护，Gemini 与 Meta AI 反而更容易生成逼真的虚假选举图像；另有研究测得聊天机器人在基础投票事实上的错误率最高达 27%。

Americans voted on Tuesday, and for the first time two AI labs ran election war rooms. OpenAI started an election operations centre a week before polling day and will keep it running until every ballot is counted. Its models now apply a cartoon filter: ask for a photograph of a long line at a polling station and you get a drawing instead. Anthropic set up its own war room, a first for the company, to watch for abuse and respond quickly. The record so far is uneven. The Brennan Center reported in October that only OpenAI had clearly tightened its defences, while Gemini and Meta AI had become easier to use for realistic fake election images. Researchers also found chatbots wrong on basic voting facts in up to twenty-seven percent of test queries.

美国人周二投了票，而两家 AI 实验室第一次开设了选举作战室。OpenAI 在投票日前一周启动「选举运营中心」，并将一直运行到所有选票统计完毕。它的模型现在会施加一层卡通化处理：如果你要一张投票站排长队的照片，得到的是一张画。Anthropic 也设立了自己的作战室——对公司而言是第一次——用来监视滥用并快速响应。到目前为止，记录并不整齐。布伦南司法中心十月的报告指出，只有 OpenAI 明显收紧了防护，而 Gemini 与 Meta AI 反而变得更容易被用来生成逼真的虚假选举图像。研究人员还发现，聊天机器人在基础投票事实上的出错率最高达到测试查询的百分之二十七。

A Prospectus With Two Stories Inside

摘要：Anthropic 11/2 向 SEC 补交 S-1/A，填上此前留白的三季度数据：营收 169.56 亿美元、同比约 12 倍；但营业利润从二季度盈利 9.74 亿美元转为亏损 5.67 亿美元，净亏损 233 亿美元中约 219 亿来自可转债与 SAFE 的公允价值重估（非现金）。算力与基础设施费用 98.63 亿美元、占营收 58%（二季度 48%），其中约 50 亿美元是按现货高价紧急补购的算力，另有 20 亿美元捐赠配捐非现金费用；调整后营业利润 25 亿美元、利润率 15%。真正压秤的是附注：采购与租赁承诺合计约 8954 亿美元，几乎全为算力，约 698 亿在一年内到期、4475 亿在 2030 年底前发生。机构投资者据报把 IPO 估值指引压到 1.5–1.6 万亿美元，低于此前近 2 万亿的传闻。

Anthropic filed the missing pages of its prospectus on Monday, and the numbers read like two different companies. Third-quarter revenue reached almost seventeen billion dollars, about twelve times the year before. But the quarter swung back to an operating loss of five hundred sixty-seven million dollars, after a rare profit in the spring. Compute and infrastructure cost nearly nine point nine billion, fifty-eight percent of revenue, up from forty-eight. Management blames five billion of emergency capacity bought at short notice. The part investors will study longest is the fine print: eight hundred ninety-five billion dollars of purchase and lease commitments, almost all of it for computing, with seventy billion due within a year. Institutions are now said to be guiding the listing toward one and a half trillion, well below the two trillion that was being whispered.

Anthropic 周一补上了招股书里缺的那几页，而数字读起来像两家不同的公司。三季度营收达到近一百七十亿美元，约为去年同期的十二倍。但这个季度又跌回五亿六千七百万美元的营业亏损——春季才刚出现过一次罕见的盈利。算力与基础设施花了近九十九亿美元，占营收的百分之五十八，高于二季度的百分之四十八。管理层把原因归为五十亿美元临时高价买入的紧急产能。投资者会研究最久的，是那份附注：八千九百五十亿美元的采购与租赁承诺，几乎全部用于算力，其中七百亿在一年内到期。据说机构投资者正把上市估值引向一点五万亿美元，远低于此前私下流传的两万亿。

A Building With a New Name

摘要：OpenAI 11/2 发布「电网守护者计划」（Grid Protectors Program），首个试点是佐治亚州埃芬厄姆县在建园区：极端天气或紧急状况下最多让出 1000 兆瓦负荷，约相当于 70 万户家庭的空调用电；与 Georgia Power 的需求响应安排上限约 1000 兆瓦，并承诺全额承担相关基建与供电成本。公司同时把自己设施改称「AI 算力园区」，试图摆脱高耗能形象。但据 Axios/Semafor，这轮改名在国会等圈子里反响冷淡；背景是约七成美国人反对在本地建数据中心，田纳西、佛罗里达、俄克拉荷马、俄勒冈等州已立法禁止把电力成本转嫁给居民。

OpenAI spent the same week trying to change what a data centre is called. On Monday it announced the Grid Protectors Program, starting at a new campus in Effingham County, Georgia, which has promised to free up as much as one thousand megawatts during extreme weather, roughly the air conditioning of seven hundred thousand homes. The company says it will pay the full cost of the infrastructure and the power. It has also begun calling its facilities AI compute campuses, a phrase meant to sound less like a smokestack. That relabelling has landed badly in some quarters, including among lawmakers, and it does not settle the underlying argument. Several states have already banned passing data centre power costs on to households, and polling suggests most Americans oppose a project near them.

OpenAI 在同一周里试图改变「数据中心」这个称呼。周一它宣布「电网守护者计划」，首个落点是佐治亚州埃芬厄姆县的一座新园区：该园区承诺在极端天气时最多让出一千兆瓦负荷，大致相当于七十万户家庭的空调用电。公司表示将全额支付相关基础设施与供电成本。它也开始把自家设施称为「AI 算力园区」，这个说法听起来不那么像一根烟囱。这次改名在包括国会议员在内的某些圈子里反响不佳，而且并没有解决底下的争论。已有多个州立法禁止把数据中心的电力成本转嫁给家庭，民调也显示多数美国人反对在自家附近建这类项目。

Google Lets You Bring a Rival Agent

摘要：谷歌 11/4 在 Android Dev Summit 发布 Android Studio Rabbit 2，「自带智能体」（BYOA）正式稳定：支持 40 多款基于开放式智能体客户端协议的编码智能体，包括 Anthropic 的 Claude Agent、OpenAI 的 Codex 与谷歌自家的 Antigravity；接入的智能体可继承项目结构、构建配置、符号以及 Compose Preview、模拟器、Logcat 等上下文。新增「变更审查」在应用修改前扫描智能体待提交改动；CLI 插件把同等能力带给 Claude Code 与 Codex，新命令 android preview 可在终端渲染 Compose 预览。谷歌称做界面任务的智能体因此快 77%、Token 最多省三倍；另有 android device remote 可调用其实验室真机。安卓产品副总裁 Matthew McCullough 强调要让开发者自由选择模型与智能体。

Google took the opposite approach to ownership. At its Android developer summit on Wednesday it shipped Android Studio Rabbit 2, which lets developers bring their own agent instead of insisting on Gemini. More than forty coding agents now plug in through an open protocol, among them Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI's Codex and Google's own Antigravity. Whichever you install inherits the project's structure, build files and logs, so it does not start by guessing. A new change review scans an agent's pending edits before they are applied, and a command line plugin carries the same tools into the terminal. Google says agents using the new preview command finish interface work seventy-seven percent faster while spending up to three times fewer tokens.

谷歌在「归属」这件事上走了相反的路。周三的安卓开发者大会上，它发布了 Android Studio Rabbit 2：开发者可以带上自己的智能体，而不必被要求用 Gemini。现在有四十多款编码智能体通过一套开放协议接入，其中包括 Anthropic 的 Claude、OpenAI 的 Codex 以及谷歌自家的 Antigravity。你装哪一款，它就继承这个项目的结构、构建文件和日志，不必从猜测开始。新增的「变更审查」会在改动被应用之前扫描智能体待提交的编辑；一个命令行插件把同样的工具带进终端。谷歌称，使用新预览命令的智能体完成界面工作快百分之七十七，同时最多只花三分之一的 Token。

The Right Name on the Wrong Package

摘要：Cursor 安全公告：10/29 太平洋时间 02:27–13:35（约 11 小时），其扩展市场上的 anthropic.claude-code 2.1.312 与 2.1.313 被替换为恶意构建，沿用真实扩展 ID 与已验证发布者身份。恶意载荷是插在正常 extension.js 之前的 66 行加载器，模块加载即执行、无需用户操作：把带 svc-telemetry 标记的攻击者公钥追加进 authorized_keys、尝试开启本地 SSH 服务、向固定 IP 建反向隧道并每 60 秒重试、用 LaunchAgent/计划任务持久化、回传主机名与 Git 邮箱等侦察数据。Cursor 称最多 5000 名用户受影响并称这是上限，强调该版本并非 Anthropic 开发；卸载扩展不会清除已写入的密钥，须升级到 2.1.314 并自查 authorized_keys。VS Code 市场与 npm 官方包未受影响。

The trust in that ecosystem took a direct hit last week. For about eleven hours on the twenty-ninth of October, a tampered build of Anthropic's Claude Code extension sat in Cursor's marketplace under the extension's real name and identity. Two versions, 2.1.312 and 2.1.313, carried a sixty-six line loader placed ahead of the real code. On load it appended an attacker's public key to the list of keys allowed to log in, tried to switch on the local SSH service, and opened a tunnel that retried every minute. Cursor estimates at most five thousand developers installed it, calls that an upper bound, and says the extension was not built by Anthropic. Uninstalling does not remove the key. The company pulled the package, briefly closed the marketplace, rotated its keys, and shipped a clean version.

这个生态里的信任在上周被直接击中。十月二十九日，在大约十一个小时里，一份被篡改的 Claude Code 扩展以真实名称和真实身份放在 Cursor 的扩展市场上。两个版本——2.1.312 与 2.1.313——在真实代码之前夹带了一段六十六行的加载器。它在加载时把攻击者的公钥追加进允许登录的密钥列表，尝试开启本地 SSH 服务，并建立一条每分钟重试的隧道。Cursor 估计最多有五千名开发者装了它，并称这是上限，同时强调该扩展并非 Anthropic 开发。卸载并不会移除那把密钥。公司下架了该包、短暂关闭市场、轮换密钥，并发布了一个干净版本。

A Model That Guesses Your Next Move

摘要：以色列初创公司 42AI 结束隐身，宣布 6250 万美元种子轮（创始团队与 Infinity 集团出资，历时三年），核心是「行为世界模型」：不靠外推历史行为，而是从 KYC、开户、客服通话的语音语调、来电次数、转账与交易等看似无关的数据点推断内在倾向，再以 Transformer 世界模型输出不同情境下各种决策的概率。已在大约十家大型券商用于预测交易员在剧烈波动中的反应；公司称交易方向与成交量的 F1 稳定在 0.8–0.85，所谓 98% 指的是「交易流落在预测区间内」而非逐笔命中。训练数据含两百万人以上的政府与生物样本库数据集、客户数据覆盖近三千万人；公司强调模型不需要个人可识别信息，「永远不知道这个人是谁」。

And a startup wants to put a number on your next decision. 42AI came out of stealth this week with sixty-two and a half million dollars and a claim that sounds like science fiction: a behavioural world model that can predict what a person will do in a situation it has never seen. Ten large brokerages already use it to guess how individual traders will react when markets lurch. The model reads tone of voice on support calls, how often someone phones, how they move money, then outputs a probability over possible choices. Its chief executive says behaviour is not deterministic but probabilistic; a week later you may be a different person, yet your tendencies still show up as a distribution. The company says the model never learns who you are.

还有一家初创公司想给你的下一个决定标上一个数字。42AI 本周结束隐身，带着六千二百五十万美元和一句听起来像科幻小说的主张：一个行为世界模型，能在它从未见过的新情境里预测一个人会怎么做。大约十家大型券商已经在用它推测市场剧烈波动时单个交易员会如何反应。模型会读取客服通话里的语调、一个人打电话的频率、他如何调动资金，然后对各种可能的选择输出一个概率。其首席执行官说，行为不是决定论的，而是概率性的；一周之后你也许已是另一个人，但你的倾向仍会以一个分布的形式呈现出来。公司称，模型从不知道你是谁。

Closing Thought

Six stories, and each one turns on a name. A war room with a company's name on the door, watching what its model says about an election. A prospectus with eight hundred ninety-five billion dollars signed in the footnotes. A building renamed so the neighbours will stop objecting. A code editor that lets you bring a rival's agent, as long as you know whose agent it is. An extension that carried the right name and the wrong author. And a model that assigns a probability to your judgement without ever learning your name. Names are cheap to print and expensive to earn. Most of this week was spent deciding which is which.

六条新闻，每一条都取决于一个名字。一间门上挂着公司名字的作战室，盯着自家模型如何谈论一场选举。一份在附注里签下八千九百五十亿美元的招股书。一栋被改了名字、好让邻居停止反对的楼。一个允许你带上对手智能体的代码编辑器，前提是你知道那是谁的智能体。一个名字正确、作者错误的扩展。还有一个给你的判断标上概率、却从不知道你名字的模型。名字印上去很便宜，挣回来很贵。这一周的大部分时间，都花在分辨这两者上。

朗读建议（Reading Tips）

  • 全文用中速朗读（约 110~120 词/分钟）；「朗读」按钮的音频按新闻播报语速（约 150 词/分钟）生成，可先跟读再脱稿。
  • 第 1 篇 "the record so far is uneven" 语气转平，uneven 稍重读，为后面的对比铺垫
  • 第 2 篇 "read like two different companies" 是全段总纲，two 与 different 之间略停
  • 第 3 篇 "a phrase meant to sound less like a smokestack" 略带讽意，smokestack 放慢
  • 第 4 篇 "bring their own agent" 连读自然，own 重读——这是谷歌与同行的分野
  • 第 5 篇 "Uninstalling does not remove the key." 短句单独成气，remove 重读后停顿
  • 第 6 篇 "behaviour is not deterministic but probabilistic" 两个形容词对比重读，but 处换气
  • 第 7 篇 "Names are cheap to print and expensive to earn." 对句结构，cheap 与 expensive 对比
  • 第 7 篇最后一句 "deciding which is which" 最慢，which is which 逐词顿开收尾
