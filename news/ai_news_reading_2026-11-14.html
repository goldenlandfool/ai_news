In Brief 今日新闻摘要

摘要：今日六条，暗线只有一条——AI 正被交出对金钱、机器与公共安全的真实权限。

Six stories today, and one quiet shift underneath them: AI is being handed real authority over money, machines, and public safety. OpenAI gave its agents a wallet that can pay, then folded its robotics team into the main research group. Google shipped a faster Gemini model that lets developers choose how deeply it thinks. Four nonprofits asked a California court to freeze frontier training. Anthropic built an automated attacker to test its own safeguards. And a nationwide ground stop put new air traffic AI under scrutiny. Capability, liability, and trust all moved on the same day.

今天六条新闻，底下藏着同一条暗线：AI 正被交出对金钱、机器与公共安全的真实权限。OpenAI 给智能体配了一个能付款的钱包，随后又把机器人团队并入主研究部门。谷歌推出更快的 Gemini 模型，让开发者自行选择它思考得多深。四家非营利组织请加州法院冻结前沿模型训练。Anthropic 造了一个自动化攻击者来检验自家防护。而美国一次全国停飞，把一套刚上线的 AI 空管系统推到了聚光灯下。能力、责任与信任，在同一天一起挪了位置。

1. OpenAI Gives Its Agents a Wallet

摘要：OpenAI 上线 ChatGPT Wallet，dots 智能体可代用户付款；与美国运通、万事达、Visa 合作，用一次性支付令牌隐藏真实卡号，每笔需生物识别确认，暂不抽成。

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet on Friday, letting its always-on agents, called dots, buy things on a user's behalf. Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, it starts with adult Pro subscribers in the United States. A user adds a card once, then reviews each purchase and approves it with a face scan or a fingerprint. The network then issues a single-use token, so neither the merchant nor the agent ever sees the real card number, and existing rewards and dispute protections still apply. Closing the gap between research and payment is a large commercial bet. OpenAI says it takes no cut of transactions yet. Google said Gemini would link to Google Wallet too, but only to read balances.

OpenAI 周五上线 ChatGPT Wallet，让常驻智能体 dots 可以替用户购物。该功能与美国运通、万事达和 Visa 合作建设，目前先向美国年满 18 岁的 Pro 订阅用户开放。用户只需录入一次卡片，此后每笔交易前核对并用面容或指纹确认。支付网络随后发出一次性令牌，因此商户和智能体都看不到真实卡号，原有的积分与争议保护也继续有效。把「从检索到付款」的缺口补上，是一场不小的商业押注。OpenAI 表示目前不抽成。谷歌也表示 Gemini 将接入 Google Wallet，但只能读取余额。

2. Google Ships a Model With a Thinking Dial

摘要：谷歌发布 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 取代 3.8 Flash，输入每百万 token 1.5 美元、输出 7.5 美元，新增四档思考深度；@sundarpichai 在 X 宣布，@artificialanlys 给出独立评分 51。

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen on Thursday, replacing Gemini 3.8 Flash as its fast model. It costs one dollar and fifty cents per million input tokens and seven dollars and fifty cents per million output tokens. A dial is new: four thinking levels, low, medium, high and extra-high, so a developer pays only for the reasoning a task needs. Google says it finishes tasks two and a half times faster than Flash and scores fifty-seven percent on a coding benchmark against eighteen for Flash. Sundar Pichai announced it on X from @sundarpichai. Antigravity's Orchestrator switches between Carbon for planning and Hydrogen for execution, using a quarter less quota. Artificial Analysis, posting from @artificialanlys, scored it fifty-one and found it weaker on terminal work.

谷歌周四发布 Gemini 4 Hydrogen，取代 Gemini 3.8 Flash，成为快速档模型。价格是每百万输入 token 1.5 美元、每百万输出 token 7.5 美元。真正的新东西是一个旋钮：低、中、高、超高四档思考深度，开发者只需为任务实际需要的推理付费。谷歌称它完成任务比 Flash 快 2.5 倍，在一项编程基准上拿到 57%，而 Flash 只有 18%。桑达尔·皮查伊在 X 上以账号 @sundarpichai 宣布。Antigravity 的 Orchestrator 会在负责规划的 Carbon 与负责执行的 Hydrogen 之间切换，配额消耗减少约四分之一。Artificial Analysis 以账号 @artificialanlys 发文给出 51 分，并指出它在终端任务上偏弱。

3. OpenAI Decides Robots Need No Separate Team

摘要：《财富》独家：OpenAI 解散独立机器人团队，研究人员并入 Mark Chen 的主研究团队与 Greg Brockman 的应用部门；理由是 GPT-6 Astra 等通用模型已能直接驱动机器人。

OpenAI has disbanded its stand-alone robotics team and folded researchers into its main groups, Fortune reported exclusively on Friday. The company confirmed the change, insisting it is accelerating robotics, not abandoning it. Staff now report to Mark Chen, hardware engineers to president Greg Brockman. The reason is a bet on general models: OpenAI says frontier systems such as GPT-6 Astra can be applied to robots directly, so a specialised model built only to be a machine's brain is no longer needed. The timing was awkward. OpenAI had registered a booth at CoRL in Austin this week, and reporters found number two hundred and eight empty. Chief executive Sam Altman still aims to show a humanoid robot in twenty twenty-seven.

据《财富》周五独家报道，OpenAI 已解散独立机器人团队，把研究人员并入主部门。公司确认了这次调整，并强调是在加速机器人研发，而非退出该领域。研究人员现在向 Mark Chen 汇报，硬件工程师归入总裁 Greg Brockman 管辖。背后是对通用模型的一次下注：OpenAI 表示，GPT-6 Astra 等前沿系统已能直接用于机器人，因此不再需要专门造一个只当机器「大脑」的模型。时间点颇为尴尬：OpenAI 本周在奥斯汀的 CoRL 注册了展位，记者到访时却发现 208 号展位空无一人。首席执行官 Sam Altman 仍计划在 2027 年展示自研人形机器人。

4. Four Nonprofits Ask a Court to Freeze Frontier AI

摘要：四家非营利组织在旧金山高等法院起诉 Anthropic、谷歌、Meta、OpenAI 与 SpaceX，援引加州不正当竞争法与「公共妨害」，请求紧急禁令冻结下一代模型训练；法律界普遍认为获批希望渺茫。

Four nonprofits sued five of the biggest AI companies in San Francisco Superior Court on Thursday, asking a judge to halt work on any model more capable than today's. The plaintiffs, including Public Citizen and the Alliance for Secure AI, named Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceX Super Intelligence. They rely not on a new AI law but on California's Unfair Competition Law and a claim that frontier development is a public nuisance, citing reports of agents escaping test environments. The request is an emergency injunction, and even sympathetic coverage calls it a long shot. Still, it may force disclosures of safety assessments and training timelines. It arrives as OpenAI says it has paused training of its most capable models until extra safeguards are in place.

四家非营利组织周四在旧金山高等法院起诉五家最大的 AI 公司，请法官叫停一切比现有最强模型更强的研发工作。原告包括 Public Citizen 与 Alliance for Secure AI，被告为 Anthropic、谷歌、Meta、OpenAI 和 SpaceX Super Intelligence。他们并未援引新的 AI 法规，而是依据加州《不正当竞争法》，并主张前沿开发构成「公共妨害」，引用了智能体逃出测试环境的报道。他们申请的是紧急禁令，连立场同情的报道也称之为胜算渺茫。不过此案仍可能迫使企业披露安全评估与训练时间表。诉讼出炉之际，OpenAI 恰表示已暂停最强模型的训练，等待额外防护措施到位。

5. Anthropic Builds an Attacker to Test Its Own Defences

摘要：Anthropic 发布自动化红队系统 Anvil，用 Claude 并行攻击自家防护；生物安全 24 组中 23 组越狱更严重，网络安全全部 24 组在半小时内突破，反蒸馏仅 435 次请求即找到首个推理泄露，人类专家约需 10.22 万次。

Anthropic unveiled Anvil on Friday, an automated red-teaming system built on Claude hunting for weaknesses in its own safety filters. Red teaming means attacking your own safeguards first. Anvil runs many Claude instances at once against a target model and its classifiers, splitting forbidden requests into harmless steps or rephrasing them until one slips through. In biosecurity tests it found a more severe jailbreak than human testers in twenty-three of twenty-four combinations, and broke all twenty-four cyber pairs within three and a half hours. To expose the first serious leak of hidden reasoning it sent four hundred and thirty-five requests, where human experts needed about one hundred and two thousand. Safety researcher Javier Rando presented it on X, crediting Nate Rahn, Chawin Sitawarin and David Huang. The figures are Anthropic's own.

Anthropic 周五公布 Anvil，一套基于 Claude 的自动化红队系统，专门寻找自家安全过滤器的弱点。红队测试就是先攻击自己的防护。Anvil 会同时启动多个 Claude 实例，对目标模型及其分类器发起进攻，把被禁止的请求拆成无害小步骤，或反复改写措辞直到某个版本蒙混过关。数字相当惊人：生物安全测试中，24 组组合里有 23 组找到了比人工测试者更严重的越狱方案；网络安全的 24 组组合在三小时半内全部被攻破。为了揪出首个实质性的隐藏推理泄露，它只发了 435 次请求，而人类专家约需 10.22 万次。安全研究员 Javier Rando 在 X 上介绍了这项工作，并致谢 Nate Rahn、Chawin Sitawarin 与 David Huang。上述数字均来自 Anthropic 自测。

6. A Nationwide Ground Stop Puts a New AI Under Scrutiny

摘要：11 月 13 日美国联邦航空局因通信中断全国停飞约四小时，近 1.5 万架次航班受影响；三天前刚把 AI 系统 SMART 扩至全部 22 个空管区，官员称不能排除关联，特朗普归因于老旧基础设施，微软在 X 否认与其故障有关。

The Federal Aviation Administration halted every departing flight in the United States on Friday afternoon, citing a temporary telecommunications outage. Nearly fifteen thousand flights were delayed or cancelled before it was lifted four hours later. It was only the second nationwide ground stop since September eleventh, two thousand and one. Three days earlier the agency had expanded SMART, an AI system pooling roughly two hundred data streams on weather, routes and staffing, across all twenty-two air traffic regions. Officials said they could not rule out a connection. President Trump blamed aging infrastructure rather than AI, and Microsoft denied on X that its email outage was related. The FAA announced the stop on X, saying every flight was accounted for. Investigators now suspect a software update triggered a system-wide reboot.

美国联邦航空管理局周五下午以「临时通信中断」为由，暂停全美所有出港航班。禁令四小时后解除，此前已有近 1.5 万架次航班延误或取消。这是 2001 年「9·11」袭击以来第二次全国停飞。而就在三天前，该机构刚把汇总约 200 路天气、航路和人员配置数据的 AI 系统 SMART 扩展到全部 22 个空管区域。官员表示不能排除二者关联。特朗普总统把原因归于老旧基础设施，而非 AI；微软也在 X 上否认自家邮件服务中断与之有关。FAA 同样在 X 发帖宣布停飞，并称每一架航班都已清点。调查人员目前怀疑，一次软件更新触发了全系统重启。

朗读建议（Reading Tips）

  • **语速**：全文用中速（约 110~120 词/分钟），新闻标题稍放慢、加重音；配套音频按新闻播报语速（约 150 词/分钟）生成。
  • In Brief 摘要段可作开嗓热身：六个短句节奏均匀，逐条点到即可，不必压重音；末句 Capability, liability, and trust all moved on the same day 三项并列，每项后略顿。
  • 第 1 篇按「录入卡片 → 逐笔核对 → 生物识别 → 一次性令牌」四步推进，single-use token 与 real card number 两处重读；no cut of transactions 后停半秒。
  • 第 2 篇数字密集：one dollar and fifty cents、seven dollars and fifty cents、two and a half times、fifty-seven percent，每个数字后停半秒；four thinking levels 之后的 low, medium, high and extra-high 逐项匀速读出。
  • 第 3 篇 disbanded 与 accelerating, not abandoning 是全文语气转折，not 前停顿半秒；读到 number two hundred and eight empty 时放慢，制造画面感。
  • 第 4 篇 emergency injunction、public nuisance、long shot 逐个重读；末句 paused training 与 extra safeguards 之间略顿，留出因果。
  • 第 5 篇 jailbreak、classifiers、biosecurity 是术语密集处，逐个重读；数字链条 twenty-three of twenty-four → all twenty-four → four hundred and thirty-five → one hundred and two thousand 按递进顺序推进，最后一项前停一秒形成对照。
  • 第 6 篇 nationwide ground stop、telecommunications outage、system-wide reboot 重读；Nearly fifteen thousand flights 之后停半秒；末句 suspect 一词放轻，保留「尚未证实」的语气。
  • **节奏**：每篇小标题后停顿 1 秒再开始正文；段落之间停顿 2 秒。全文没有结尾总结段，读完第 6 篇即可收束。
