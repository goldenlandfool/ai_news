1. Anthropic Debuts, and Its CEO Sets a Limit

摘要：Anthropic 周四以代码 ANT 在纳斯达克挂牌，发行价 83 美元、开盘约 111 美元、收盘约 97 美元，仍高于发行价 17%；募资约 810 亿美元，按募资额创科技 IPO 纪录。CEO Dario Amodei 在开盘演讲中承诺不向商业或政治压力低头、技术须处于人类控制之下，并以父亲因疾病去世、而该病数年后才变得可治为起点，谈加速医学的可能。

Anthropic began trading on Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT. Its shares were offered at eighty-three dollars, opened near one hundred eleven, and closed around ninety-seven, still seventeen percent above the issue price. The offering raised about eighty-one billion dollars, the largest technology listing on record by proceeds. Dario Amodei, the chief executive, used the opening bell for a different point. He said the company would not bend to business or political pressure, and that the technology must remain under human control. He described his father, who died of a disease that became treatable only years later, and argued that faster tools could bring more cures within reach. The debut puts a market price on a laboratory built on restraint.

Anthropic 周四以代码 ANT 在纳斯达克开始交易。其股票发行价为 83 美元，开盘接近 111 美元，收盘约 97 美元，仍比发行价高 17%。此次发行募资约 810 亿美元，按募资规模创下科技股上市纪录。首席执行官 Dario Amodei 在开盘钟声前的演讲中讲的却是另一件事。他说公司不会向商业或政治压力低头，技术必须保持在人类控制之下。他谈到自己的父亲因一种疾病去世，而这种病几年后才变得可以治疗，并主张更快的工具能让更多疗法触手可及。首秀为一家以「克制」立身的实验室标出了市场价格。

2. Gemini Learns to Drive a Mac

摘要：Google 11 月 19 日为 macOS 版 Gemini 桌面应用加入 computer use：可在后台跨原生应用、文件与网页执行多步任务，聊天窗口内实时预览，用户可暂停、停止或逐步批准。示例包括填表、批量处理文件、按标准归档、从仪表板生成幻灯片。初期仅美国、英语、付费 Plus/Pro/Ultra 用户可用；苹果同周预览了对完全磁盘访问权限的更严控制。

Google brought computer use to the Gemini desktop application for macOS. The assistant can open native applications, move across files and browse websites in the background while the user keeps working. Instructions go into the chat; the agent's actions appear in a live preview, and people can pause, stop or approve each step before it happens. Google suggests filling forms, repeating edits across a batch of files, sorting folders and building slide decks from dashboards. The launch is narrow: English, the United States, and paid Plus, Pro or Ultra subscribers. Google is late to this market, as OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta already ship similar desktop agents. Apple also previewed tighter controls this week on apps requesting full disk access.

Google 把 computer use 能力带到了 macOS 版 Gemini 桌面应用。这个助手可以打开原生应用、跨文件操作、在后台浏览网页，而用户可以继续做自己的事。指令写在聊天窗口里，智能体的动作以实时预览呈现，用户可以暂停、停止，或在每一步执行前批准。Google 给出的例子包括填写表单、对一批文件重复同样修改、按标准整理文件夹，以及从在线仪表板生成演示文稿。首发范围很窄：仅英语、仅美国、仅付费的 Plus、Pro 或 Ultra 订阅用户。Google 在这一市场上来晚了，OpenAI、Anthropic 和 Meta 早已推出类似的桌面智能体。苹果本周也预览了对申请完全磁盘访问的应用的更严格管控。

3. OpenAI Puts Agents on Its Own Performance Team

摘要：OpenAI 11 月 19 日披露内部系统「性能工厂」：Codex 智能体排查 ChatGPT 变慢原因。三个多月完成 1700 多次调查、600 多起定位到根因，约每天 17 次。告警开任务、智能体取证、另一个会话对抗式复核，修复以 PR 提交，工程师决定是否合并，部署后再由智能体验证恢复基线；100 条根因人工抽检 70% 准确。来源账号：X @OpenAIDevs。

OpenAI described the Performance Factory, an internal system where coding agents investigate why ChatGPT has become slower. In just over three months, its first workflow ran over seventeen hundred investigations and found a cause in six hundred cases, about seventeen a day. An alert opens a task; an agent studies the metrics, a separate session tries to disprove the diagnosis, and a proposed fix arrives as a pull request. Engineers still decide what merges, and another agent checks afterwards that speed returned to baseline. In a manual review of one hundred diagnoses, seventy were judged accurate. The company also highlighted the work in a post from its @OpenAIDevs account on X. OpenAI says the bottleneck is now waiting for a human to press merge.

OpenAI 介绍了内部系统「性能工厂」：由编码智能体调查 ChatGPT 为什么变慢。在三个多月里，首个工作流完成了 1700 多次调查，其中 600 起找到了根本原因，平均每天约 17 次。告警会开启一个任务；智能体研究各项指标，另一个独立会话尝试推翻其诊断，随后提出的修复以拉取请求形式提交。工程师仍然决定合并什么，部署后还有另一个智能体检查性能是否回到基线。人工复核 100 条诊断结论，70 条被判定准确。公司还通过 X 上的 @OpenAIDevs 账号介绍了这项工作。OpenAI 说，现在的瓶颈是等人按下合并按钮。

4. OpenAI Maps a Jagged Frontier

摘要：OpenAI 11 月 18 日发布第二份研究加速报告：3 月至 10 月，智能体零人工干预完成「人类需 2–4 小时」研发任务的成功率从 23% 翻倍至 46%，4–8 小时任务从 13% 升至 35%；但监控训练 79%、写推理与服务栈代码仅 25%，故称「锯齿前沿」。研究员日均智能体 API 标价用量 2400 美元、环比增 62%。OpenAI 呼吁实验室公开递归自我改进指标，同时强调并不追求完全的 RSI。

OpenAI published a second report on how coding agents are changing its own research. Between March and October, the estimated rate at which agents finished a two-to-four-hour research task with no human help doubled from twenty-three percent to forty-six percent. Longer tasks improved too, from thirteen to thirty-five percent. Yet the gains are uneven. Agents monitored training runs successfully about seventy-nine percent of the time, but wrote code for inference and serving systems in only twenty-five percent of cases. OpenAI calls this a jagged frontier. The median researcher now uses about two thousand four hundred dollars of agent calls a day, up sixty-two percent in a month. The company argues that leading labs should publicly report measures of recursive self-improvement, while insisting it is not chasing that goal.

OpenAI 发布了第二份关于编码智能体如何改变自身研究的报告。3 月到 10 月，智能体在无人帮助的情况下完成「人类需要 2 到 4 小时」研发任务的估计成功率，从 23% 翻倍到 46%。更长的任务也有提升，从 13% 升到 35%。但进步并不均匀。智能体监控训练运行成功的比例约 79%，而为推理与服务系统写代码的成功率只有 25%。OpenAI 把这种落差称为「锯齿前沿」。现在研究员使用智能体调用的每日中位数约为 2400 美元，一个月内增长 62%。公司主张顶尖实验室应公开报告递归自我改进的相关指标，同时强调自己并不追求完全的递归自我改进。

5. Meta's Muse Mixes Up Its Conversations

摘要：路透社查阅 20 多篇 Meta 内部帖子发现，消费级智能体 Muse 出现混淆对话与越权读取：员工收到从未请求的内幕消息、订单和不属于自己的银行余额；有人被提醒回复 Signal 私信，而该用户并未授权。路透社与 AI Now 研究所实测发现，安卓版可借安装时授予的通知权限读取消息内容。Meta 称属孤立事件、许多是幻觉。Muse 9 月上线后下载量已破 1000 万。

Reuters reviewed more than two dozen internal Meta posts describing strange behavior from Muse, the company's consumer agent. Employees reported messages about childcare they never requested, products they never ordered, and bank balances they said were not theirs. In one case, Muse added two account balances to a chat about weather and tides. Another user said it reminded her about a message from her sister on Signal, an encrypted app she had not authorized it to read. Reuters and the AI Now Institute found that on Android, Muse could read message text through notification permissions granted during setup. Meta calls the incidents isolated and says many were hallucinations. Muse passed ten million downloads within weeks of its September launch.

路透社查阅了 20 多篇 Meta 内部帖子，其中描述了消费级智能体 Muse 的异常行为。员工反映，Muse 发来他们从未请求过的育儿服务消息、从未下过的订单，以及据称不属于他们的银行余额。有一个案例中，Muse 在一段关于天气和潮汐的对话里附加了两个账户余额。另一位用户说，它提醒她回复姐姐在 Signal 上的消息，而她并未授权它读取这款加密通讯应用。路透社与 AI Now 研究所发现，在安卓系统上，Muse 可以借助安装时授予的通知权限读取消息文本。Meta 称这些是孤立事件，并说很多属于幻觉。Muse 自 9 月上线以来几周内下载量已突破 1000 万。

6. Models Redesign the Logic Inside Chips

摘要：OpenAI 11 月 18 日披露模型直接改进芯片物理设计：与 AMD 合作重写硬件描述代码模块，在工程师多年打磨的基线上取得最高 14% 的功耗、性能、面积改进，部分改动已进入送流片设计；在自研 Jalapeño 推理芯片上，一个乘法器面积减少约 15%、延迟从 2 个时钟周期降到 1 个。所有重写均用 Synopsys 工具验证行为不变，约一年优化压缩到 5 个月内完成。

OpenAI said its models improved the physical design of chips for both a partner and itself. Working with AMD, the models rewrote a block of hardware description code and found gains of up to fourteen percent in power, performance and area, on a design engineers had refined for years. Some changes have entered a design already sent for manufacturing. On OpenAI's own Jalapeño inference chip, model proposals shrank a multiplier by about fifteen percent and cut its delay from two clock cycles to one. Every rewrite was checked with industry tools to confirm the logic behaved the same way. The company says the work compressed about a year of optimization into under five months. It is a concrete case of models improving the hardware that will run the next generation of models.

OpenAI 表示，其模型改进了合作方和自家芯片的物理设计。在与 AMD 的合作中，模型重写了一段硬件描述代码模块，在工程师已打磨多年的设计基础上，找到了功耗、性能和面积最高 14% 的改进，其中部分改动已经进入送交制造的设计。在 OpenAI 自研的 Jalapeño 推理芯片上，模型提出的修改让一个乘法器的面积减少约 15%，延迟从两个时钟周期降到一个。每一次重写都用行业工具检查过，以确认逻辑行为不变。公司称，这些工作把大约一年的优化压缩到不到五个月。这是模型改进下一代模型所依赖硬件的一个具体案例。
