In Brief 今日新闻摘要

摘要：今日六条，主线是「谁来掌舵」——从英伟达换帅到智能体被拦在购票之外，权力、信任与资金同一天重新洗牌。

Six stories today, one question: who is in charge? Nvidia lost the founder who led it for thirty-three years and handed the company to its finance chief. Google and Samsung began selling Gemini glasses that put privacy before features. DeepMind taught a model to read DNA one cell at a time. TSMC agreed to invest a billion dollars in the labs that buy its chips. United Airlines told AI agents they may look at flights but not book them. And a coding tool left thirty-five thousand private apps open to strangers. Leadership, trust, and money moved on the same day.

今天六条新闻，一个问题：谁在掌舵？英伟达失去了执掌三十三年的创始人，把公司交给财务负责人。谷歌与三星开始发售把隐私摆在功能之前的 Gemini 眼镜。DeepMind 教会模型逐个细胞地读取 DNA。台积电同意向采购自家芯片的实验室投资十亿美元。美联航告诉 AI 智能体：可以看航班，不能替你下单。一款编程工具把三万五千个私人应用晾在了陌生人面前。领导力、信任与资金，在同一天一起挪了位置。

1. Nvidia Loses the Founder Who Defined an Era

摘要：黄仁勋即刻辞去英伟达总裁、CEO 及董事职务，CFO 科莱特·克雷斯任临时负责人，股价开盘跌近 14%；开源阵营担忧 Hugging Face 收购与开放权重路线生变。

Nvidia said on Thursday that Jensen Huang had resigned as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately, after a board investigation into his personal conduct. Colette Kress, its finance chief, will serve as interim leader while the board searches for a successor. Shares fell almost fourteen percent at the open, briefly pulling its value below four trillion dollars. The loss reaches beyond one company: Huang was the loudest supporter of open-weight models, and Nvidia's plan to buy Hugging Face for nearly thirteen billion dollars is already under Justice Department review. Hours later, Hugging Face chief executive Clement Delangue posted on X from @ClementDelangue, calling it a really sad day but promising to keep fighting for open-source AI harder than ever.

英伟达周四宣布，黄仁勋已辞去总裁、首席执行官及董事职务，即刻生效；此前董事会就其个人行为展开调查。财务负责人科莱特·克雷斯将出任临时负责人，董事会同时寻找正式继任者。开盘股价下跌近 14%，市值一度跌破四万亿美元。影响远超一家公司：黄仁勋一直是开放权重模型最响亮的支持者，而英伟达以近 130 亿美元收购 Hugging Face 的计划已在接受司法部审查。几小时后，Hugging Face 首席执行官克莱芒·德朗格在 X 上以账号 @ClementDelangue 发文，称这是「非常难过的一天」，但承诺会比以往更努力地为开源 AI 而战。

2. Google Sells Glasses by Promising Not to Watch

摘要：谷歌与三星开放 Gemini 智能眼镜预订，395 美元起；无显示屏，靠录制指示灯、禁直播与禁人脸识别把隐私做成卖点。

Google and Samsung opened preorders on Thursday for the first glasses built around Gemini, starting at three hundred and ninety-five dollars. With no display, a camera and speakers let you ask about a poster or hear walking directions. After Google Glass became a joke about surveillance, Google is selling privacy as the main feature. An outward light must glow whenever the camera records, and if it is covered or broken, the camera refuses to turn on. Live streaming and face recognition are blocked, faces are blurred before processing, and images are not saved to Gemini history or used to train models or target ads. Ars Technica notes two gaps: voice chats lack that promise, and data is not encrypted on Google's servers.

谷歌与三星周四开放了首款围绕 Gemini 打造的智能眼镜预订，起价 395 美元。眼镜没有显示屏，摄像头和扬声器让你可以询问眼前海报的内容，或听取步行导航。在 Google Glass 因监控争议沦为笑柄之后，谷歌把隐私当成了主打卖点。只要摄像头在工作，朝外的指示灯就必须亮起；若指示灯被遮挡或失效，摄像头干脆拒绝开启。直播和人脸识别都被禁止，画面送交处理前会先模糊人脸，拍摄的图像不会存入 Gemini 历史记录，也不会用于训练模型或投放广告。Ars Technica 指出两处缺口：语音对话并不享受同等承诺，数据在谷歌服务器上也不加密。

3. DeepMind Reads DNA One Cell at a Time

摘要：DeepMind 发布 AlphaGenome 2，按细胞类型预测 DNA 变异效应，训练覆盖上亿个单细胞图谱，免疫细胞基准上因果变异召回由 16% 升至 30%。

Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, which predicts how a DNA change behaves inside one specific kind of cell. Every cell carries the same genome, yet a heart cell and an immune cell read different parts of it. Earlier models averaged across whole tissues and missed such effects. The new model is conditional: give it a stretch of DNA plus a cell type or cell state, and it predicts gene expression, chromatin accessibility and DNA methylation for that condition. Trained on more than one hundred million single-cell profiles, it nearly doubled the share of causal variants recovered on an immune-cell benchmark, from sixteen percent to thirty percent. DeepMind offers it through an API for non-commercial research and warns it is a research tool, not a clinical test.

谷歌 DeepMind 发布了 AlphaGenome 2，它能预测一段 DNA 变化在某一种特定细胞里会产生什么影响。每个细胞都携带相同的基因组，但心肌细胞和免疫细胞读取的部位不同。早先的模型把答案在整块组织上取平均，漏掉了这类效果。新版是条件模型：给它一段 DNA，再加一个细胞类型或细胞状态，它便据此预测该条件下的基因表达、染色质可及性和 DNA 甲基化。它在一亿多个单细胞图谱上训练，在一项免疫细胞基准上找出的因果变异占比几乎翻倍，从 16% 升到 30%。DeepMind 已通过 API 向非商业研究开放，并明确提醒：这是研究工具，不是临床检测。

4. The Chipmaker Buys a Stake in Its Own Customers

摘要：台积电董事会批准最多 10 亿美元投资 Anthropic 与 OpenAI 各 5 亿，被指「循环融资」新样本；英伟达已分别投入 100 亿与 300 亿美元。

TSMC, the Taiwanese foundry that makes most of the world's advanced AI chips, has approved investing up to one billion dollars in two of its own best customers. According to Ars Technica, the board cleared five hundred million dollars for OpenAI preferred stock and the same amount for Anthropic common shares. Analysts call this circular financing: the supplier funds the customer, who then spends the money on the supplier's chips, which makes real demand hard to measure. TSMC can afford it: six straight quarters of record profit, including more than twenty-three billion dollars in the three months through September. Nvidia has already put thirty billion dollars into OpenAI and ten billion into Anthropic, so TSMC's billion is modest, but it ties a supposedly neutral manufacturer more tightly to the boom.

台积电是全球多数先进 AI 芯片的实际制造者，其董事会已批准向自家两个最大客户投资最多十亿美元。据 Ars Technica 报道，董事会同意以五亿美元认购 OpenAI 优先股，并以同样金额买入 Anthropic 普通股。分析师称之为「循环融资」：供应商出钱给客户，客户再拿这笔钱买供应商的芯片，真实需求因此变得难以衡量。台积电下得起这个注：连续六个季度刷新利润纪录，仅截至 9 月的三个月就赚了超过 230 亿美元。相比之下，英伟达已向 OpenAI 投入 300 亿美元、向 Anthropic 投入 100 亿美元，台积电这十亿美元并不算大，却把一家本应中立的制造商更紧地绑上了这轮繁荣。

5. United Airlines Draws a Line at the Ticket Counter

摘要：美联航以安全与联邦航空法规为由，禁止未经授权的第三方个人 AI 智能体订票或改签；美洲银行上周刚提醒客户勿把密码交给 AI 助手。

United Airlines said it will stop personal AI assistants from booking flights or managing reservations on a traveler's behalf. The airline is updating its booking, ticketing and distribution policies to block unauthorised third-party agents, on its own platforms and through aggregators, according to TechCrunch and The Verge. Sharing a password can expose sensitive information or lead to unintended purchases and spent MileagePlus miles. Federal aviation rules also require a human to complete check-in and confirm that baggage holds no banned items. The move follows a warning from Bank of America, which last week asked customers not to hand passwords to AI assistants. Southwest has made its inventory visible inside ChatGPT, though the traveler still finishes on the airline's site. The line is about completing a transaction, not reading a timetable.

美联航表示，将阻止个人 AI 助手替旅客订票或管理预订。据 TechCrunch 与 The Verge 报道，这家航空公司正在更新订票、出票和分销政策，在自有平台及聚合渠道上封禁未经授权的第三方智能体。共用密码可能泄露敏感信息，或导致误下单、错花里程积分。联邦航空法规也要求由真人完成值机，并确认行李中不含违禁物品。此前一周，美洲银行刚提醒客户不要把密码交给 AI 助手。西南航空则让自己的航班库存出现在 ChatGPT 里，但旅客仍要回到航司网站完成交易。这条界线划在「完成交易」上，而不是「查询时刻表」。

6. A Coding Tool Left Thirty-Five Thousand Apps Open

摘要：安全公司 Claroc 发现 Grok Build 生成的 3.5 万多个应用无需登录即可访问，秘密藏在子域名被被动 DNS 收录；研究者在 X 上 @elonmusk 后，涉事内部应用才被撤下。

A security firm found that more than thirty-five thousand small apps built with Grok Build, the coding agent from xAI, were readable by anyone, because their addresses were listed in public databases. Claroc tested about forty-three thousand addresses under grok.me; of the thirty-seven thousand still online, roughly ninety-five percent opened with no sign-in. The flaw is in the design: each app hides its secret in four random words placed in the subdomain, where passive DNS services record and publish it, while ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini keep it in the path of the link. About one in ten held private material, including a hospital staff roster and a timesheet with eighteen passwords in plain text. Twenty-four belonged to SpaceX and X staff. Claroc says those came down only after it tagged @elonmusk on X.

一家安全公司发现，用 xAI 编程智能体 Grok Build 生成的三万五千多个小应用，任何人都能打开，因为这些地址已被收录进公开数据库。Claroc 测试了 grok.me 下约四万三千个地址；仍在线的三万七千个里，约 95% 无需登录即可打开。问题出在设计上：每个应用把秘密藏在四个随机单词里，却放在子域名位置，被动 DNS 服务会记录并公开它；ChatGPT、Claude、Gemini 则把秘密放在链接路径中。约十分之一含有私密内容，包括一份医院员工名册和一张写着十八个明文密码的工时表。其中二十四个属于 SpaceX 与 X 的员工。Claroc 称，直到它在 X 上点名 @elonmusk，这些应用才被撤下。

朗读建议（Reading Tips）

  • **语速**：全文用中速（约 110~120 词/分钟），新闻标题稍放慢、加重音；配套音频按新闻播报语速（约 150 词/分钟）生成。
  • In Brief 摘要段可作开嗓热身：连续六个短句节奏均匀，末句 Leadership, trust, and money moved on the same day 三项并列，每项后略顿。
  • 第 1 篇 effective immediately、interim leader、open-weight models 三处重读；almost fourteen percent 与 four trillion dollars 之后各停半秒，让听众消化冲击。
  • 第 2 篇按「价格 → 功能 → 隐私」三层推进，读到 is selling privacy as the main feature 时放慢；two gaps 之后的两个分句各成一个语气单元。
  • 第 3 篇术语密集：genome、variant、chromatin accessibility、DNA methylation，逐个重读并在逗号处停顿；from sixteen percent to thirty percent 前后形成对照，可略提速。
  • 第 4 篇 circular financing 是全文最该加重的一个短语，读完后停一秒再解释 supplier 与 customer 的循环；six straight quarters、twenty-three billion 之后略顿。
  • 第 5 篇 unauthorised third-party agents 与 completing a transaction 是关键词；末句 The line is about completing a transaction, not reading a timetable 前后两半对照，not 前停顿半秒。
  • 第 6 篇 subdomain、passive DNS、in plain text 逐个重读；数字链条 about forty-three thousand → thirty-seven thousand → ninety-five percent 按时间顺序推进，每个数字后停半秒。
  • **节奏**：每篇小标题后停顿 1 秒再开始正文；段落之间停顿 2 秒。全文没有结尾总结段，读完第 6 篇即可收束。
