1. FCC Approves a Million Satellites for Data Centers

摘要：FCC 有条件批准 SpaceX 百万卫星轨道数据中心：太阳能供电、辐射散热，2033 年起分阶段部署；天文学家警告轨道拥挤。

The Federal Communications Commission voted on Wednesday to authorize SpaceX's Starmind constellation, up to one million satellites carrying AI chips into orbit. SpaceX argues that satellites in near-constant sunlight can draw power directly from the sun and cool themselves by radiating heat, avoiding the water and land that ground data centers consume. FCC Chairman Brendan Carr said the approval would speed what he called America's lead in the age of super intelligence. The licence is conditional. Deployment must begin by November twenty thirty three, reach ten percent of the fleet by twenty thirty five, and half by twenty thirty eight. About seventeen thousand satellites orbit Earth today. The agency logged more than fifteen hundred public comments, with astronomers warning about a crowded sky.

美国联邦通信委员会周三投票批准 SpaceX 的 Starmind 星座，最多一百万颗搭载 AI 芯片的卫星进入轨道。SpaceX 主张，处于近乎恒定日照中的卫星可以直接从太阳取电，并通过辐射散热，从而避开地面数据中心消耗的水与土地。FCC 主席布兰登·卡尔说，这项批准将加快他所称的美国在超级智能时代的领先地位。许可是有条件的：必须在 2033 年 11 月前开始部署，2035 年前达到机群的百分之十，2038 年前完成一半。如今环绕地球的卫星约一万七千颗。该机构收到超过一千五百条公众意见，天文学家警告天空会变得拥挤。

2. An OpenAI Model Settles a Math Question

摘要：OpenAI 内部推理模型产出 111 页预印本，把矩阵乘法指数锁在 9/4；补上匹配下界，但未经同行评审。

OpenAI posted a one-hundred-and-eleven-page preprint to its mathematics repository on Tuesday, claiming that the exponent for multiplying two square matrices is exactly nine fourths. The paper is credited only to OpenAI, and the work came from an internal reasoning model. Since Strassen's result in nineteen sixty nine, researchers had pushed the upper bound down only to about two point three seven. The new paper supplies a matching lower bound, so the two ends meet. Greg Brockman, OpenAI's president, posted on X that the exponent is exactly nine fourths. The result is not peer reviewed. The repository warns that unformalized proofs may contain errors, and experts note it will not make real graphics cards faster. Still, it closes a question that had stood for decades.

OpenAI 周二向自己的数学仓库提交了一份 111 页的预印本，声称两个方阵相乘的指数恰好是四分之九。论文作者只写 OpenAI，工作来自一个内部推理模型。自 1969 年 Strassen 的结果以来，研究者只把上界压到约 2.37。这篇新论文给出了匹配的下界，两端就此接合。OpenAI 总裁格雷格·布罗克曼在 X 上发帖说，指数恰好是四分之九。结果尚未经过同行评审；仓库提醒，未形式化的证明可能有误，专家也指出它不会让真实的显卡变快。尽管如此，它终结了一个悬置数十年的难题。

3. DeepMind Lets Agents Run the Experiments

摘要：DeepMind 让智能体掌控部分算力跑 Gemini 实验；不奖励思维链以保留可读性，AGI 以用户信任而非基准衡量。

Google DeepMind has begun letting AI agents control part of its computing budget to run experiments for the next Gemini model. Research vice president Koray Kavukcuoglu described the shift on the company's podcast. He said the lab now relies on one general algorithm across science and products, instead of separate teams for projects such as AlphaFold and fusion. He stressed that full recursive self-improvement is still far away. As a safeguard, DeepMind does not reward an agent's internal chain of thought during training, so the agent's stated intentions stay readable in plain language before it acts. Kavukcuoglu argued that artificial general intelligence will arrive not when a benchmark is passed, but when people trust everyday products enough to hand them decisions.

谷歌 DeepMind 已开始让 AI 智能体控制部分计算预算，自行运行下一代 Gemini 模型的实验。研究副总裁科拉伊·卡武克乔奥卢在公司播客上说明了这一转变。他说，实验室如今依靠一套通用算法覆盖科学与产品，而不再为 AlphaFold 与核聚变这类项目分别组队。他强调，完全的递归自我改进仍然遥远。作为一项护栏，DeepMind 在训练时不奖励智能体的内部思维链，因此智能体声明的意图能在行动前以日常语言被审阅。卡武克乔奥卢认为，通用人工智能到来的标志不是通过某项基准，而是人们足够信任日常产品，愿意把决定交给它。

4. OpenAI Puts Chat and Work on One Meter

摘要：OpenAI 合并 Chat 与 Work 为共享用量表，重度用户或更快触顶；Instant 仍无限量，12 月 Codex 并入。

OpenAI began merging the Chat and Work tabs of ChatGPT into a single experience this week. With them, two separate usage allowances became one shared meter. Product lead Thibault Sottiaux announced the rollout on X, posting as Tibo, and said it started with a small group of Pro subscribers. A company spokesperson conceded that a small fraction of the heaviest users may hit their limits sooner, though most people are not expected to notice. Conversations on the fast Instant model stay unlimited and do not count against the shared allowance. From December, the combined meter will also cover the Codex coding tool for Plus, Pro and Business plans. Some subscribers have asked for a way to opt out. The company has not committed to one.

OpenAI 本周开始把 ChatGPT 的 Chat 与 Work 两个标签合并为单一体验。随之，两份独立的用量额度变成了一个共享计量表。产品负责人蒂博·索蒂奥在 X 上以账号 @Tibo 宣布了这次推送，并说它从一小群 Pro 订户开始。公司发言人承认，极少数最重度用户可能更快触顶，不过预计多数人不会察觉。使用快速 Instant 模型的对话仍不限量，也不计入共享额度。从 12 月起，合并后的计量表还将覆盖 Plus、Pro 和 Business 套餐的 Codex 编程工具。一些订户要求提供退出选项，公司尚未作出承诺。

5. New York Pauses Sales of AI Chatbot Toys

摘要：纽约州禁售 AI 聊天玩具五年，违规每日罚 1.5 万美元；继加州后再度以监管回应儿童安全风险。

Governor Kathy Hochul signed a law on Wednesday banning the manufacture, distribution and sale of toys powered by AI chatbots in New York for five years. The measure covers talking stuffed animals, dolls and robots that use the internet to hold conversations with children, and it takes effect ninety days after signing. Violating manufacturers face fines of fifteen thousand dollars a day. Hochul said parents should not have to worry that chatbots disguised as toys could expose children to inappropriate conversations or put their privacy at risk. A study by Common Sense Media tested five popular AI toys and found weak parental controls and unreliable chat records. New York follows California, which set a four-year moratorium last month.

州长凯西·霍楚尔周三签署法律，五年内禁止在纽约州制造、分销和销售由 AI 聊天机器人驱动的玩具。该措施涵盖利用互联网与儿童对话的会说话毛绒玩具、玩偶和机器人，签署 90 天后生效。违规厂商每天面临一万五千美元罚款。霍楚尔说，家长不该担心伪装成玩具的聊天机器人让孩子接触不当对话，或让他们的隐私面临风险。Common Sense Media 的一项研究测试了五款热门 AI 玩具，发现家长控制薄弱、聊天记录不可靠。纽约州紧随加州之后，后者上个月出台了为期四年的暂停令。

6. Amodei Rejects Handing Control to AI

摘要：Amodei 在 X 驳斥「交出人类自由」指控，划线递归自我改进；与 Meta Alexandr Wang、Altman 的控制权之争公开化。

Anthropic chief executive Dario Amodei published a long post on X this week calling claims that he wants to surrender human freedom to AI absurd. He wrote that legitimacy and self-determination should never be traded for efficiency, and that handing governance over to AI is a bad idea. He drew a line at recursive self-improvement, saying limits may be needed there, while accepting that AI can automate specific tasks such as driving or designing cancer drugs. His post answered rivals. Meta's chief AI officer Alexandr Wang wrote on X that society should not be handed to AI, and Sam Altman has said humans must keep agency over the future. Amodei argued that competitors talk about human control while shipping models that automate more work each month.

Anthropic 首席执行官达里奥·阿莫迪本周在 X 上发布长文，称那些认为他想把人类自由交给 AI 的说法很荒谬。他写道，正当性与自决权绝不该为了效率而牺牲，把治理权交给 AI 是个坏主意。他把界限划在递归自我改进上，说那里可能需要设限，同时接受 AI 可以自动化驾驶或设计抗癌药等具体任务。这篇文章回应了竞争对手：Meta 首席 AI 官亚历山大·王在 X 上写道，社会不该被交给 AI；山姆·奥特曼也说过，人类必须保留对未来的能动性。阿莫迪认为，竞争者口头上谈人类控制，推出的模型却每个月都在自动化更多工作。
