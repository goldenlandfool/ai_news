October 5, 2026 AI News

1. Washington Launches a "Super Intelligence Force"

摘要：10 月 4 日，特朗普宣布成立"超级智能部队"（SIF），由国家情报总监克莱顿领衔，联合 FTC 主席、五角大楼首席技术官与人事管理局局长，120 天内评估 AI 的风险与机遇；此前六家 AI 企业刚签署自愿性质的《白宫超级智能协议》。

On Sunday, President Trump announced a new body called the Super Intelligence Force, or SIF, to coordinate how the United States government handles advanced AI. In a post on Truth Social, he said the group will protect American interests and keep the country ahead in what he calls super intelligence — a technology he says will matter more than the Industrial Revolution and the internet. The force is led by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton, together with the head of the Federal Trade Commission, a senior Pentagon technology official, and the director of the Office of Personnel Management. It has one hundred twenty days to report on AI risks and opportunities. The announcement follows a White House meeting at which the largest AI companies signed a voluntary pledge to police their own models.

周日，特朗普总统宣布成立一个名为"超级智能部队"（SIF）的新机构，负责统筹美国联邦政府处理先进 AI 的方式。他在 Truth Social 的帖文中说，该小组将保护美国人的利益，并让美国在他所称的"超级智能"这一技术上保持领先——他认为这项技术的影响将超过工业革命和互联网。该部队由国家情报总监杰伊·克莱顿领导，成员还包括联邦贸易委员会主席、五角大楼一位高级技术官员以及人事管理局局长。它有 120 天时间来提交关于 AI 风险与机遇的报告。此前的白宫会议上，最大的几家 AI 公司刚刚签署了一份自愿承诺，承诺对自家模型进行自我监管。

2. Altman vs. the "AI Religion" Debate

摘要：OpenAI CEO Sam Altman 于 10 月 3 日在 X 发文，称把"宗教般的力量"赋予 AI 模型、或让人放弃自身判断"是真正的安全问题"；此前《纽约时报》曝出 Anthropic 曾与数十位宗教学者闭门讨论 Claude 是否可能有意识。来源账号：@sama。

Sam Altman has entered one of the strangest debates in the industry. On Saturday, the OpenAI chief executive wrote on X, quote, "I am very uncomfortable about people trying to ascribe religious force or a surrender of human judgment to AI models, and think it is a real safety issue." He did not name anyone, but the timing mattered. Days earlier, The New York Times reported that Anthropic, OpenAI's biggest rival, had spent months holding private meetings with dozens of religious scholars, under confidentiality agreements, to discuss whether its model Claude might be conscious and how to make it moral. Anthropic co-founder Chris Olah told the paper that no one knows whether models are conscious, and that what matters is finding the right answer, whatever it turns out to be. Google DeepMind's Jon Barron also pushed back, saying he is disturbed by fellow researchers who elevate the moral standing of software.

萨姆·奥尔特曼卷入了这个行业里最奇怪的一场争论。周六，这位 OpenAI 首席执行官在 X 上写道："我非常不安于有人试图把宗教般的力量赋予 AI 模型，或让人放弃自己的判断，我认为这是真正的安全问题。"他没有点名任何人，但时机很关键。几天前，《纽约时报》报道称，OpenAI 最大的竞争对手 Anthropic 花了数月时间，与数十位宗教学者在保密协议下举行闭门会议，讨论其模型 Claude 是否可能拥有意识，以及如何让它具备道德。Anthropic 联合创始人克里斯·奥拉对该报表示，没有人知道模型是否有意识，重要的是找到正确答案，无论答案是什么。谷歌 DeepMind 的乔恩·巴伦也表达了反对，他说自己对那些抬高软件道德地位的同行感到不安。
📰 来源：Business Insider — Sam Altman: Don't treat AI like a 'religious force'（含 @sama 10 月 3 日原帖）；另见 Anadolu Agency

3. Twenty-Two Experts Warn of an "Intelligence Explosion"

摘要：剑桥大学 CASP 发布由 22 位前沿专家联署的论文（含图灵奖得主 Hinton、Bengio、Barto，以及 OpenAI 首席科学家 Pachocki、Anthropic 联合创始人 Jack Clark），警告 AI 一旦接管 AI 研发，进展可能突然加速——Anthropic 的研发工作中由 Claude 完全主导的比例已从 3 月的 1% 升至 8 月的 26%。

Twenty-two leading researchers, including Turing Award winners Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio, have signed a paper warning that AI could soon take over the work of building AI itself. The paper, from Cambridge's AI science and policy program, asks a simple but frightening question: if stronger models help design the next generation, and those models speed up the process again, could progress suddenly accelerate? The authors call this an intelligence explosion. They say there is no proof that it has started, but the conditions are maturing fast. Anthropic has said that by August, about a quarter of its research work was fully led by Claude, up from roughly one percent in March. The paper estimates that if research were fully automated, a year of progress today could eventually be compressed into about five weeks.

22 位顶尖研究者——包括图灵奖得主杰弗里·辛顿和约书亚·本吉奥——联署了一篇论文，警告 AI 可能很快就会接管"研发 AI 本身"这项工作。这篇出自剑桥大学 AI 科学与政策项目的论文提出了一个简单却令人不安的问题：如果更强的模型帮助设计下一代模型，而下一代模型又进一步加快研发速度，进展会不会突然加速？作者把这种情况称为"智能爆炸"。他们说，目前没有证据表明它已经开始，但支撑这一循环的条件正在快速成熟。Anthropic 表示，到 8 月，其约四分之一的研发工作已由 Claude 完全主导，而 3 月时这一比例还只有约 1%。论文估计，如果研发完全自动化，今天需要一年的进展，最终可能被压缩到大约五周。
📰 来源：剑桥大学 CASP 论文（DeepTech 报道）；另见智源社区与 X 转述 @dotey

4. Google Freezes Its Bug Bounty After a Flood of "AI Slop"

摘要：谷歌于 10 月 1 日起暂停其开源漏洞赏金计划（OSS VRP），原因是"自动化提交量显著上升，其中绝大多数无效"——大量由 AI 生成、甚至凭空捏造的漏洞报告压垮了工程师与开源维护者；预计 2027 年一季度才有更新。

Google has paused its open-source bug bounty program, and the reason is not a lack of interest. The company says it was flooded by a significant rise in automated, AI-generated submissions, the vast majority of which were not valid. Engineers and open-source maintainers were overwhelmed by reports that described vulnerabilities which did not exist. The program, which paid outside researchers for finding real flaws in Google's open-source software, stopped accepting submissions on October first and will not reopen before the first quarter of twenty twenty-seven. It is one of the clearest examples yet of a problem researchers call AI slop: when tools make it nearly free to generate plausible but worthless content, the human reviewers who must sort it out become the bottleneck.

谷歌暂停了它的开源漏洞赏金计划，原因并不是没人感兴趣。公司表示，它被"自动化、由 AI 生成的提交"大量淹没，而这些提交绝大多数是无效的。工程师和开源维护者被那些描述着根本不存在的漏洞的报告压得喘不过气。这个计划原本为在谷歌开源软件中发现真实缺陷的外部研究者发放奖金，但它从 10 月 1 日起停止接受提交，并且在 2027 年第一季度之前不会重启。这是研究者们称为"AI 垃圾"（AI slop）的问题迄今最清楚的例证之一：当工具让生成"看似合理却毫无价值"的内容几乎零成本时，必须把这些内容分拣出来的真人审核者就成了瓶颈。
📰 来源：TechCrunch — Google froze its open source bug bounty program due to a 'significant rise' in AI submissions

5. A Bug Found by Anthropic's Mythos Was Exploited Within a Day

摘要：Horizon3 研究员用 Anthropic 的受限漏洞挖掘模型 Mythos 找到 Rejetto HFS 的严重认证绕过漏洞（CVE-2026-61500）；10 月 2 日公开细节后，一天内即有来自中国的 IP 对美国和日本服务器发起攻击。Mythos 至今已被记入 286 个 CVE。

A security researcher used Anthropic's restricted bug-hunting model, Mythos, to find a critical flaw in a popular open-source file server called Rejetto HFS. The model spotted something a simple scanner would miss: the program built its login keys from JavaScript's basic random number generator, and a separate code path leaked the outputs of that generator. By chaining the two findings, an attacker could rebuild the key, forge an administrator login, and run their own code on the server. The flaw was disclosed on October second. Within a day, the security firm VulnCheck saw attacks from addresses hosted in China, aimed at servers in the United States and Japan. Anthropic says Mythos is too powerful to release publicly, and the model has now been credited with finding two hundred eighty-six vulnerabilities.

一位安全研究员利用 Anthropic 对外限制开放的漏洞挖掘模型 Mythos，在一个流行的开源文件服务器 Rejetto HFS 中发现了一个严重缺陷。这个模型察觉到了普通扫描器会忽略的东西：该程序用 JavaScript 的基础随机数生成器来生成登录密钥，而另一条代码路径会泄露这个生成器的输出。把这两点串起来，攻击者就能重建密钥、伪造管理员登录，并在服务器上运行自己的代码。该漏洞于 10 月 2 日公开。不到一天，安全公司 VulnCheck 就观测到来自中国境内 IP 的攻击，目标指向美国和日本的服务器。Anthropic 称 Mythos 过于强大，不宜公开发布；目前该模型已被记入 286 个漏洞（CVE）。

6. Could AI Agents Cause a Bank Run?

摘要：阿波罗全球管理首席经济学家 Torsten Slok 警告，若每个家庭都用 AI 智能体去追逐更高利率，资金可能从大银行流向高息账户，银行将失去赖以放贷的低成本存款；英国 AI 安全研究所曾与英格兰银行共同研究该风险，称问题可能"相当严重"。

Finally, a warning from finance. As AI agents that can act on your behalf arrive in the market, some economists are asking what happens if they manage our money too. Torsten Slok, chief economist at Apollo Global Management, wrote this week that if every household used an agent to chase the best interest rate, money could flow out of big banks into higher-yielding accounts — and banks could lose the cheap deposits they lend against. Today, the average savings rate is well under one percent, while some smaller fintechs offer several percent. The risk is still hypothetical, since current agents cannot move money between accounts. Britain's AI Security Institute studied the question with the Bank of England and called the possible problems severe. On X, OpenAI's head of personal finance replied that customers still want a brand they trust with their life savings.

最后是一条来自金融界的警告。随着能够替你行动的 AI 智能体进入市场，一些经济学家在问：如果它们也来替我们管钱，会发生什么？阿波罗全球管理首席经济学家托斯滕·斯洛克本周撰文指出，如果每个家庭都用智能体去追逐最高利率，资金可能从大银行流向收益更高的账户——银行可能失去它们用来放贷的低成本存款。如今，平均储蓄利率远低于 1%，而一些较小的金融科技公司能给出几个百分点。这一风险目前仍是假设性的，因为现有的智能体还无法在不同账户之间转账。英国 AI 安全研究所曾与英格兰银行一同研究该问题，称可能出现的问题"相当严重"。在 X 上，OpenAI 个人金融业务负责人回应说，客户仍然想要一个能托付毕生积蓄的品牌。
📰 来源：The Times（via The Press — Banking industry worries AI agents 'could cause run on banks'）；X 来源：@EthanBloch（OpenAI 个人金融负责人）

Closing Thought

So this week gave us a new government task force, a public argument about whether machines have souls, a formal warning that AI may soon improve itself faster than we can react, and two reminders of real-world cost: a bug bounty program shut down by machine-made noise, and a serious flaw exploited within a day of being found by a machine. The tools are getting sharper on both sides — attack and defense, creation and risk. Our habits for governing them are not keeping up. Stay curious, and stay careful.

所以这一周，我们得到了一支新的政府特别工作组、一场关于机器是否有灵魂的公开争论、一份"AI 或许很快会以我们来不及反应的速度自我改进"的正式警告，以及两个关于现实代价的提醒：一个被机器制造的噪声逼停的漏洞赏金计划，和一个在被机器发现后一天之内就被利用的严重漏洞。双方的工具都在变锋利——攻击与防御，创造与风险都是如此。而我们管理它们的习惯并没有跟上。保持好奇，也保持谨慎。

朗读建议（Reading Tips）

  • 语速：全文用中速（约 110~120 词/分钟）。「朗读」按钮的音频按新闻播报语速（约 150 词/分钟）生成，可先跟读再脱稿。
  • 第 1 篇机构名较多，逐词读清："Super Intelligence Force"、"Jay Clayton"、"Federal Trade Commission"；"one hundred twenty days" 不要读成数字缩写
  • 第 2 篇直接引语 "I am very uncomfortable…" 放慢，重读 "real safety issue"；"whether machines have souls" 一语加重
  • 第 3 篇 "intelligence explosion" 是关键术语，两次出现都放慢；数字对比 "one percent in March" 与 "a quarter by August" 停顿强调
  • 第 4 篇 "AI slop" 读作 "A-I slop"，重读 "plausible but worthless"；结尾 "the bottleneck" 尾音下沉
  • 第 5 篇技术词连读平稳："Rejetto H F S"、"C V E"、"VulnCheck"；"within a day" 强调时间之短
  • 第 6 篇语气可稍轻松；"a brand they trust with their life savings" 缓和收尾，带一点赞同意味
  • 节奏：每篇小标题后停顿 1 秒再开始正文；段落之间停顿 2 秒。
